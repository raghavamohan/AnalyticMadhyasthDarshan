      Study format Contributing guide
      House-style checklist (topical studies)
      • Open with the study’s question. State specific assumptions or limits where needed; the shared method is in Our approach.
      • Continuous scholarly prose — avoid scaffold labels like **Step N—**, **Verdict:**, or ## How to read this study.
      • ### N.M Descriptive title subsections for argument steps; cross-reference with §2.3.
      • A ## References section grouping sources, linking ../References/… or external URLs.
      • Author name and slug stay consistent with the approved proposal.

      Preview checks headings, tables, equations and Mermaid diagrams. Images are listed without downloading them. Publication also applies study metadata, glossary links and PDF pagination; review the CI artifacts before approval.

      Submitting creates a public GitHub pull request. Maintainers review it before publication. Review time varies; My Submissions shows who acts next. Email preferences are on the dashboard.
