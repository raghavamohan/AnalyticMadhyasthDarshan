## Study proposal

This comparative study broadens the dialogue between Madhyasth Darshan and classical Indian philosophical systems beyond Advaita Vedanta, focusing on classical Samkhya-Yoga and early/Madhyamaka Buddhism.

Key areas of inquiry:
1. Comparing coexistence of all-pervasive Omnipresence (satta) and active units (ikayi) with Samkhya's dualism of Prakriti and Purusha; examining samyama and sanyam.
2. Confronting the Buddhist doctrine of Anatta (non-self) and Pratityasamutpada (dependent origination) with Madhyasth Darshan's assertion of the permanent, conscious, non-decaying jeevan.
3. Contrasting the Buddhist diagnosis of universal suffering (dukkha) with Madhyasth Darshan's thesis that happiness is the constitutional dharma of the human knower.
4. Contrasting liberation/isolation models (kaivalya, nirvana) with awakening (jagriti) and participation in an undivided society.
5. Primary texts: Manav Vyavahar Darshan, Anubhavatmak Adhyatmavad, Samkhyakarika, and Buddhist sutras.
