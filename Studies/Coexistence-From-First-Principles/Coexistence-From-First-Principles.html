Coexistence From First Principles

Author: AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan.org — a group of people studying philosophy. Source repository: raghavamohan/AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan.

Edited on: July 3, 2026, 10:10 AM IST

A generative re-founding of the coexistence template: three kernel primitives — , triadic activity, and saturation — from which units, relationships, values, composition, and transmission are constructed, with a recovery theorem against The Coexistence Template as the adequacy criterion.

Study proposal

Question. Can the six primitives of The Coexistence Template (O, U, R, V, κ, τ) be reduced to a minimal generative kernel that captures the main concepts of as constructions rather than postulates — and where exactly does derivation stop and textual data begin?

Primary texts. MVD, SB, JV (page warrants below are already verified against the local mirrors by the repo quote tool or cited in Released studies).

The kernel (three primitives).

  1. O — : indivisible, omnipresent, actionless, energy in equilibrium, knowledge-natured (MVD pp. 11–12; JV p. 31).
  2. A — activity, with the triad built into its definition: every activity has effort (shram), motion (gati), and result (parinam) as aspects, each the joint form of the other two (MVD p. 105; SB p. 58); the combined activity is basic impulsion (mool cheshta, MVD p. 40).
  3. Saturation — the single primitive relation: every activity is in O, energy-replete through that co-location (“Unit + Energy fullness = Activeness”, SB p. 69); O is not an activity.

Kernel axioms (working set).

  • K1 (Energisation): every activity is energy-replete through saturation; activity is neither created nor destroyed — conservation (template A3) becomes a corollary of results transforming rather than annihilating.
  • K2 (Boundedness/closure): activity clusters into bounded loci whose motion-path closes — “this motion-path itself is that atom’s line of regulation and also its boundary” (SB p. 58).
  • K3 (Coupling): activities couple; one activity’s results enter another’s as the pressure of mutuality (MVD p. 114); complementarity of deficiency and surplus (hungry/overfull, MVD p. 8) is the definite form of coupling.
  • K4 (Recognition-as-law): provision to recognise others based on law is inherent in saturation (SB p. 57).
  • K5 (Completeness orientation): each triad moment has a completeness goal — immortality of result, restfulness of effort, destination of motion (SB pp. 58, 71).

Constructions (the former primitives, derived).

  • C1 Unit := maximal bounded cluster of activity with closed motion-path (from K2). Genuinely derivable; the kernel’s strongest result.
  • C2 Relationship := definite coupling of triads; E(r) = the complementarity profile (from K3–K4). L2–L3 become structure of coupling rather than separate laws.
  • C3 Value := what a coupling realises — one unit’s participation-as-usefulness in another’s activity (SB p. 50). Derivable as a slot; the six value kinds and nine established values remain textual data filling it.
  • C4 Composition := coupling that closes a new joint motion-path (new boundary, new signature) = compound; coupling without new closure = mixture (MVD p. 42 + K2).
  • C5 Transmission := the composition method (rachna vidhi) recurring as constitutive input in successive members (JV p. 82; MVD p. 93). Constructor schema only — the carrier ladder (constitution/seed/lineage/) stays irreducible data.

Knowledge order. = a unit whose result-aspect is immortal (; inexhaustible effort and motion, SB pp. 61, 80); its additional capabilities are the triad applied reflexively — visualises triads (MVD p. 327), evaluation is second-order assessment of couplings (JV p. 139), and the template’s P3 discontinuity becomes: reflexive triads must achieve the definiteness non-reflexive triads have by constitution.

Explicitly irreducible (stated up front). O; saturation; the triad structure of A; boundedness; the content of admissible complementarity (the template’s L3 guard); the τ carrier ladder; evaluation’s restriction to (D6); ’s substantiality; the samadhi warrant (meta to any formalism). Net effect: six primitives reduce to three, plus explicitly labelled textual data.

Adequacy criterion (the central deliverable). A recovery theorem: every clause D1–D13 and L1–L7 of The Coexistence Template is recovered with one of three statuses — derived from the kernel, definitional (conservative naming), or substantive textual data — with a countermodel exhibited for each kernel axiom’s independence. This is also the vehicle for the independence-audit programme announced in Category Theory Explained §6.16.4 and The Coexistence Template §9.

Mathematical target. The kernel is naturally an operad / wiring-diagram story: units as boxes with ports (deficiencies and surpluses), coupling as wiring, compound-mode composition as collapsing a wired diagram into one box with a new interface (the template’s L6 iteration), mixture as wiring without collapse. This gives the categorical formalisation a successor to bare colimits and types the L3 guard as a constraint on wirings.

Scope and non-goals. The Coexistence Template remains the textual articulation and the benchmark; this study does not replace it. Every derivation is flagged as this paper’s construction, with the template as control — the discipline that separates this from stipulated-minimality claims criticised in the MD-TOPOS review. No SI units or physics claims (those live in Restfulness and Least Action §6.2 as interpretive comparison). Mechanisation of the recovery theorem is named as future work.

Planned comparisons. The Coexistence Template (articulation vs derivation); MD-TOPOS (Meena 2025 — predicate ledger vs generative kernel; their minimality proof as methodological precedent); operadic/wiring-diagram open-systems literature (Spivak school) for the mathematical form.
