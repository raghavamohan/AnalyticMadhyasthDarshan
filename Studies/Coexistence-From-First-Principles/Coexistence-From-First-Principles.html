Coexistence From First Principles

The kernel and reconstructed structure of Madhyasth Darshan

Author: AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan.org — a group of people studying philosophy. Source repository: raghavamohan/AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan.

Edited on: July 13, 2026, 12:12 AM IST

The thesis: ’s ontology can be reconstructed from a small kernel — three primitives (the omnipresent medium , real countable units, and their saturation), two axioms (endowment and completeness-orientation), and one generative move (complementary units close into a new unit, iterated up the tiers). Activeness — the inseparable triad of effort, motion, and result — is not a further primitive but the manifestation the first axiom confers, so the equation Unit + energy-fullness = activeness (SB p. 69) reads as a theorem within the reconstruction, not a postulate. From this kernel the darshan’s structure is reconstructed: couplings as definite mutualities, value as what a coupling realises, composition as coupling that closes a new boundary, transmission as the recurrence of a composition method, and — at the fixed point where a compound atom reaches constitutional completeness — , whose activity takes activity as its object, switching on evaluation, justice, and the one tier of the structure that can fail. A recovery audit names, for every notion, its provenance and epistemic standing — textual assertion, formal reconstruction, mathematical analogy, taught datum, or open metaphysical posit — and a single categorical spine carries the parts mathematics can carry, naming exactly where it stops. The claim is that the kernel organizes the darshan’s structure — not that the texts derive it.

1. One schema, across the orders

is usually read through its human programme — justice in relationships, family-based order, humane education. The texts support a stronger reading: the general structure is asserted first, and the human case is organized as its most demanding instance. Samadhanatmak Bhautikvad opens in fully general terms —

“Each unit is a whole along with its environment.” — SB p. 13–14

“Each unit is orderliness with its ness and participates in overall orderliness.” — SB p. 13–14 (ness = self-ness in the bilingual edition; PDF extraction drops the prefix)

— and Manav Vyavahar Darshan states the same universality: “All units saturated in the Omnipresence (permeative and transparent) have form, properties, essential nature & , and have inherent orderliness & participate in overall orderliness” (MVD p. 11). Manav Karm Darshan, whose title-page principle is simply “Effort – Motion – Result” (KD, Principle), develops the same ground as a science of activity from the atom upward.

So the task is not extrapolation from humans to atoms but articulation: stating, in one structure, what the texts assert piecewise about atoms (MVD p. 8; JV p. 67), cells (JV p. 82), organisms (SB p. 16), and human collectives (MVD p. 55, p. 161). The whole of it can be held in one picture before any formalism:

  • Ground. There is one omnipresent, actionless medium, , and there are countless real units. Every unit is saturated in the medium — soaked, submerged, surrounded. That saturation, and nothing added to it, makes each unit energy-replete and law-legible, so each unit is active: its activity is effort, motion, and result together.
  • Couple. Units couple by complementarity — one’s result entering the other as input, never competition. Complementarity takes an order-specific form; its material instance is a unit short of completeness meeting one in surplus (deficiency and surplus).
  • Close and recur. When a complementary coupling closes a new boundary, it is a new unit, one tier up, with its own signature. Because the output is again a unit, the same closure schema reappears — literally in physicochemical and biological composition (particles into atoms, atoms into molecules and planets, cells into organisms), and in an order-specific, increasingly analogical form up to individuals into families and undivided society.
  • Run reflexively. At the fixed point where a composite atom reaches constitutional completeness, its activity can take activity itself as its object. That reflexive turn is , the sentient unit — and with it, uniquely, come evaluation, justice, and the possibility of failure.

One closure schema organizes the tiered structure; at constitutional completeness a distinct reflexive move (§2.3) opens the knowledge order. A worked instance runs through the whole paper in miniature: two atoms, one hungry and one overfull (MVD p. 8), couple by complementarity, close into a molecule with a new conduct (MVD p. 42), which re-enters the same schema in order-specific form toward the planetary and biological lines; carried up the development progression to constitutional completeness, the same activity turns reflexive and is (SB p. 61).

Precision earns its keep by letting the structure be carried to systems Shri Nagraj did not himself analyse. Forward, as design: an organisation, an economy, or an institution is humane and internally self-sustaining exactly when it satisfies each reconstructed clause — identifiable units, definite couplings with explicit expectation profiles, capacity sufficient for value to flow, composition by complementary need, persistence conditional on fulfilment, and an explicit carrier of transmission across member turnover. (This is a criterion for humane, coercion-free order — not a law of empirical survival.) In reverse, as diagnosis: a failing system is walked clause by clause until the unmet one is found, and the knowledge-order discontinuity predicts that human-tier systems fail first at the understanding-dependent steps.

Three cautions govern what follows. The texts assert piecewise; this paper reconstructs — every reconstruction is this paper’s construction, warranted by citations but not identical with them. The formalism is a lens for clarity, not a proof machine: it proves nothing about the reality of the medium or of , which are exactly where the darshan parts from mainstream science. And a reduction can flatten: the kernel keeps units co-eternal with the medium — real carriers, not clusters distilled from activity — and refuses to reconstruct away what the texts present as taught content.

Model status. Every claim carries a status along two independent axes, and the paper is explicit about both. Textual provenance — is the claim a textual assertion (stated in MVD/SB/JV/KD), a formal reconstruction (the paper’s own kernel-level organisation — κ, the coupling anatomy), or a mathematical analogy (the categorical spine of §6, the physical readings)? Epistemic standing — is it established within the model (a result of the reconstruction), a taught datum (content the texts fix and the model cannot produce), or an open metaphysical posit (asserted, not established)? The two axes cross: and are textual assertions and open metaphysical posits; κ is a formal reconstruction established within the model; the six value-kinds are textual assertions that remain taught data. The audit’s role-tags (primitive, axiom, definition, theorem, data) locate a notion within the reconstruction — so a “theorem” there is a result within the model, not a proof about the world. The kernel organizes the darshan’s structure; it does not claim the texts derive it.

2. The kernel

2.1 Three primitives

O (, Omnipresence) — one, real, all-pervasive, actionless, state-complete, and energy- and knowledge-natured (): “Space is uniform in all dimensions… this reality is omnipresent” (JV p. 31); state-complete while everything else is state-dynamic (MVD p. 12). O is not a unit, and no statement quantifying over units applies to O.

U (units, ) — real, co-eternal, countable carriers: “Nature is in the form of entities that can be counted” (JV p. 45). A unit bears a state and an activity; it is never built from activity, occasions, or process. This carrier-primacy is a commitment, not a convenience: a unit is not identified with its behaviour (§6, §10).

Saturation — the one ground relation: every unit is soaked, submerged, and surrounded in the single O, with “no provision in existence to separate units of nature from Omnipresence” (JV p. 18). Saturation is constitutive co-location in a shared medium, the first ontological relation, distinct from any unit-to-unit coupling, and not a physical drawing from O (SB pp. 49, 62).

Activeness is deliberately not a primitive. The texts state its status as an identity, “Unit + Energy fullness = Activeness” (SB p. 69) — a co-presence conferred by saturation, recovered below as a theorem (§2.4), not a fourth building block.

2.2 Two axioms

A1 (Endowment — the firewall). Through saturation, O endows each unit’s carrier with exactly two invariants: (a) inexhaustible energy, so the unit is active and its activity manifests as the triad of effort (shram), motion (gati), and result (parinam) — “There is no unit or particle in nature which is not replete with energy… This activeness is seen in every unit in the form of effort, motion and result” (SB p. 69; KD 3.1); and (b) lawful intelligibility, so the activity is determinate and unit-to-unit recognisable, “there is provision in every unit for recognising one another based on law” (SB p. 57). The firewall is part of the axiom: O is never an input to, nor a summand of, a unit’s activity — it supplies determinacy and non-halting, contributing no push, no update term, and suffering no depletion. O energises and regulates by being-soaked-into, never by acting; the nucleus within each atom, and mediative atma within , localise this regulation without O ever becoming an agent (MVD p. 26).

A2 (Completeness-orientation). Every unit’s activity is directed toward development and completeness — “each unit, embedded in the complete, is active and developing solely for completeness” (SB p. 51) — and not toward indefinite quantitative increase. This orientation is universal, but its terminal triadic form is tiered. Along the atomic development line it culminates at constitutional completeness — “every atom continues to undergo insertion and expulsion of particles until constitutional completeness” (SB p. 71), the threshold at which appears (T1). The fully-realized terminal goals — restfulness of effort, destination of motion, immortality of result (SB p. 58) — are the awakening targets of (activity- and conduct-completeness, T2–T3); the first three orders realize completeness as development toward order, not as consciously-attained restfulness. The darshan does not treat indefinite quantitative increase as the telos; this reconstruction therefore represents completeness as a qualitative terminal condition, not an unbounded scalar objective (§8).

2.3 The generative move

κ (compound-closure). Given saturated units whose ports are complementary — in the material instance a deficiency met by a surplus, with complementarity taking an order-specific form at higher tiers (§7) — their activities couple and, when the coupling closes a new motion-path, present one bounded whole: a new unit, one tier up, with a fresh signature whose components “discard their own conducts, and present another kind of conduct” (MVD p. 42). Written as a rule:

  u₁, …, uₙ ∈ U saturated in O        ports complementary (deficiency ↔ surplus)        closure of a new motion-path
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        κ(u₁, …, uₙ) ∈ U   —   a new unit, new signature, +1 tier

The rule’s premises are the anatomy of a coupling, spelled out in §4.3: recognition (ρ) fixes the expectation profile — reading, by A1(b), that one unit’s deficiency complements another’s surplus; fulfilment (φ) is that coupling executing, results flowing along the profile; and closure asks whether the executed coupling settles into one new bounded motion-path. So κ is not a third operator standing beside ρ and φ but their outcome — κ(u₁,…,uₙ) = closure(φ(ρ(u₁,…,uₙ))), the closure of a recognised, fulfilled coupling. Mixture (mishran) is the same coupling when it does not close: φ still delivers results — “components all maintain their respective conducts” (MVD p. 42) — but no new boundary forms and the aggregate cannot re-enter κ. Compound and mixture are therefore not two combinators but the two fates of one fulfilled coupling, sorted by closure — which is exactly why “composition is not development” (SB pp. 75–76): only a coupling that closes crosses an order plateau. Because κ’s output is again a unit, the closure schema reappears in an order-specific form — literal compound-closure in the physicochemical and biological lines, extended analogically at the knowledge order (§4.4) — and the tiered hierarchy is its iteration (§4.10).

The reflexive turn. κ iterated along the development progression (vikas-kram) — “in the atom in the form of hungry and overfull atoms” (MVD p. 8) — reaches a distinguished fixed point: a composite atom whose result-aspect attains its A2 terminal, constitutional completeness, after which “both effort and motion become inexhaustible” (SB p. 61). At that fixed point the unit’s activity can take activity itself as its object. This reflexive re-application is , and it is the sole locus of evaluation and of possible failure (§4.8). Reflexivity is a distinct move, not a second literal firing of κ; it is an interpretive model of the knowledge order — this paper’s organising reading, not a derived theorem — flagged as such in the audit.

2.4 Corollaries

Activeness. By A1(a), every saturated unit is energy-replete and therefore active; its activity is the triad. “Unit + Energy fullness = Activeness” (SB p. 69) is thereby a theorem, and activeness is a family indexed by units, not a substrate they are built from. The triad’s internal constitution is a joint form — each moment the outcome of the other two (MVD p. 105) — and its combined activity is basic impulsion (mool cheshta, MVD p. 40). KD states each aspect’s sense directly: effort is the grandeur of the existent-state, motion the grandeur of spreading effect, result the grandeur of a different existent-state (KD 3.11).

Conservation. The texts assert existence is beginless and stable — “Existence neither increases nor decreases” (JV p. 127). Conservation of activity does not strictly follow from constancy of existence; the reconstruction takes it as a model postulate, consistent with A1’s inexhaustible source and A2’s non-annihilating orientation but warranted textually rather than derived.

Coupling and non-isolation. That units couple does not follow from shared saturation and activeness alone — independently active units need not interact. The texts supply the missing premise directly: “Nothing is isolated – that is the principle” (JV p. 43). The reconstruction therefore takes no-isolation as a premise (a textual assertion): every unit participates in at least one coupling, and universal coupling holds because of it — rather than separating coupling as a theorem from a residual totality assumption.

3. Notation: category theory in brief

The formal spine of §6 uses category theory; this section gives the working vocabulary in plain language so the paper is self-contained, and readers at home with it can skip ahead.

Category theory replaces “what is each thing made of?” with “how does each thing relate to everything else?”. An object is a thing to talk about; a morphism (arrow) is a relationship or a way from one object to another; composition chains arrows, and every object has an identity arrow. Two dual readings matter here. An algebra for a construction is a thing built up by a rule — the natural home of “generated”: the units of nature can be encoded as the algebra generated by κ, so definitions over units become definitions by recursion on how a unit was built. A coalgebra is the dual — a carrier together with a map that unfolds its behaviour without identifying the carrier with that behaviour; it keeps a unit a substance that bears an activity rather than a bundle of relations. A colimit (gluing) joins pieces into one whole along what they share — the shape of families joining into one society. These three — the generating algebra upward, the carrier-coalgebra downward, and the gluing colimit sideways — are all the machinery the spine needs; the richer constructions are collected as optional annotations (§6.3).

4. The generated structure

Everything below is generated from §2: each notion is tagged [def] (a name for kernel structure), [thm] (a consequence), or [data] (taught content the kernel organises but cannot produce). The tags are collected in the audit (§5).

4.1 Units, activeness, and signature

A unit [def] is a saturated carrier in U, active as the triad (§2.4), disciplined along the motion-path that is at once its boundary and line of regulation. Insentient units are “active within the bounds of their length, width and height”; sentient units are “active… beyond the bounds” (MVD p. 33) — the motion-path bounds the activity’s extent without bounding its reach. An atom (parmanu) is not the elementary particle of physics: its kind, state, and measure are fixed by the number of particles in its nucleus (madhyansh) and its orbiting particles (MVD p. 42; KD 3.1).

Each unit carries a signature [def, with data] — the four-tuple form (roop), properties (gun), essential nature (svabhav), and (MVD p. 11) — minted fresh at each compound tier. The four-tuple form is definitional; the per-order values are taught data:

Order Essential nature (svabhav) Dharma (cumulative)
Material (padarth) integration–disintegration existence (astitva)
Bio (pran) vitalising–devitalising + growth (pushti)
Animal (jeev) cruel–uncruel + hope to live
Knowledge / human (gyan) fortitude, courage, generosity, kindness, grace, compassion + happiness (sukh)

4.2 Relationships and value

Every unit-to-unit mutuality is a coupling — the general term, at every order. A relationship () [def] is the knowledge-order coupling whose expectation profile E(r) is predetermined “in the sense of completeness” between -bearing units (MVD p. 61–62); an association is a coupling whose profile is adopted. The lower orders have definite coupling and mutuality, not in this value-laden sense. The value of a unit in a coupling [thm-slot, with data] is what that coupling realises — its essentiality (maulikta), “the essentiality of units in every plane and order… It is values that are reciprocated and mutually recognised” (SB p. 50). That value is real at every tier because coupling is; which values fill the slot is taught data. The texts sort essentiality into six kinds — object values (utility and art) operating at all orders, and , human, established, and expression values operating only at the knowledge order and presupposing (MVD p. 306; JV pp. 43, 138–139). The nine established values flow from the moment a relationship is recognised — “when a mother has recognised her child then motherly care starts surging by itself” (JV p. 138) — in a generative order (gratitude → affection, love, trust; JV p. 108), so V carries a partial ordering.

4.3 Recognition, fulfilment, and capacity

Recognition ρ [def] is A1(b) exercised between units — identification of a coupling and its profile. Fulfilment φ [def] is the coupling executing: “Fulfilling is to evidence use, right-use and purposeful-use” (MVD p. 27, p. 62). Fulfilment is modulated by capacity [data] — the intellectual means capacity (kshamata), ability (yogyata), and receptivity (patrata) (MVD p. 62): φ is realised at the level these permit, not by receptivity alone. In the material order the conversion of capacity and ability into effort is ascending (agreshan); its shortfall is frustration (kshobh), “incompleteness of receptivity” (MVD p. 79) — the triad failing to close toward restfulness, which at the human tier is “their yearning for restfulness” that drives awakening (MVD p. 104). Recognition and fulfilment are the two phases of every coupling; the generative move κ (§2.3, §4.4) is what a fulfilled coupling becomes when it closes. At the knowledge order the coupling gains a third phase, evaluation μ (§4.9), and the closed cycle ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction is what the texts call justice.

4.4 Composition: compound and mixture

Composition is the generative move κ (§2.3). Compound (yaugik) closes a new motion-path and mints a new unit with a new signature; mixture (mishran) does not, and yields a non-generating aggregate whose components “all maintain their respective conducts” (MVD p. 42) — so mere enlargement never crosses an order plateau (“composition is not development”, SB pp. 75–76).

κ operates literally in physicochemical and biological composition — atoms bond into molecules and planets, cells into organisms — and is extended analogically to human organization, where constitutionally complete humans organize into families, communities, and undivided society (MVD p. 55; §4.4 below). Two things in particular are confined. The sentience-creating instance of κ — compound-closure reaching constitutional completeness (T1) — runs only along the atomic development progression (vikas-kram): new sentience is an atomic threshold (SB p. 92; KD 3.2–3.3), so composing yields an assembly of , never a larger . And at the knowledge order κ changes character — composition becomes achieved: voluntary, understanding-mediated, and fallible, requiring shared cause, goal, and programme and a carrier of transmission (§4.5, §4.10), where the first three orders bond definitely. Awakening Progression (jagriti-kram) is not κ at all: it runs within an already constitutionally complete toward T2–T3 (§4.8).

At the knowledge order the closure test acquires definite content. A human assembly is treated in the reconstruction analogically as compound-like — a formal conduct-compound — exactly when its collective coupling closes: shared cause and goal recognised (ρ), values fulfilled (φ), mutual satisfaction reached (μ — justice at scale, §4.9), and the method carried across member turnover by education- (τ, §4.5). Such an assembly organizes as a whole that exhibits the four human goals — resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence — and persists as a self-continuing tradition in coexistence (MVD p. 55; SB pp. 246–247) — the goal of human assembly, its assembly-scale analogue of the awakening transitions T2–T3. An assembly held together by fear, accumulation, or enforcement fails the humane test — it is not self-reproducing without coercion and lacks internal orderliness — even where it survives empirically for a time by enforcement; the criterion (P3) concerns humane, coercion-free order, not a law of institutional survival. Two cautions keep the analogy honest: it is a conduct-compound, not a sentience-compound — no collective atma, since sentience-creating closure (T1) is atomic-only; and the texts support an organized family, community, and undivided society, not clearly a new yaugik unit in the same sense as a molecule — so “compound” here is a formal analogy, not a claim that the assembly is a fresh unit of nature. This is the exact sense in which composing yields an organized assembly of , never a larger . The chain is instantiated in How To Form Self-Sustaining Organizations and, at the scale of humankind, How Undivided Society Is Established.

4.5 State, persistence, and transmission

A unit is in its natural state [def] when its couplings are fulfilled within conducive conditions and in an excited state otherwise: “Each unit moves towards development in its natural state and decline in its excited state” (SB p. 14). In triad terms the excited state is the activity failing to close toward its A2 target. Persistence [thm, of humane order] follows: an assembly’s self-sustaining, coercion-free order holds exactly while its couplings stay fulfilled and lapses when they do not — natural-state closure sustained. This is a criterion for humane, internally-reproduced order, not a necessary-and-sufficient law of empirical survival: a coercive assembly can endure for a time by enforcement. Conducive conditions are order-relative; in the material order even an excited state can be complementary — an expelled particle feeds a hungry atom (SB p. 15), a fulfilled coupling (φ) that delivers results without closing into a new unit — while in the deluded knowledge order excitation harms both self and others (SB p. 16).

Transmission τ [def, with data] re-instantiates an assembly’s method of composition (rachna vidhi) in new member-units, “inherent in the pran sutra situated in the cell” (JV p. 82). Its form — a result whose content is a method, recurring across member turnover — is generated; its carriers are taught data, ascending with the orders (constitution / seed / lineage / education-; JV p. 48; MVD p. 93). At the knowledge order τ carries evidenced understanding only: “ignorance cannot flow in tradition” (JV p. 49).

4.6 Orders and the regulation ladder

U is partitioned into four orders [def, with data] — material, pranic/bio, animal, knowledge (JV p. 47; MVD p. 9) — each a persistence regime differing in how a unit’s method survives:

Order Added capability Regulation regime Mode
Material recognising, fulfilling result-/structural-conformance definite
Pranic / bio + selection seed-conformance (beej) definite
Animal + sensation, hope species/lineage-conformance (vansh) definite
Knowledge + knowing, believing, evaluation, choice sanskar-conformance achieved

Grounding: “The material order is regulated by structural conformity… the biological order by seed conformity… the animal order by species conformity… Humans achieve definite conduct through -conformity” (JV p. 48; SB p. 236). The regulation ladder [def] is the reading order of A1 and the orders, not new axioms: saturation provisions regulation in the unit; regulation becomes evident as law (universal across orders); the same law appears as order-specific conformance (definite in the first three, achieved in the knowledge order); within constitutionally complete , mediative atma disciplines the faculties (§4.8); and justice is the knowledge-order closure (§4.9). Orderliness at the order level is self-regulation (swatah-saspurt), inherent in co-existing orders, not dispensed by O acting as governor.

4.7 Planes and completeness transitions

Alongside the orders, SB names four planes (pad) — physicochemical, delusional, deific, and divine — as stages in nature’s progression toward completeness. The three completeness stages [thm-orientation, with data] are the fulfilled moments of the triad and map to plane transitions:

Transition Completeness From → to
T1 Constitutional (gathanpurnata) Physicochemical → delusional
T2 Activity (kriyapurnata) Delusional → deific
T3 Conduct (vyavaharpurnata) Deific → divine

T1 is the reflexive fixed point of §2.3 — irreversible at the atomic level (SB p. 92); T2 and T3 are awakening milestones within constitutionally complete . Four progressions must not be collapsed: existential (niyati-kram, the fixed order emergence material → pranic → animal → knowledge), way of existence (niyati-vidhi, definiteness of each order’s conduct), development (vikas-kram, through the physicochemical complex until T1), and awakening (jagriti-kram, within toward T2–T3).

Vikas-kram and jagriti-kram are one engine in two regimes, divided by the reflexive turn. Below it, a unit’s completeness is constitutional and its deficiency is met from outside — surplus particles supplied by complementary units through κ. At and above it, constitution is already complete, so the remaining deficiency is realizational: the faculties are not yet aligned and understanding is not yet attained, and this deficiency is met not by acquiring anything from without but by the ’s own activity turning inward. The drive is the same completeness-orientation (A2) carried by the same triad — kshobh, the “frustration of effort [that] itself is their yearning for restfulness” (MVD p. 104), is what A2 feels like once the activity has become reflexive. What changes across the turn is only the currency: from particles-and-bonding to understanding-and-alignment. So “the constitution is already complete, what develops?” has a clean answer — realization develops, not constitution.

4.8 Jeevan and the reflexive turn

[def, with data] is the unit at the reflexive fixed point (§2.3): a constitutionally complete composite atom (gathanpurna parmanu) whose result-aspect has reached immortality, after which “both effort and motion become inexhaustible” (SB p. 61). Its constitution is the atom’s nucleus-and-orbit structure — “The nucleus of the sentient unit… is referred to as atma. The particles in its first orbit… , the second… , the third… , the fourth… ” (MVD p. 78) — operating through the projection–reflection cycle (paravartan–pratyavartan; KD 3.12): selection and taste in , analysis and deliberation in , visualisation and contemplation in , resolve and enlightenment in , authenticity and realisation in atma (JV p. 92; MVD p. 13). The faculty inventory is taught data; that is where activity turns reflexive is the kernel’s structural claim. Since atma is mediative, the faculties and body are naturally regulated by it, and effort resisting that regulation is discord (MVD pp. 77, 277) — inward regulation parallel to the atomic nucleus. A human (and an animal) is the joint form of body and : acts through the body as instrument, is not dissolved at the body’s death, and the body is provisioned to evidence understanding (JV p. 59; KD 3.16). Formally the joint form is an action of on body-states, act : × Body → Body — asymmetric (the acts, the body is the instrument) and inseparable in function, not a symmetric pairing; which animates which body, and when, is a selection predicate the kernel hosts but does not fix — taught data, at the same tier as the T1 predicate (§10).

What differs between an animal and a human is not the but the body it operates. In an animal the sentient atom’s outer orbits — , , — are only partially active (MVD p. 79), so the body runs a narrow circuit: it senses, selects, and acts, but knowing and believing stay severed (MVD p. 278). Animal and deluded human alike run on four koshas; only the awakened human, in a fully developed medhas-body, opens the fifth — vigyanmaya, discernment (MVD pp. 49–50; KD 3.16). The body is thus an expression-gate: ’s capability is intrinsic and complete, but how much of it can function is fixed by the body, and full expression waits on a human body. This is why the human tier is the unfinished one — not because is incomplete, its constitution being already done, but because its realization is gated twice: by an understanding that can fail and by a body that must be human.

The realizational development (§4.7) has a definite mechanism. Each activity, if it is justified — if the reflexive cycle closes as justice, ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction (§4.9) — deposits the values, happiness and peace; misjudged activity does not. Happiness is therefore not a substance acquires but the result of justified conduct, and awakening is that result accumulated under the inward regulation of atma. Death and continuity are treated in the planned Death, Continuity, and Rebirth study; here the dyad enters only as the asymmetry that evaluation and evidence belong to , never to the body alone (JV p. 39).

4.9 Evaluation, justice, and the human goals

Evaluation μ [thm] is the reflexive comparison of value delivered against value inherent in a relationship. Because only a reflexive activity can take a coupling as its object, μ is automatically confined to — “ evaluates the values that emanate from itself… No external instrument is needed” (JV p. 139); “Values and evaluation are processes of understanding; they are not bodily or mechanical” (JV p. 39). That confinement is derived within the reflexive model (§2.3) — conditional on that interpretive reading, not an unconditional result. Over-, under-, and mis-evaluation (MVD p. 38) are the failure modes the reflexive tier alone admits.

Justice () [def] is the closed reflexive cycle — “Recognising relationships, fulfilling values, evaluating, and achieving mutual satisfaction is justice” (MVD p. 311; JV p. 55): the core cycle ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction, an operator over value, not a member of it. This is the knowledge-order coupling’s full anatomy — recognition, fulfilment, evaluation — so compound-closure κ at the human tier (an organisation, §4.4) fires exactly when this cycle closes collectively. Trust (vishwas) [data] is the value-name of a successful φ: “Trust is the act of fulfilling the inherent expectation of values in mutuality” (MVD p. 73). Justice is the operation; trust is the value it deposits (MVD p. 72). The knowledge order’s telos [data] is fourfold: “The goal of is happiness, and the human goal is resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence” (JV p. 165) — what the justice cycle evidences when it closes, scaled to humankind as undivided society (akhand samaj, SB pp. 246–247). These are provisions of coexistence, not automatic outcomes: delusion, accumulation detached from right-use, and fear-based sociality remain live failures (KD ch. 1).

4.10 Laws and propositions

The laws and propositions the texts assert, read through the reconstruction:

  • Universal recognition-fulfilment — every unit recognises and fulfils at the level of its order (JV p. 69); definite and inerrant for the first three orders, achieved and fallible for .
  • Complementarity, not struggle — the coupling engine is mutual offering and acceptance, “there is no conflict in existence” (JV p. 157). The texts reject conflict and the pursuit of “more” (JV p. 41) as the engine; the reconstruction accordingly models complementarity rather than competition — a choice of telos, not a proof that optimization is unrepresentable.
  • Assembly by complementary need — κ’s trigger: hungry/overfull bonding (MVD p. 8), atoms into molecules into structures (JV p. 67), gravitation itself read as bonding participation (JV p. 150; KD 3.8).
  • Persistence ⇔ fulfilment — §4.5.
  • Transmission by order — §4.5.
  • Closure / iteration — κ’s output is a unit, so the schema re-applies; this organizes the tiered hierarchy.
  • Self-evidencing closure — complete knowledge (paripoorna , SB p. 116) requires the reflexive cycle to close in evidenced conduct and transmissible tradition (MVD p. 12).

Four propositions the texts also assert follow. The tiered hierarchy (particles → atoms → molecules → planets; cells → organisms; individual → family → undivided society, MVD p. 161) is asserted by the texts and organised by κ’s iteration. Stability at the ends, dynamism between — O is state-complete and existence stable, while units develop definitely within that stability (MVD p. 5, p. 12). The knowledge-order discontinuity is a conditional consequence of the reflexive model (§2.3): first-order operations execute by constitution and are inerrant, while the reflexive operations of route through a self-model and can misfire, so human assembly is the unique unfinished tier — “This comprises only humans, who are yet to achieve orderliness” (JV p. 47). Boundedness of extraction — a unit’s couplings with lower-order units are governed by right-use bounded by regeneration (MVD p. 264; KD 3.13); extraction beyond regeneration is an unfulfilled coupling that destabilises the containing assembly (JV p. 77).

4.11 Time as duration of activity

Time () enters the reconstruction not as a background parameter but as a measure on activity: “Duration of an activity is referred to as time” (MVD p. 34); “time is measured by the duration of an activity” (SB p. 65). Formally [formal reconstruction], take a commutative duration monoid (D, ⊕, 0) and grade the activity dynamics of §6.1 over it: every activity-execution e (a φ-run, one turn of the triad) carries a duration δ(e) ∈ D, identities carry δ = 0, and durations accumulate along a stream, δ(e₂ ∘ e₁) = δ(e₁) ⊕ δ(e₂). Time is thus the ⊕-accumulation of a unit’s own triad-turnover — activity-indexed, not clock-indexed — and it attaches to the morphisms (activity), never to units at rest and never to O.

’s timelessness is then a corollary of the A1 firewall, not a separate posit: O contributes no update term, so its only “activity” is the identity, which carries δ = 0 — -shunya entails -shunya. O is present at all times only in the sense of omnipresence (it pervades every activity), not of undergoing duration (JV p. 42; full treatment in Nature of Time and The Ontology of Coexistence §1.6.4). The present is the currently-executing activity and alone carries efficacy; a “clock” is a distinguished recurring sub-activity whose ⊕-iterates scale other durations, and past and future are recurrence-indexed positions relative to the live activity — so numerical clock-time is taught convention (KD 3.15), not the primitive.

Time must not be conflated with the four progressions (§4.7). A progression orders states toward A2 (ordinal, teleological); measures the duration of the activity that carries a unit between states (a magnitude, non-teleological). They come apart: an atom cycling in natural-state motion, or a stalled deluded , accrues much with no vikas or jagriti at all. Progression is where A2 points; is how much activity has turned over. The /rachna/vistar taxonomy and the units of reckoning are taught data (MVD p. 196).

4.12 The natural state as an attractor: least excitation

The natural/excited distinction (§4.5) has a dynamical reading [mathematical analogy]. On its motion-path M (§4.1), read as the admissible-configuration space, give a unit a non-negative excitation functional K (kshobh, §4.3) that vanishes exactly on the natural-state set-point N. Driven by A1’s triad, oriented by A2, and disciplined by the mediative regulation of the nucleus (MVD p. 26) — a two-way influence that corrects both too-far and too-near — the realized activity is a relaxation (Lyapunov) flow: K is non-increasing along activity, so N is an asymptotically stable rest configuration and K > 0 is the excited, off-attractor state. Locally, κ-closure is an excitation nulled; mixture is excitation that does not null. Globally, A2 carries a unit through an ascending family of set-points toward a terminal attractor — constitutional completeness (T1) on the atomic line. Persistence (§4.5) is that attractor sustained. This is the process content the texts describe when excitation “works toward natural-state motion” and heat decays to its normal value (KD 3.5; companion analysis in The Ontology of Coexistence physics research note).

This is regulation, not optimization, and it is exactly the representation the reframed A2 (§2.2) permits: a qualitative terminal condition represented by minimizing deviation K from a bounded set-point, not an unbounded scalar objective. The two-way restoring tendency is the signature — an optimizer pushes one direction toward “more”; a regulator returns a variable to its home from either side. So least-excitation is the mathematical form of “not indefinite quantitative increase”, not a claim that optimization is unrepresentable.

Hamilton’s least action is the tempting-but-wrong mapping. It is conservative and time-symmetric, whereas MD’s development is directed and irreversible (T1 irreversible, SB p. 92) with an arrow that runs against the entropic one; it extremizes a definite conserved scalar ∫(T−V)dt, whereas MD conserves the countability of units, not any action integral; and its “least” is really stationary — an extremum of a path pinned at both ends — whereas MD asserts a terminal state reached asymptotically. Three honesty flags travel with the reading: K is not energy (A1 conservation is untouched — activeness is inexhaustible and natural-state motion never stops; what relaxes is deviation from the set-point, not the underlying activity); K’s content — what “restfulness” and the set-points are — is taught data; and the two load-bearing physics-denials — the mediative tendency as a primitive regulator and the development arrow against entropy — are open metaphysical posits, exactly where a physicist will press. The reading is literal at the material tier (an excited atom relaxing to natural-state motion) and analogical at the tier (kshobh → restfulness of effort, §4.7), inheriting the reflexive model’s conditionality; the two rests — physical ease and terminal completeness — stay distinct. The full category-theoretic comparison — a forgetful functor to physics, the gradient-flow formalization — is deferred to the planned Restfulness and Least Action study.

6. The formal spine

The template invites precise notation. Instead of a system of many categories, one dual-facing spine carries the load, with richer constructions kept as optional annotations.

6.1 Two readings: carrier-behaviour and generation

Downward — carrier-coalgebra. A unit is a carrier x together with a behaviour map x → F(x) unfolding its activity (the triad and its couplings). The carrier is not quotiented by bisimulation and the theory does not pass to the final coalgebra — so the carrier exceeds its behaviour. This is the co-structuralist dual of the Yoneda principle, and it is what keeps a unit substantial and a real entity rather than a functional role, while letting O stay actionless: A1 grounds that the behaviour exists and is lawful, contributing no term to the unfolding.

Upward — generation as an initial algebra. As a formal reconstruction, the units of nature can be encoded as the algebra generated by κ from the primitive units — an initial-algebra / W-type presentation. This is the paper’s encoding, not machinery the texts contain; within it, definitions over units (value, transmission, evaluation) become definitions by recursion on how a unit was built, and mixture is typed off the inductive sort, so “composition is not development” reads as a typing fact.

6.2 The two load-bearing constructs

Two constructs carry most of the encoding’s weight. The closure operator κ (§6.1) organizes the hierarchy and iterates by construction. The society gluing colimit encodes undivided society: families and communities with shared members glue along overlaps, and the gluing is coherent exactly when local value-structures agree on what they share. This is κ at the social tier, and it is the one place the encoding adds a compatibility observation rather than merely restating the darshan: the gluing is coherent only if overlapping assignments actually agree — a descent-style condition whose textual bridge the applied study How Undivided Society Is Established §4.6 supplies.

The compatibility condition this colimit turns on is not a formal hope — the darshan asserts it. “exists in all humans in the same form, with identical strengths and powers”, and “since the goal of is happiness, all humans aspire for happiness” (JV p. 40); “All human endeavours and experimentations are aimed at achieving satisfaction or happiness” (MVD p. 64); “Human kind is one… Human is one” (MVD p. 13). The agreement is conditioned on awakening — “We humans are united in the right and divided in the wrong” (JV p. 17) — so the diagram is compatible and the colimit glues into an undivided whole exactly in the awakened case, while delusion is where local value-structures disagree and the gluing degenerates (P3). Undivided society is therefore not an incidental output but the telos the whole structure is driven toward: the four human goals are the shared value-content every aspires to, and the society colimit closes coherently precisely when that content is recognised across members. The content of those shared values remains taught data; that the aspiration is one, and the agreement real in the right, is what the texts assert — the substantive premise the compatibility condition names.

6.3 Optional annotations

Beyond the spine, several richer categorical models sharpen specific clauses without carrying the theory, and are offered as annotations rather than foundations: reduction to physics as a non-faithful forgetful functor (value-distinct acts collapse); delusion as a retract mistaken for an isomorphism (comfort a genuine part of fulfilment, not the whole, MVD Ch. 4); right-use as a natural transformation uniform across domains; graded fulfilment as enrichment over a satisfaction preorder, fibred over capacity; the activity triad as a traced joint form and κ-bookkeeping as a Petri net; the faculty cycle as a projection–reflection sketch (KD 3.12); and the actualisation of sentience at T1 as a guarded partial morphism — the Saturation-Reflector reading whose selection predicate is taught data, developed in The Ontology of Coexistence §§1.2, 1.6.1 (open problems in §6.2.1); time as a grading over a duration monoid (D, ⊕, 0) with O the monoidal unit (δ = 0; §4.11); and the natural/excited state as a Lyapunov/attractor structure on the coalgebra — K descending to the set-point N, with the terminal attractor at T1 (§4.12). Each is conditional — a structural claim relative to a named Madhyasth premise — and none is a theorem about reality.

8. What the structure is not

The most natural modern glosses quietly replace the structure with something else. It is not driven by indefinite increase: the darshan does not make unbounded “more” the telos — conduct in the first three orders is definite (JV p. 113), object-usefulness is constant (JV p. 123), and “more” is the diagnosed pathology (JV p. 41). A terminal state can, of course, be represented by minimising distance to it; the claim is not that optimisation is unrepresentable but that indefinite quantitative increase is not the goal, so this reconstruction encodes completeness as a qualitative terminal condition rather than an unbounded scalar objective. It is not struggle or selection: the assembly engine is complementarity, explicitly opposed to conflict and survival-of-the-fittest (JV p. 157); decomposition is the loss of structure when relations go unfulfilled, not its designer. It is not bottom-up emergence alone: capacities are provisioned in existence and manifest when composition reaches the right tier (JV p. 59), and O energises every tier at once — a purely emergentist reading drops O and turns energisation into magic. And it is not value-as-epiphenomenon: value is the content of fulfilment at every tier (SB p. 50), and the human innovation is a second-order operation on it, located in , “not bodily or mechanical” (JV p. 39).

9. Relation to other frameworks, and uses

Stated this generally, the structure invites comparison — useful chiefly for locating what is distinctive.

Framework Shared structure Where it differs
Holarchy (Koestler) every unit is whole and part no medium O, no value in relations, no transmission
Autopoiesis (Maturana & Varela) persistence by continuous self-production living systems only; the structure runs from atoms to societies and grounds persistence in fulfilment
Multilevel selection nested units persisting or failing as wholes selectionist engines are competitive and stochastic; here the engine is complementarity and definite
Thermodynamic self-organisation larger structures from flows between non-equilibrium units dissipative structures need free-energy throughput; here units are energised constitutively by saturation
Category-theoretic composition relationships as morphisms; assemblies as colimits; tiers as a partial order notation for κ and iteration, but silent on which compositions occur; complementarity is the selection rule the notation lacks

Two commitments are genuinely distinctive: the medium O as a constitutive, energising, regulating presence at every tier at once — a precondition of physics, not an aether within it; and value as the universal content of relations, with evaluation appearing only at the top tier. Frameworks that drop the first must find energy and regulation inside the units; frameworks that drop the second must reconstruct value as preference or fitness.

The uses follow the forward and reverse readings of §1. For human organisations, a deliberate assembly persists only if its relationships are recognised and their values actually fulfilled — not merely incentivised — and it transmits its understanding to incoming members; an organisation that fulfils values but does not transmit decays on turnover, one that transmits rules without fulfilment holds only as long as enforcement does (How To Form Self-Sustaining Organizations; How Undivided Society Is Established). For ecology and economics, boundedness of extraction turns sustainability from an ethical add-on into a stability condition. For AI and value alignment, graded fulfilment is an argument for multi-level objectives over one scalar reward — optimising a single number maximises the wrong thing, which is the darshan’s own diagnosis of “more”. And for the study of the darshan itself, the kernel is a checklist for reading any passage: identify the unit, the tier, the couplings, the expectation profile, the value, the conformance regime, and the transmission carrier, and much apparent repetition resolves into the same move at different tiers.

10. Limitations and open problems

Formalisation is interpretive. The symbols (O, U, A1, A2, κ, μ, τ, δ) do not appear in the texts; they are this paper’s compression, warranted by the citations. Every reconstruction is this paper’s construction.

The heavy posits, named. The simplification concentrates the theory’s contestable weight onto A2’s teleology — completeness as a terminal orientation — and onto two selection predicates the kernel hosts but does not derive: which atomic configuration reaches constitutional completeness (T1), and which animates which body (the act pairing, §4.8) — and, in the dynamical reading, the mediative tendency as a primitive regulator and the development arrow against entropy (§4.12). A physicist critic will press exactly there. That the reflexive turn at that fixed point yields evaluation is this paper’s organising reading, not a quotation (§2.3, §4.8); it is offered because it explains with one device why evaluation exists only where constitutional completeness does.

The residual premise. Universal coupling rests on the no-isolation premise (JV p. 43; §2.4) — a textual assertion the reconstruction adopts rather than derives; that units couple at all does not follow from A1 alone. This is the one place the kernel leans on a premise beyond its axioms.

Substantiality and Yoneda. Category theory is structuralist by default, so a faithful categorical reading tends to re-describe as a functional role. The carrier-coalgebra reading (§6.1), with carrier-primacy stipulated (§2.1) and the final coalgebra refused, holds the anti-structuralist line by commitment rather than leaving it open — but the tension with structuralist reduction is a boundary of the tools.

Translation risk. Quotations from MVD, SB, and JV follow the published English translations; several key terms (vyavastha as “orderliness”, as “saturated”) carry translators’ choices. Citations to Manav Karm Darshan (KD) are to a machine-assisted working translation, not a published one; they are given as corroboration only where MVD/SB/JV already carry the claim, and KD-only material should be verified against the Hindi page images before release. The corpus beyond these four — Vyavaharatmak Janvad and Anubhavatmak Adhyatmvad — is not yet cited.

No empirical contact. Nothing here yields a measurement or prediction of , coexistence, or constitutional completeness. Elegant diagrams can create an illusion of proof; the conditional framing and this list are the safeguard.

Open problems. Formal countermodels establishing the independence of A1 and A2; discharging or confirming the no-isolation assumption; a mechanised recovery audit; sharpening the reflexive reading into a definition that derives the three evaluation failures from second-order coupling structure; checking the society-gluing descent condition; the full category-theoretic comparison of the least-excitation reading (§4.12) to the least-action principle — a forgetful functor to physics, a gradient-flow formalization, and the development-arrow-versus-entropy question — deferred to the planned Restfulness and Least Action study; and the selection predicate for constitutional completeness, which converts the emergence question into a selection question without discharging it.

11. Conclusion

Read from first principles, ’s ground is coexistence itself: one actionless medium and countless real units, bound by saturation. From that ground two axioms — endowment and completeness-orientation — and one generative move — complementary closure — organize the structure: activeness as manifestation, value as what a coupling realises, composition as closure that mints a new unit, transmission as method recurring across turnover, and the tiered world as the iteration of the one schema — literal in the physical lines, analogical at the human tier. A distinct reflexive move at constitutional completeness opens the knowledge order — evaluation, justice, and the single tier that must pass through understanding, and can therefore fail. Time is the duration of that activity, not a backdrop for it; and the natural state reads as the attractor a unit relaxes toward. Three primitives, two axioms, one move: what the reconstruction organizes it labels as such — textual assertion, formal reconstruction, mathematical analogy, or open posit — and what it cannot organize it names, once and plainly, as the taught content of the darshan.
