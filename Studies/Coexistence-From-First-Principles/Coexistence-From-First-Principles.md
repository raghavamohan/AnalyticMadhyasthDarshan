# Coexistence From First Principles

## The kernel and reconstructed structure of Madhyasth Darshan

**Author:** [AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan.org](https://github.com/raghavamohan/AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan) — a group of people studying Madhyasth Darshan philosophy. Source repository: [raghavamohan/AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan](https://github.com/raghavamohan/AnalyticMadhyasthDarshan).

**Status:** Draft
**The thesis:** Madhyasth Darshan's ontology **can be reconstructed** from a small kernel — **three primitives** (the omnipresent medium *satta*, real countable units, and their saturation), **two axioms** (endowment and completeness-orientation), and **one generative move** (complementary units close into a new unit, iterated up the tiers). Activeness — the inseparable triad of effort, motion, and result — is not a further primitive but the manifestation the first axiom confers, so the equation *Unit + energy-fullness = activeness* (SB p. 69) reads as a theorem within the reconstruction, not a postulate. From this kernel the darshan's structure is **reconstructed**: couplings as definite mutualities, value as what a coupling realises, composition as coupling that closes a new boundary, transmission as the recurrence of a composition method, and — at the fixed point where a compound atom reaches constitutional completeness — *jeevan*, whose activity takes activity as its object, switching on evaluation, justice, and the one tier of the structure that can fail. A recovery audit names, for every notion, its **provenance and epistemic standing** — textual assertion, formal reconstruction, mathematical analogy, taught datum, or open metaphysical posit — and a single categorical spine carries the parts mathematics can carry, naming exactly where it stops. The claim is that the kernel **organizes** the darshan's structure — not that the texts derive it.

## 1. One schema, across the orders

Madhyasth Darshan is usually read through its human programme — justice in relationships, family-based order, humane education. The texts support a stronger reading: the general structure is asserted first, and the human case is organized as its most demanding instance. *Samadhanatmak Bhautikvad* opens in fully general terms —

> **"Each unit is a whole along with its environment."**
> — SB p. 13–14

> **"Each unit is orderliness with its ness and participates in overall orderliness."**
> — SB p. 13–14 (*ness* = *self-ness* in the bilingual edition; PDF extraction drops the prefix)

— and *Manav Vyavahar Darshan* states the same universality: "All units saturated in the Omnipresence (permeative and transparent) have form, properties, essential nature & dharma, and have inherent orderliness & participate in overall orderliness" (MVD p. 11). *Manav Karm Darshan*, whose title-page principle is simply "Effort – Motion – Result" (KD, Principle), develops the same ground as a science of activity from the atom upward.

So the task is not extrapolation from humans to atoms but **articulation**: stating, in one structure, what the texts assert piecewise about atoms (MVD p. 8; JV p. 67), cells (JV p. 82), organisms (SB p. 16), and human collectives (MVD p. 55, p. 161). The whole of it can be held in one picture before any formalism:

- **Ground.** There is one omnipresent, actionless medium, *satta*, and there are countless real units. Every unit is *saturated* in the medium — soaked, submerged, surrounded. That saturation, and nothing added to it, makes each unit energy-replete and law-legible, so each unit is **active**: its activity is effort, motion, and result together.
- **Couple.** Units couple by **complementarity** — one's result entering the other as input, never competition. Complementarity takes an order-specific form; its material instance is a unit short of completeness meeting one in surplus (deficiency and surplus).
- **Close and recur.** When a complementary material coupling closes a new boundary, it *is* a new unit, one tier up, with its own signature. The closure schema reappears in typed forms: literal *yaugik* compound-closure in the physicochemical line, compositional organization in the biological line, and an explicit conduct analogy for individuals organizing into families and undivided society.
- **Run reflexively.** At the fixed point where a composite atom reaches constitutional completeness, its activity can take *activity itself* as its object. That reflexive turn is *jeevan*, the sentient unit — and with it, uniquely, come evaluation, justice, and the possibility of failure.

One closure schema organizes the tiered structure; at constitutional completeness a distinct **reflexive** move (§2.3) opens the knowledge order. A worked instance runs through the whole paper in miniature: two atoms, one hungry and one overfull (MVD p. 8), couple by complementarity, close into a molecule with a new conduct (MVD p. 42), which re-enters the same schema in order-specific form toward the planetary and biological lines; carried up the development progression to constitutional completeness, the same activity turns reflexive and is *jeevan* (SB p. 61).

Precision earns its keep by letting the structure be **carried to systems Shri Nagraj did not himself analyse**. Forward, as design: an organisation, an economy, or an institution is **humane and internally self-sustaining** exactly when it satisfies each reconstructed clause — identifiable units, definite couplings with explicit expectation profiles, capacity sufficient for value to flow, composition by complementary need, persistence conditional on fulfilment, and an explicit carrier of transmission across member turnover. (This is a criterion for humane, coercion-free order — not a law of empirical survival.) In reverse, as diagnosis: a failing system is walked clause by clause until the unmet one is found, and the knowledge-order discontinuity predicts that human-tier systems fail first at the understanding-dependent steps.

Three cautions govern what follows. The texts *assert* piecewise; this paper *reconstructs* — every reconstruction is this paper's construction, warranted by citations but not identical with them. The formalism is a **lens for clarity**, not a proof machine: it proves nothing about the reality of the medium or of *jeevan*, which are exactly where the darshan parts from mainstream science. And a reduction can flatten: the kernel keeps units co-eternal with the medium — real carriers, not clusters distilled from activity — and refuses to reconstruct away what the texts present as taught content.

**Model status.** Every claim carries a status along **two independent axes**, and the paper is explicit about both. **Textual provenance** — is the claim a **textual assertion** (stated in MVD/SB/JV/KD), a **formal reconstruction** (the paper's own kernel-level organisation — κ, the coupling anatomy), or a **mathematical analogy** (the categorical spine of §6, the physical readings)? **Epistemic standing** — is it **established within the model** (a result of the reconstruction), a **taught datum** (content the texts fix and the model cannot produce), or an **open metaphysical posit** (asserted, not established)? The two axes cross: *satta* and *jeevan* are textual assertions *and* open metaphysical posits; κ is a formal reconstruction established within the model; the six value-kinds are textual assertions that remain taught data. The audit's role-tags (*primitive*, *axiom*, *definition*, *theorem*, *data*) locate a notion *within* the reconstruction — so a "theorem" there is a result *within the model*, not a proof about the world. The kernel **organizes** the darshan's structure; it does not claim the texts derive it.

## 2. The kernel

### 2.1 Three primitives

**O** (*satta*, Omnipresence) — one, real, all-pervasive, **actionless**, state-complete, and energy- and knowledge-natured (*gyan*): "Space is uniform in all dimensions… this reality is omnipresent" (JV p. 31); state-complete while everything else is state-dynamic (MVD p. 12). O is not a unit, and no statement quantifying over units applies to O.

**U** (units, *ikai*) — real, co-eternal, countable **carriers**: "Nature is in the form of entities that can be counted" (JV p. 45). A unit *bears* a state and an activity; it is never *built from* activity, occasions, or process. This carrier-primacy is a commitment, not a convenience: a unit is not identified with its behaviour (§6, §10).

**Saturation** — the one ground relation: every unit is soaked, submerged, and surrounded in the single O, with "no provision in existence to separate units of nature from Omnipresence" (JV p. 18). Saturation is constitutive co-location in a shared medium, the *first* ontological relation, distinct from any unit-to-unit coupling, and not a physical drawing from O (SB pp. 49, 62).

Activeness is deliberately **not** a primitive. The texts state its status as an identity, "Unit + Energy fullness = Activeness" (SB p. 69) — a co-presence conferred by saturation, recovered below as a theorem (§2.4), not a fourth building block.

### 2.2 Two axioms

**A1 (Endowment — the *satta* firewall).** Through saturation, O endows each unit's carrier with three distinguishable but inseparable invariants. **(a) Activeness:** inexhaustible energy manifests as the triad of effort (*shram*), motion (*gati*), and result (*parinam*) — "There is no unit or particle in nature which is not replete with energy… This activeness is seen in every unit in the form of effort, motion and result" (SB p. 69; KD 3.1). **(b) Recognition-capability:** activity is unit-to-unit legible, since "there is provision in every unit for recognising one another based on law" (SB p. 57). **(c) Inherent regulation:** that lawfulness disciplines a unit's activity in its natural state and appears at successive orders as structural-, seed-, species-, and *sanskar*-conformance (§4.6). Recognition and regulation are two consequences of lawful intelligibility, not one undifferentiated capacity: the former opens mutuality, the latter maintains definite conduct. The firewall is part of the axiom: **O is never an input to, nor a summand of, a unit's activity** — it provisions energy, legibility, and regulation without push, update term, or depletion. The nucleus within each atom, and mediative *atma* within *jeevan*, localise regulation without O ever becoming an agent (MVD p. 26).

**A2 (Completeness-orientation).** Every unit's activity is directed toward **development and completeness** — "each unit, embedded in the complete, is active and developing solely for completeness" (SB p. 51) — and not toward **indefinite quantitative increase**. This orientation is universal, but its **terminal triadic form** is tiered. Along the atomic development line it culminates at **constitutional completeness** — "every atom continues to undergo insertion and expulsion of particles until constitutional completeness" (SB p. 71), the threshold at which *jeevan* appears (T1). The fully-realized terminal goals — **restfulness of effort, destination of motion, immortality of result** (SB p. 58) — are the awakening targets of *jeevan* (activity- and conduct-completeness, T2–T3); the first three orders realize completeness as development toward order, not as consciously-attained restfulness. The darshan does not treat indefinite quantitative increase as the telos; this reconstruction therefore represents completeness as a **qualitative terminal condition**, not an unbounded scalar objective (§8).

### 2.3 The generative move

**κ (compound-closure).** Given saturated units whose ports are **complementary** — in the material instance a deficiency met by a surplus, with complementarity taking an order-specific form at higher tiers (§7) — their activities couple and, when the coupling closes a *new* motion-path, present one bounded whole: a new unit, one tier up, with a fresh signature whose components "discard their own conducts, and present another kind of conduct" (MVD p. 42). Written as a rule:

```text
  u₁, …, uₙ ∈ U_o saturated in O        C_o(u₁, …, uₙ)        closure of a new motion-path
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        κ(u₁, …, uₙ) ∈ U   —   a new unit, new signature, +1 tier
```

Here `U_o` is the type of units at order `o`, and `C_o` is the **order-indexed complementarity relation**. Deficiency met by surplus is the material instance; nutrient and seasonal fit, species and lineage fit, and understood value-expectation are higher-order instances (§7). The rule's premises are the **anatomy of a coupling**, spelled out in §4.3: **recognition** (ρ) identifies that `C_o` holds and fixes the expectation profile; **fulfilment** (φ) executes the coupling, with results flowing along that profile; and **closure** asks whether the executed coupling settles into one new bounded motion-path. Thus **κ_o(u₁,…,uₙ) = closure(φ_o(ρ_o(u₁,…,uₙ)))**. **Mixture** (*mishran*) is the same coupling when it does **not** close: φ still delivers results — "components all maintain their respective conducts" (MVD p. 42) — but no new boundary forms. Compound and mixture are the two closure outcomes of a fulfilled material coupling, which is why "composition is not development" (SB pp. 75–76). Because κ's output is again a unit, the closure schema reappears in an order-specific form — literal compound-closure in the physicochemical line, compositional organization in the biological line, and a declared formal analogy at the knowledge order (§4.4).

**The reflexive turn.** κ iterated along the development progression (*vikas-kram*) — "in the atom in the form of hungry and overfull atoms" (MVD p. 8) — reaches a distinguished fixed point: a composite atom whose result-aspect attains its A2 terminal, *constitutional completeness*, after which "both effort and motion become inexhaustible" (SB p. 61). At that fixed point the unit's activity can take *activity itself* as its object. This reflexive re-application is **jeevan**, and it is the sole locus of evaluation and of possible failure (§4.8). Reflexivity is a distinct move, not a second literal firing of κ; it is **an interpretive model** of the knowledge order — this paper's organising reading, not a derived theorem — flagged as such in the audit.

### 2.4 Corollaries

**Activeness.** By A1(a), every saturated unit is energy-replete and therefore active; its activity is the triad. "Unit + Energy fullness = Activeness" (SB p. 69) is thereby a theorem, and activeness is a *family indexed by units*, not a substrate they are built from. The triad's internal constitution is a joint form — each moment the outcome of the other two (MVD p. 105) — and its combined activity is basic impulsion (*mool cheshta*, MVD p. 40). KD states each aspect's sense directly: effort is the grandeur of the existent-state, motion the grandeur of spreading effect, result the grandeur of a different existent-state (KD 3.11).

**Conservation.** The texts assert existence is beginless and stable — "Existence neither increases nor decreases" (JV p. 127). Conservation of *activity* does not strictly follow from constancy of *existence*; the reconstruction takes it as a **model postulate**, consistent with A1's inexhaustible source and A2's non-annihilating orientation but warranted textually rather than derived.

**Coupling and non-isolation.** That units couple does not follow from shared saturation and activeness alone — independently active units need not interact. The texts supply the missing premise directly: "Nothing is isolated – that is the principle" (JV p. 43). The reconstruction therefore takes **no-isolation as a premise** (a textual assertion): every unit participates in at least one coupling, and universal coupling holds because of it — rather than separating coupling as a theorem from a residual totality assumption.

## 3. Notation: category theory in brief

The formal spine of §6 uses category theory; this section gives the working vocabulary in plain language so the paper is self-contained, and readers at home with it can skip ahead.

Category theory replaces "what is each thing made of?" with "how does each thing relate to everything else?". An **object** is a thing to talk about; a **morphism** (arrow) is a relationship or a way from one object to another; **composition** chains arrows, and every object has an **identity** arrow. Two dual readings matter here. An **algebra** for a construction is a thing *built up* by a rule — the natural home of "generated": the units of nature can be encoded as the algebra generated by κ, so definitions over units become definitions by recursion on how a unit was built. A **coalgebra** is the dual — a carrier together with a map that *unfolds* its behaviour without identifying the carrier with that behaviour; it keeps a unit a substance that bears an activity rather than a bundle of relations. A **colimit (gluing)** joins pieces into one whole along what they share — the shape of families joining into one society. These three — the generating algebra upward, the carrier-coalgebra downward, and the gluing colimit sideways — are all the machinery the spine needs; the richer constructions are collected as optional annotations (§6.3).

## 4. The reconstructed structure

The kernel organizes the notions below without claiming to generate all their content. Each notion is tagged **[def]** (a name for kernel structure), **[thm]** (a consequence within the reconstruction), or **[data]** (taught content the kernel cannot produce). The fuller provenance and epistemic audit appears in §5.

### 4.1 Units, activeness, and signature

A **unit** **[def]** is a saturated carrier in U, active as the triad (§2.4), disciplined along the motion-path that is at once its boundary and line of regulation. Insentient units are "active within the bounds of their length, width and height"; sentient units are "active… beyond the bounds" (MVD p. 33) — the motion-path bounds the activity's extent without bounding its reach. An **atom** (*parmanu*) is not the elementary particle of physics: its kind, state, and measure are fixed by the number of particles in its nucleus (*madhyansh*) and its orbiting particles (MVD p. 42; KD 3.1).

Each unit carries a **signature** **[def, with data]** — the four-tuple form (*roop*), properties (*gun*), essential nature (*svabhav*), and *dharma* (MVD p. 11) — minted fresh at each compound tier. The four-tuple *form* is definitional; the per-order values are taught data:

| Order | Essential nature (*svabhav*) | *Dharma* (cumulative) |
|---|---|---|
| Material (*padarth*) | integration–disintegration | existence (*astitva*) |
| Bio (*pran*) | vitalising–devitalising | + growth (*pushti*) |
| Animal (*jeev*) | cruel–uncruel | + hope to live |
| Knowledge / human (*gyan*) | fortitude, courage, generosity, kindness, grace, compassion | + happiness (*sukh*) |

### 4.2 Relationships and value

Every unit-to-unit mutuality is a **coupling** — the general term, at every order. A **relationship** (*sambandh*) **[def]** is the knowledge-order coupling whose expectation profile **E(r)** is predetermined "in the sense of completeness" between *jeevan*-bearing units (MVD p. 61–62); an **association** is a coupling whose profile is adopted. The lower orders have definite coupling and mutuality, not *sambandh* in this value-laden sense. The **value** of a unit in a coupling **[thm-slot, with data]** is what that coupling realises — its essentiality (*maulikta*), "the essentiality of units in every plane and order… It is values that are reciprocated and mutually recognised" (SB p. 50). That value is real at every tier because coupling is; *which* values fill the slot is taught data. The texts sort essentiality into six kinds — object values (utility and art) operating at all orders, and *jeevan*, human, established, and expression values operating only at the knowledge order and presupposing *jeevan* (MVD p. 306; JV pp. 43, 138–139). The nine established values flow from *jeevan* the moment a relationship is recognised — "when a mother has recognised her child then motherly care starts surging by itself" (JV p. 138) — in a generative order (gratitude → affection, love, trust; JV p. 108), so V carries a partial ordering.

### 4.3 Recognition, fulfilment, and capacity

**Recognition** ρ **[def]** is A1(b) exercised between units — identification of a coupling and its profile. **Fulfilment** φ **[def]** is the coupling executing: "Fulfilling is to evidence use, right-use and purposeful-use" (MVD p. 27, p. 62). Fulfilment is modulated by **capacity** **[data]** — the intellectual means capacity (*kshamata*), ability (*yogyata*), and receptivity (*patrata*) (MVD p. 62): φ is realised at the level these permit, not by receptivity alone. In the material order the conversion of capacity and ability into effort is *ascending* (*agreshan*); its shortfall is frustration (*kshobh*), "incompleteness of receptivity" (MVD p. 79) — the triad failing to close toward restfulness, which at the human tier is "their yearning for restfulness" that drives awakening (MVD p. 104). Recognition and fulfilment are the two phases of every coupling; the generative move κ (§2.3, §4.4) is what a fulfilled coupling becomes when it closes. At the knowledge order the coupling gains a third phase, evaluation μ (§4.9), and the closed cycle ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction is what the texts call justice.

### 4.4 Composition: compound and mixture

Composition is the generative move κ (§2.3). **Compound** (*yaugik*) closes a new motion-path and mints a new unit with a new signature; **mixture** (*mishran*) does not, and yields a non-generating aggregate whose components "all maintain their respective conducts" (MVD p. 42) — so mere enlargement never crosses an order plateau ("composition is not development", SB pp. 75–76).

κ names literal *yaugik* compound-closure in the physicochemical line. The same formal schema organizes biological composition without asserting that every cell-to-organism organization is *yaugik* in exactly the atomic sense, and it is extended only analogically to human organization, where constitutionally complete humans organize into families, communities, and undivided society (MVD p. 55). The **sentience-creating** instance of closure — constitutional completeness (T1) — runs only along the atomic development progression (*vikas-kram*): new sentience is an atomic threshold (SB p. 92; KD 3.2–3.3), so composing *jeevan* yields an assembly of *jeevan*, never a larger *jeevan*. At the knowledge order composition is achieved: voluntary, understanding-mediated, and fallible, requiring shared cause, goal, programme, and transmission. Awakening progression (*jagriti-kram*) is not κ; it runs within an already constitutionally complete *jeevan* toward T2–T3 (§4.8).

At the knowledge order the closure test acquires definite content. A human assembly is treated in the reconstruction **analogically as compound-like** — a **formal conduct-compound** — exactly when its collective coupling closes: shared cause and goal recognised (ρ), values fulfilled (φ), mutual satisfaction reached (μ — justice at scale, §4.9), and the method carried across member turnover by education-*sanskar* (τ, §4.5). Such an assembly **organizes as a whole that exhibits the four human goals — resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence** — and persists as a self-continuing tradition in coexistence (MVD p. 55; SB pp. 246–247) — the *goal* of human assembly, its assembly-scale analogue of the awakening transitions T2–T3. An assembly held together by fear, accumulation, or enforcement fails the *humane* test — it is not self-reproducing without coercion and lacks internal orderliness — even where it survives empirically for a time by enforcement; the criterion (P3) concerns humane, coercion-free order, not a law of institutional survival. Two cautions keep the analogy honest: it is a **conduct-compound, not a sentience-compound** — no collective *atma*, since sentience-creating closure (T1) is atomic-only; and the texts support an *organized* family, community, and undivided society, not clearly a new *yaugik* unit in the same sense as a molecule — so "compound" here is a **formal analogy**, not a claim that the assembly is a fresh unit of nature. This is the exact sense in which composing *jeevan* yields an *organized assembly* of *jeevan*, never a larger *jeevan*. The chain is instantiated in [How To Form Self-Sustaining Organizations](../How-To-Form-Self-Sustaining-Organizations/How-To-Form-Self-Sustaining-Organizations.pdf) and, at the scale of humankind, [How Undivided Society Is Established](../How-Undivided-Society-Is-Established/How-Undivided-Society-Is-Established.pdf).

### 4.5 State, persistence, and transmission

A unit is in its **natural state** **[def]** when its couplings are fulfilled within conducive conditions and in an **excited state** otherwise: "Each unit moves towards development in its natural state and decline in its excited state" (SB p. 14). In triad terms the excited state is the activity failing to close toward its A2 target. **Persistence** **[thm, of humane order]** follows: an assembly's *self-sustaining, coercion-free* order holds exactly while its couplings stay fulfilled and lapses when they do not — natural-state closure sustained. This is a criterion for humane, internally-reproduced order, not a necessary-and-sufficient law of empirical survival: a coercive assembly can endure for a time by enforcement. Conducive conditions are order-relative; in the material order even an excited state can be complementary — an expelled particle feeds a hungry atom (SB p. 15), a fulfilled coupling (φ) that delivers results without closing into a new unit — while in the deluded knowledge order excitation harms both self and others (SB p. 16).

**Transmission** τ **[def, with data]** re-instantiates an assembly's method of composition (*rachna vidhi*) in new member-units, "inherent in the pran sutra situated in the cell" (JV p. 82). Its form — a result whose content is a method, recurring across member turnover — is generated; its carriers are taught data, ascending with the orders (constitution / seed / lineage / education-*sanskar*; JV p. 48; MVD p. 93). At the knowledge order τ carries *evidenced* understanding only: "ignorance cannot flow in tradition" (JV p. 49).

### 4.6 Orders and the regulation ladder

U is partitioned into four **orders** **[def, with data]** — material, pranic/bio, animal, knowledge (JV p. 47; MVD p. 9) — each a persistence regime differing in how a unit's method survives:

| Order | Added capability | Regulation regime | Mode |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|------|
| Material | recognising, fulfilling | result-/structural-conformance | definite |
| Pranic / bio | + selection | seed-conformance (*beej*) | definite |
| Animal | + sensation, hope | species/lineage-conformance (*vansh*) | definite |
| Knowledge | + knowing, believing, evaluation, choice | *sanskar*-conformance | achieved |

Grounding: "The material order is regulated by structural conformity… the biological order by seed conformity… the animal order by species conformity… Humans achieve definite conduct through sanskar-conformity" (JV p. 48; SB p. 236). The **regulation ladder** **[def]** is the reading order of A1 and the orders, not new axioms: saturation provisions regulation *in* the unit; regulation becomes evident as law (universal across orders); the same law appears as order-specific conformance (definite in the first three, achieved in the knowledge order); within constitutionally complete *jeevan*, mediative *atma* disciplines the faculties (§4.8); and justice is the knowledge-order closure (§4.9). Orderliness at the order level is self-regulation (*swatah-saspurt*), inherent in co-existing orders, not dispensed by O acting as governor.

### 4.7 Planes and completeness transitions

Alongside the orders, SB names four **planes** (*pad*) — physicochemical, delusional, deific, and divine — as stages in nature's progression toward completeness. The three completeness stages **[thm-orientation, with data]** are the fulfilled moments of the triad and map to plane transitions:

| Transition | Completeness | From → to |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Constitutional (*gathanpurnata*) | Physicochemical → delusional |
| **T2** | Activity (*kriyapurnata*) | Delusional → deific |
| **T3** | Conduct (*vyavaharpurnata*) | Deific → divine |

T1 is the reflexive fixed point of §2.3 — irreversible at the atomic level (SB p. 92); T2 and T3 are awakening milestones within constitutionally complete *jeevan*. Four progressions must not be collapsed: existential (*niyati-kram*, the fixed order emergence material → pranic → animal → knowledge), way of existence (*niyati-vidhi*, definiteness of each order's conduct), development (*vikas-kram*, through the physicochemical complex until T1), and awakening (*jagriti-kram*, within *jeevan* toward T2–T3).

*Vikas-kram* and *jagriti-kram* are one engine in **two regimes**, divided by the reflexive turn. Below it, a unit's completeness is **constitutional** and its deficiency is met from outside — surplus particles supplied by complementary units through κ. At and above it, constitution is already complete, so the remaining deficiency is **realizational**: the faculties are not yet aligned and understanding is not yet attained, and this deficiency is met not by acquiring anything from without but by the *jeevan*'s own activity turning inward. The drive is the same completeness-orientation (A2) carried by the same triad — *kshobh*, the "frustration of effort [that] itself is their yearning for restfulness" (MVD p. 104), is what A2 feels like once the activity has become reflexive. What changes across the turn is only the currency: from particles-and-bonding to understanding-and-alignment. So "the constitution is already complete, what develops?" has a clean answer — *realization* develops, not constitution.

### 4.8 *Jeevan* and the reflexive turn

**Jeevan** **[def, with data]** is the unit at the reflexive fixed point (§2.3): a constitutionally complete composite atom (*gathanpurna parmanu*) whose result-aspect has reached immortality, after which "both effort and motion become inexhaustible" (SB p. 61). Its constitution *is* the atom's nucleus-and-orbit structure — "The nucleus of the sentient unit… is referred to as atma. The particles in its first orbit… buddhi, the second… chitta, the third… vritti, the fourth… mun" (MVD p. 78) — operating through the projection–reflection cycle (*paravartan*–*pratyavartan*; KD 3.12): selection and taste in *mun*, analysis and deliberation in *vritti*, visualisation and contemplation in *chitta*, resolve and enlightenment in *buddhi*, authenticity and realisation in *atma* (JV p. 92; MVD p. 13). The faculty inventory is taught data; that *jeevan* is where activity turns reflexive is the kernel's structural claim. Since *atma* is mediative, the faculties and body are naturally regulated by it, and effort resisting that regulation is discord (MVD pp. 77, 277) — inward regulation parallel to the atomic nucleus. A human (and an animal) is the joint form of body and *jeevan*: *jeevan* acts through the body as instrument, is not dissolved at the body's death, and the body is provisioned to evidence understanding (JV p. 59; KD 3.16). Formally the joint form is an **action of *jeevan* on body-states, act : Jeevan × Body → Body** — asymmetric (the *jeevan* acts, the body is the instrument) and inseparable in function, not a symmetric pairing; *which* *jeevan* animates *which* body, and when, is a selection predicate the kernel hosts but does not fix — taught data, at the same tier as the T1 predicate (§10).

What differs between an animal and a human is not the *jeevan* but the **body** it operates. In an animal the sentient atom's outer orbits — *chitta*, *vritti*, *mun* — are only partially active (MVD p. 79), so the body runs a narrow circuit: it senses, selects, and acts, but knowing and believing stay severed (MVD p. 278). Animal and deluded human alike run on four *koshas*; only the awakened human, in a fully developed *medhas*-body, opens the fifth — *vigyanmaya*, discernment (MVD pp. 49–50; KD 3.16). The body is thus an **expression-gate**: *jeevan*'s capability is intrinsic and complete, but how much of it can function is fixed by the body, and full expression waits on a human body. This is why the human tier is the unfinished one — not because *jeevan* is incomplete, its constitution being already done, but because its realization is gated twice: by an understanding that can fail and by a body that must be human.

The realizational development (§4.7) has a definite mechanism. Each activity, **if it is justified** — if the reflexive cycle closes as justice, ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction (§4.9) — deposits the *jeevan* values, happiness and peace; misjudged activity does not. Happiness is therefore not a substance *jeevan* acquires but the result of justified conduct, and awakening is that result accumulated under the inward regulation of *atma*. Death and continuity are treated in the planned *Death, Continuity, and Rebirth* study; here the dyad enters only as the asymmetry that evaluation and evidence belong to *jeevan*, never to the body alone (JV p. 39).

### 4.9 Evaluation, justice, and the human goals

**Evaluation** μ **[thm]** is the reflexive comparison of value delivered against value inherent in a relationship. Because only a reflexive activity can take a coupling as its object, μ is *automatically* confined to *jeevan* — "Jeevan evaluates the values that emanate from itself… No external instrument is needed" (JV p. 139); "Values and evaluation are processes of understanding; they are not bodily or mechanical" (JV p. 39). That confinement is **derived within the reflexive model** (§2.3) — conditional on that interpretive reading, not an unconditional result. Over-, under-, and mis-evaluation (MVD p. 38) are the failure modes the reflexive tier alone admits.

**Justice** (*nyaya*) **[def]** is the closed reflexive cycle — "Recognising relationships, fulfilling values, evaluating, and achieving mutual satisfaction is justice" (MVD p. 311; JV p. 55): the core cycle ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction, an operator over value, not a member of it. This is the knowledge-order coupling's full anatomy — recognition, fulfilment, evaluation — so compound-closure κ at the human tier (an organisation, §4.4) fires exactly when this cycle closes collectively. **Trust** (*vishwas*) **[data]** is the value-name of a successful φ: "Trust is the act of fulfilling the inherent expectation of values in mutuality" (MVD p. 73). Justice is the operation; trust is the value it deposits (MVD p. 72). The knowledge order's telos **[data]** is fourfold: "The goal of jeevan is happiness, and the human goal is resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence" (JV p. 165) — what the justice cycle *evidences* when it closes, scaled to humankind as undivided society (*akhand samaj*, SB pp. 246–247). These are provisions of coexistence, not automatic outcomes: delusion, accumulation detached from right-use, and fear-based sociality remain live failures (KD ch. 1).

### 4.10 Laws and propositions

The laws and propositions the texts assert, read through the reconstruction:

- **Universal recognition-fulfilment** — every unit recognises and fulfils at the level of its order (JV p. 69); definite and inerrant for the first three orders, achieved and fallible for *jeevan*.
- **Complementarity, not struggle** — the coupling engine is mutual offering and acceptance, "there is no conflict in existence" (JV p. 157). The texts reject conflict and the pursuit of "more" (JV p. 41) as the engine; the reconstruction accordingly models complementarity rather than competition — a choice of telos, not a proof that optimization is unrepresentable.
- **Assembly by complementary need** — κ's trigger: hungry/overfull bonding (MVD p. 8), atoms into molecules into structures (JV p. 67), gravitation itself read as bonding participation (JV p. 150; KD 3.8).
- **Persistence ⇔ fulfilment** — §4.5.
- **Transmission by order** — §4.5.
- **Closure / iteration** — κ's output is a unit, so the schema re-applies; this organizes the tiered hierarchy.
- **Self-evidencing closure** — complete knowledge (*paripoorna gyan*, SB p. 116) requires the reflexive cycle to close in evidenced conduct and transmissible tradition (MVD p. 12).

Four propositions the texts also assert follow. The **tiered hierarchy** (particles → atoms → molecules → planets; cells → organisms; individual → family → undivided society, MVD p. 161) is asserted by the texts and organised by κ's iteration. **Stability at the ends, dynamism between** — O is state-complete and existence stable, while units develop definitely within that stability (MVD p. 5, p. 12). The **knowledge-order discontinuity** is a **conditional consequence of the reflexive model** (§2.3): first-order operations execute by constitution and are inerrant, while the reflexive operations of *jeevan* route through a self-model and can misfire, so human assembly is the unique unfinished tier — "This comprises only humans, who are yet to achieve orderliness" (JV p. 47). **Boundedness of extraction** — a unit's couplings with lower-order units are governed by right-use bounded by regeneration (MVD p. 264; KD 3.13); extraction beyond regeneration is an unfulfilled coupling that destabilises the containing assembly (JV p. 77).

### 4.11 Time as duration of activity

Time (*kaal*) enters the reconstruction not as a background substance or cause but as a **measure of activity**: "Duration of an activity is referred to as time" (MVD p. 34); "time is measured by the duration of an activity" (SB p. 65). Let `E` be the category of unit-executions and let `(D, ⊕, 0, ≤)` be an ordered commutative duration monoid. A duration grading `δ : E → D` assigns each execution `e` a duration and preserves sequential composition:

```text
δ(id_u) = 0                    δ(e₂ ∘ e₁) = δ(e₁) ⊕ δ(e₂)
```

The identity is the no-change execution of a **unit** `u`; it is not *satta*. O is outside `E`, since O is not a unit and performs no activity (§2.1). Consequently `δ(O)` is undefined rather than zero. *Kriya-shunya* entails *kaal-shunya* because duration has no application where there is no activity, not because O is a zero-duration event. O's presence "at all times" names its omnipresence with every temporally active unit, not temporal becoming in O (JV p. 42; [Nature of Time](../Nature-Of-Time/Nature-Of-Time.pdf)).

The equation above grades **sequential** execution only. Coupled units may act concurrently, and their elapsed duration is not obtained by summing every participant's duration; parallel composition requires interval overlap, a supremum, or another order-appropriate synchronization rule. A clock is a distinguished recurring unit-activity used as a reference. Duration is therefore activity-grounded ontologically but clock-compared numerically. The further claim that studiable causal efficacy belongs to currently executing activity, while past and future are retained as traces, anticipations, and numerical relations, is an interpretive temporal model developed in [Nature of Time](../Nature-Of-Time/Nature-Of-Time.pdf), not a theorem of the monoid grading.

Time does not select a path, supply energy, or orient activity toward completeness. It records how long an execution or transition lasts. A progression orders states toward A2; *kaal* measures the activity carrying a unit between or within those states. An atom may cycle in natural-state motion, and a deluded *jeevan* may repeat activity, accumulating duration without *vikas* or *jagriti*. The *kaal/rachna/vistar* taxonomy and numerical conventions remain taught data (MVD p. 196).

### 4.12 The natural state as an attractor: least excitation

The natural/excited distinction (§4.5) admits a **conditional Lyapunov analogy**, but not a universal least-action law. For each order `o`, let `X_o` be an admissible state space, `N_o ⊆ X_o` its natural-state set, and `Φ_o(t,x)` the evolution of a unit under specified conducive conditions. For the continuous reading take `t ∈ R≥0`; a discrete sequence may be used where no differentiable flow is warranted. Let `K_o : X_o → R≥0` measure deviation from `N_o`:

```text
K_o(x) = 0  iff  x ∈ N_o
K_o(Φ_o(t,x)) ≤ K_o(x)  during an unforced relaxation episode
```

`N_o` is an invariant natural-motion regime, not inactivity: activeness continues when `K_o = 0`. The inequality is local and conditional. Fresh perturbation, adverse environment, interaction, decomposition, or human error may increase `K_o`; the texts do not supply a global monotonicity theorem. The natural/excited axis is also independent of the compound/mixture axis. A mixture may remain naturally stable without closing a new boundary, and a compound may be excited after it forms.

Natural-state restoration must be separated from development across orders. Relaxation maps a state toward `N_o`; it does not by itself move every unit toward constitutional completeness. A guarded partial transition `T1 : N_material ⇀ X_sentient`, controlled by the still-unknown selection predicate `σ_T1`, represents the exceptional atomic passage to constitutional completeness. Most units continue in their order-relative natural regimes. T1 is an attained irreversible threshold in the texts, not an asymptote that every trajectory approaches.

Minimizing `K_o` is mathematically a form of bounded, constrained optimization. What distinguishes the darshan is not the absence of optimization mathematics but the meaning of the target: restoration to an order-relative qualitative natural set, not indefinite increase of one quantity. At the knowledge order no single scalar is adequate. Relationship fulfilment, justice, faculty harmony, conduct-evidence, and social participation form a vector or satisfaction preorder (§§4.13–4.15); improving one coordinate cannot silently compensate for violating another.

Hamilton's principle supplies a contrast, not the governing model. It selects stationary histories of an action functional under boundary constraints; the action is not generally a conserved quantity, and time symmetry or conservation depends on the particular Lagrangian and its symmetries. Lyapunov relaxation instead describes forward local stability. Neither formalism is stated in the primary texts. The textual claim is natural/excited regulation; `K_o`, `Φ_o`, and the attractor reading are mathematical analogies. Nor need development oppose thermodynamic entropy: local organization may increase while total entropy also increases. The open questions are which physical variables, if any, realize `K_o`, and whether the analogy yields testable dynamics rather than retrospective description.

### 4.13 Knowing, awakening, and the harmonies of *jeevan*

The knowledge order adds two operations before the recognition and fulfilment already present below it. Let `ν` be **knowing** — adequate conception of a unit, relation, or principle — and `β` be **believing or accepting** that conception as relevant to one's own activity. Humane conformance then has the typed sequence

```text
ν → β → ρ → φ → μ → mutual satisfaction
```

The first three orders enact `ρ → φ` definitely through constitution, seed, or species. A human can know without accepting, accept without recognising a present relationship, or recognise without fulfilling it; these broken composites are the formal locations of delusion. Projection (*paravartan*) carries resolve, desire, and thought outward into conduct; reflection (*pratyavartan*) returns result and evaluation through the faculties. Awakening is not a new substance but increasing coherence of this recurrent circuit under mediative *atma* (KD 3.12; JV pp. 70, 92).

The four *jeevan* values are order-sensitive harmonies, not quantities in one utility scale. Write `H = (h_mv, h_vc, h_cb, h_ba)` for accordance between *mun–vritti*, *vritti–chitta*, *chitta–buddhi*, and *buddhi–atma*. The texts name their evidenced states happiness, peace, contentment, and bliss respectively (JV p. 138). The tuple is read by a componentwise or constraint preorder, so a gain in one harmony does not license damage to another. T2, activity completeness, is the closure of knowing and realisation through the faculty circuit; T3, conduct completeness, is the stable projection of that understanding into relationship, work, and tradition. The conditions are textual doctrine organized by the model, not derived from A1 and A2 alone.

### 4.14 Karma, result, and conduct-evidence

At the knowledge order the universal effort–motion–result triad acquires intentional articulation. A compact karma cycle is

```text
desire → thought → selection → bodily/work action → result → evaluation
   ↑                                                        │
   └──────── revised understanding and sanskar ─────────────┘
```

This is not a deterministic pipeline: capacity, ability, receptivity, bodily conditions, other units, and environment mediate the transition from intention to result. Evaluation compares the realised result and value against the recognised relationship; it may confirm understanding or expose over-, under-, or mis-evaluation. Repeated cycles alter *sanskar*, which conditions later projection without making human conduct constitutionally fixed.

Madhyasth Darshan's evidence condition closes the epistemic and practical sides. A conception is not complete merely because it is internally consistent: understanding must become reproducible in experiment or work, relationship and behaviour, and realisation, and must remain transmissible without contradiction (MVD pp. 12, 336; JV p. 49). Formally, authenticity is a coherence condition across `ν`, `β`, `ρ`, `φ`, `μ`, and `τ`; success in one domain cannot substitute for failure in another. This is why conduct-evidence belongs inside the model rather than as an external test appended after it.

### 4.15 Human goals and the dimensions of universal order

The individual cycle scales into family and social participation through a vector of non-substitutable domains. Let

```text
S = (education–sanskar, justice–security, production–work,
     exchange–reserve, health–restraint)
```

These five dimensions are the taught institutional content of universal order (JV p. 141). The formal claim is only that social closure requires compatible fulfilment across all five: education carries understanding, justice secures relationships, production provisions prosperity, exchange and reserve coordinate right-use and continuity, and health maintains the body through which *jeevan* evidences conduct. A society cannot compensate for failed justice by producing more goods, or for failed education by stronger enforcement; `S` is therefore assessed by constraints or a partial order, not one maximand.

The four human goals name outcomes at connected scales: resolution in the person, prosperity in the family, fearlessness in society, and coexistence in universal order (JV pp. 61, 165). They do not arise automatically from assembling institutions. They are evidenced when the knowledge-order cycle (§4.13), karma and authenticity (§4.14), and the five dimensions close together and transmit through humane tradition. This supplies the substantive content that the conduct-compound analogy of §4.4 previously left abstract.

### 4.16 Causation as conditioned activity and result

The reconstruction does not assign efficient causation to O. O provisions the invariants of A1 while remaining actionless; particular changes belong to units in mutuality. A local causal episode is therefore a typed execution in which a unit's effort and motion, its coupling partners, and its environment jointly condition a result. Since effort, motion, and result are inseparable aspects rather than three detachable events (MVD p. 105), the model does not reduce causation to a single preceding impact:

```text
(unit state, C_o, environment, capacity) ──activity──▶ result state
```

The result may become a condition for a later execution, giving causal composition without creation from nothing. Duration `δ` grades the execution but does not cause it; A2 orients admissible development but does not determine every local result. In the first three orders the transition is constitutionally definite under its conditions. In the knowledge order intention, body, other persons, evaluation, and *sanskar* make the same structure reflexive and fallible (§4.14). This separates ontological provision, local efficient activity, temporal measure, and teleological orientation instead of collapsing them into one notion of cause.

## 6. The formal spine

The template invites precise notation. Instead of a system of many categories, one dual-facing spine carries the load, with richer constructions kept as optional annotations.

### 6.1 Two readings: carrier-behaviour and generation

**Downward — carrier-coalgebra.** A unit is a carrier `x` together with a behaviour map `x → F(x)` unfolding its activity (the triad and its couplings). The carrier is *not* quotiented by bisimulation and the theory does *not* pass to the final coalgebra — so the carrier exceeds its behaviour. This is the co-structuralist dual of the Yoneda principle, and it is what keeps a unit **substantial** and *jeevan* a real entity rather than a functional role, while letting O stay actionless: A1 grounds *that* the behaviour exists and is lawful, contributing no term to the unfolding.

**Upward — generation as an initial algebra.** As a **formal reconstruction**, the units of nature can be **encoded** as the algebra generated by κ from the primitive units — an initial-algebra / W-type presentation. This is the paper's encoding, not machinery the texts contain; within it, definitions over units (value, transmission, evaluation) become **definitions by recursion** on how a unit was built, and mixture is typed *off* the inductive sort, so "composition is not development" reads as a typing fact.

### 6.2 The two load-bearing constructs

Two constructs carry most of the encoding's weight. The **closure operator** κ (§6.1) organizes the hierarchy and iterates by construction. The **society gluing colimit** encodes undivided society: families and communities with shared members glue along overlaps, and the gluing is coherent exactly when local value-structures agree on what they share. This is κ at the social tier, and it is the one place the encoding adds a compatibility **observation** rather than merely restating the darshan: the gluing is coherent only if overlapping assignments actually agree — a descent-style condition whose textual bridge the applied study [How Undivided Society Is Established](../How-Undivided-Society-Is-Established/How-Undivided-Society-Is-Established.pdf) §4.6 supplies.

The compatibility condition this colimit turns on is not a formal hope — the darshan **asserts** it. *Jeevan* "exists in all humans in the same form, with identical strengths and powers", and "since the goal of *jeevan* is happiness, all humans aspire for happiness" (JV p. 40); "All human endeavours and experimentations are aimed at achieving satisfaction or happiness" (MVD p. 64); "Human kind is one… Human dharma is one" (MVD p. 13). The agreement is conditioned on awakening — "We humans are united in the right and divided in the wrong" (JV p. 17) — so the diagram is compatible and the colimit glues into an undivided whole exactly in the awakened case, while delusion is where local value-structures disagree and the gluing degenerates (P3). Undivided society is therefore not an incidental output but the **telos the whole structure is driven toward**: the four human goals are the shared value-content every *jeevan* aspires to, and the society colimit closes coherently precisely when that content is recognised across members. The *content* of those shared values remains taught data; that the aspiration is one, and the agreement real in the right, is what the texts assert — the substantive premise the compatibility condition names.

### 6.3 Optional annotations

Beyond the spine, several richer categorical models sharpen specific clauses without carrying the theory, and are offered as annotations rather than foundations: reduction to physics as a non-faithful forgetful functor (value-distinct acts collapse); delusion as a retract mistaken for an isomorphism (comfort a genuine part of fulfilment, not the whole, MVD Ch. 4); right-use as a natural transformation uniform across domains; graded fulfilment as enrichment over a satisfaction preorder, fibred over capacity; the activity triad as a traced joint form and κ-bookkeeping as a Petri net; the faculty cycle as a projection–reflection sketch (KD 3.12); and the actualisation of sentience at T1 as a guarded partial morphism — the *Saturation-Reflector* reading whose selection predicate is taught data, developed in [The Ontology of Coexistence](../The-Ontology-of-Coexistence/The-Ontology-of-Coexistence.pdf) §§1.2, 1.6.1 (open problems in §6.2.1). Time is a grading of **unit-executions** over `(D, ⊕, 0)`, with `δ(id_u) = 0` and no value assigned to O (§4.11). Natural/excited regulation is an order-indexed Lyapunov family under specified conditions, kept distinct from the guarded T1 transition (§4.12). Each construction is conditional, and none is a theorem about reality.

## 8. What the structure is not

The most natural modern glosses quietly replace the structure with something else. It is **not driven by indefinite increase**: the darshan does not make unbounded "more" the telos — conduct in the first three orders is definite (JV p. 113), object-usefulness is constant (JV p. 123), and "more" is the diagnosed pathology (JV p. 41). A terminal state can, of course, be *represented* by minimising distance to it; the claim is not that optimisation is unrepresentable but that **indefinite quantitative increase is not the goal**, so this reconstruction encodes completeness as a qualitative terminal condition rather than an unbounded scalar objective. It is **not struggle or selection**: the assembly engine is complementarity, explicitly opposed to conflict and survival-of-the-fittest (JV p. 157); decomposition is the *loss* of structure when relations go unfulfilled, not its designer. It is **not bottom-up emergence alone**: capacities are provisioned in existence and manifest when composition reaches the right tier (JV p. 59), and O energises every tier at once — a purely emergentist reading drops O and turns energisation into magic. And it is **not value-as-epiphenomenon**: value is the content of fulfilment at every tier (SB p. 50), and the human innovation is a second-order operation on it, located in *jeevan*, "not bodily or mechanical" (JV p. 39).

## 9. Relation to other frameworks, and uses

Stated this generally, the structure invites comparison — useful chiefly for locating what is distinctive.

| Framework | Shared structure | Where it differs |
|---|---|---|
| **Holarchy** (Koestler) | every unit is whole and part | no medium O, no value in relations, no transmission |
| **Autopoiesis** (Maturana & Varela) | persistence by continuous self-production | living systems only; the structure runs from atoms to societies and grounds persistence in fulfilment |
| **Multilevel selection** | nested units persisting or failing as wholes | selectionist engines are competitive and stochastic; here the engine is complementarity and definite |
| **Thermodynamic self-organisation** | larger structures from flows between non-equilibrium units | dissipative structures need free-energy throughput; here units are energised constitutively by saturation |
| **Category-theoretic composition** | relationships as morphisms; assemblies as colimits; tiers as a partial order | notation for κ and iteration, but silent on which compositions occur; complementarity is the selection rule the notation lacks |

Two commitments are genuinely distinctive: the medium O as a constitutive, energising, regulating presence at every tier at once — a precondition *of* physics, not an aether within it; and value as the universal content of relations, with evaluation appearing only at the top tier. Frameworks that drop the first must find energy and regulation inside the units; frameworks that drop the second must reconstruct value as preference or fitness.

The uses follow the forward and reverse readings of §1. For **human organisations**, a deliberate assembly persists only if its relationships are recognised and their values actually fulfilled — not merely incentivised — and it transmits its understanding to incoming members; an organisation that fulfils values but does not transmit decays on turnover, one that transmits rules without fulfilment holds only as long as enforcement does ([How To Form Self-Sustaining Organizations](../How-To-Form-Self-Sustaining-Organizations/How-To-Form-Self-Sustaining-Organizations.pdf); [How Undivided Society Is Established](../How-Undivided-Society-Is-Established/How-Undivided-Society-Is-Established.pdf)). For **ecology and economics**, boundedness of extraction turns sustainability from an ethical add-on into a stability condition. For **AI and value alignment**, graded fulfilment is an argument for multi-level objectives over one scalar reward — optimising a single number maximises the wrong thing, which is the darshan's own diagnosis of "more". And for the **study of the darshan itself**, the kernel is a checklist for reading any passage: identify the unit, the tier, the couplings, the expectation profile, the value, the conformance regime, and the transmission carrier, and much apparent repetition resolves into the same move at different tiers.

## 10. Limitations and open problems

**Formalisation is interpretive.** The symbols (O, U, A1, A2, `C_o`, κ, ν, β, ρ, φ, μ, τ, δ, `X_o`, `N_o`, `Φ_o`, `K_o`, `σ_T1`, `H`, `S`) do not appear in the texts; they are this paper's compression, warranted by the citations. Every reconstruction is this paper's construction.

**The heavy posits, named.** The simplification concentrates the theory's contestable weight onto A2's teleology, the mediative regulation asserted in A1, and two selection predicates the kernel hosts but does not derive: which atomic configuration reaches constitutional completeness (T1), and which *jeevan* animates which body (the *act* pairing, §4.8). A physicist critic will press exactly there. The reflexive turn, duration grading, and Lyapunov family organize textual claims but do not establish their physical realization.

**The residual premise.** Universal coupling rests on the **no-isolation premise** (JV p. 43; §2.4) — a textual assertion the reconstruction adopts rather than derives; that units couple at all does not follow from A1 alone. This is the one place the kernel leans on a premise beyond its axioms.

**Substantiality and Yoneda.** Category theory is structuralist by default, so a faithful categorical reading tends to re-describe *jeevan* as a functional role. The carrier-coalgebra reading (§6.1), with carrier-primacy stipulated (§2.1) and the final coalgebra refused, holds the anti-structuralist line by commitment rather than leaving it open — but the tension with structuralist reduction is a boundary of the tools.

**Translation risk.** Quotations from MVD, SB, and JV follow the published English translations; several key terms (*vyavastha* as "orderliness", *samprikt* as "saturated") carry translators' choices. Citations to *Manav Karm Darshan* (KD) are to a machine-assisted **working** translation, not a published one; they are given as corroboration only where MVD/SB/JV already carry the claim, and KD-only material should be verified against the Hindi page images before release. The corpus beyond these four — *Vyavaharatmak Janvad* and *Anubhavatmak Adhyatmvad* — is not yet cited.

**No empirical contact.** Nothing here yields a measurement or prediction of *jeevan*, coexistence, or constitutional completeness. Elegant diagrams can create an illusion of proof; the conditional framing and this list are the safeguard.

**Open problems.** Formal countermodels establishing the independence of A1 and A2; discharging or confirming no-isolation; identifying the order-relative relations `C_o`; a mechanised recovery audit; deriving the three evaluation failures from the reflexive cycle; testing the society-gluing compatibility condition; specifying concurrency in the duration model; finding physical variables and domains for any `K_o`; relating local organization to thermodynamics without positing an anti-entropic law; formalizing the non-scalar preorders `H` and `S`; and identifying the selection predicates for T1 and *jeevan*–body pairing. Comparison with stationary action and gradient flows remains work for the planned *Restfulness and Least Action* study.

## 11. Conclusion

Read from first principles, Madhyasth Darshan's ground is coexistence itself: one actionless medium and countless real units, bound by saturation. A1 provisions activeness, recognition, and inherent regulation; A2 supplies order-relative orientation toward completeness; typed complementarity and closure organize material compounds, biological composition, and the declared analogy of humane social order. Time grades the duration of unit-activity without causing it. Natural/excited restoration admits a conditional stability model, kept separate from the selected transitions of development. At constitutional completeness the interpretive reflexive model opens the knowledge order, where knowing, believing, recognising, fulfilling, evaluating, and transmitting must cohere. Karma makes that understanding answerable to result and conduct; faculty harmonies give non-scalar content to happiness, peace, contentment, and bliss; the five social dimensions carry the same requirement into universal order. The reconstruction is strongest when every claim keeps its textual provenance, within-model role, and epistemic standing visible — especially where a mathematical analogy organizes an open metaphysical posit without establishing it.
