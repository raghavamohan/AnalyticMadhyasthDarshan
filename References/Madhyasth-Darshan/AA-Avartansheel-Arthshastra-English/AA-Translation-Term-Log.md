# AA translation term log, chapters 3–9

This log records, chapter by chapter, the terminology choices made while chapters 3–9
and the trailing pages were first translated (13 October 2026) that were not already
settled in [`AA-Glossary-Additions.md`](AA-Glossary-Additions.md). Each row gives the
authority relied on (MD-Mapping, Rakesh Gupta's MVD/SB/JV, KD, or the translator's own
choice) and the reason. Nothing here is a decision: choices that need the author's
approval are collected in the glossary's
"Proposed in translation batches 1-9" table, and a
choice becomes settled only when the glossary records it as `approved`.

**Harmonised after merging.** Where chapters differed, the manuscript and the rows below
follow one rendering: अनुसंधान = exploration (MD; also in chapter 2), मानव कुल =
humankind, मौलिकता = essentiality (MD), स्वत्व = identity (MVD) in the triad of identity,
freedom and authority ("own" where it means what is one's own, p. 73), कृत, कारित,
अनुमोदित = done, caused and intended (the author's KD decision), स्वतंत्रता =
independence (MVD; the author's decision), अस्तित्व दर्शन ज्ञान = knowledge of coexistence
(the author's decision), bare अस्तित्व दर्शन = the holistic view of existence (the
author's decision, following KD), and अर्थव्यवस्था = economy (with
key term 1, आवर्तनशील अर्थव्यवस्था = cyclical economy, also corrected in chapter 2).
