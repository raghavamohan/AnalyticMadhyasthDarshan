# AA passages with unclear structure

This index lists the passages of the AA working translation whose **Hindi structure is
unclear**: the subject or referent is uncertain, the clauses of a long sentence can be
related in more than one way, a word seems to be missing or misprinted, or the sentence has
no main verb. The English in the manuscript gives the translator's best reading. Each
passage awaits the author's re-reading and consultation before it is settled.

**Finding a passage.** Each entry carries an ID. In
[`AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-English.md`](AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-English.md) the
passage is preceded by an invisible marker, `<!-- unclear U-nnn -->`. In the side-by-side
[`AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-Hindi-English.pdf`](AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-Hindi-English.pdf)
the same ID appears as a yellow tag in the English column, beside the Hindi page.

**Settling a passage.** Replace the English in the manuscript with the agreed wording,
delete its marker, move the entry to [Resolved](#resolved) with the date and the reviewer,
log the change in the
[review ledger](AA-Source-Image-Review-Ledger.md#correction-log), and rebuild the review
PDF (`python Scripts/_aa_build_hindi_english_pdf.py`). IDs are never reused.

### U-001

- **Page:** PDF 11 / p. 1 (chapter 1, second paragraph)
- **Hindi (opening):** इसके पहले अभी तक व्यक्तिवादी समुदायों के रूप में गुज़री बीती मानव परंपरा में तत्कालीन विधियों से मानव को ही स्वीकृत अर्थ का स्वरूप…
- **English (as drafted):** "Before this, in the human tradition that has passed until now in the form of individualistic communities, the methods of those times gave a general clarification of the form of resources as accepted by humans, … Considering it appropriate, we have presented that too."
- **Issue:** The sentence has one verb, प्रस्तुत किए हैं (we have presented), and "सामान्य स्पष्टीकरण को उचित समझकर" (considering the general clarification appropriate). It is unclear whose clarification it is: one made by "the methods of those times" and now reproduced, or the author's own general clarification of how those times worked. The draft takes the first reading.

### U-002

- **Page:** PDF 13 / p. 3
- **Hindi (opening):** इस अवधि तक यह आक्रमण और प्रतिरोध का कार्यक्रम दो विधाओं में गुजरते रहे। पहली विधा क्रूर जन्तुओं के आक्रमण का प्रतिरोध और दूसरी भयभीत मानवों के ऊपर किए गये आक्रमण और उसका प्रतिरोध के इस अवधि तक औजारों को…
- **English (as drafted):** "Up to this period, this programme of attack and resistance passed through two forms. The first was resistance to the attacks of fierce beasts, and the second was the attacks made upon frightened humans and the resistance to them. Up to this period, tools … went on being exchanged voluntarily between people. Second, one person kept seizing another's objects…"
- **Issue:** The Hindi runs the second form of attack and resistance straight into the voluntary exchange of objects ("…उसका प्रतिरोध के इस अवधि तक औजारों को… आदान-प्रदान होते रहा"), with no sentence break and a case ending (के) that does not fit. A following "दूसरा" (second: seizure of objects) has no matching "first". The draft supposes a missing break, with voluntary exchange as the first way of obtaining objects and seizure as the second.

### U-003

- **Page:** PDF 15 / p. 5
- **Hindi (opening):** यहाँ उल्लेखनीय बात यही है कि विगत में सोचा गया। दोनों प्रकार के अर्थशास्त्र यह भी मानते आये हैं… यही बिन्दु है इस बिन्दु से इंगित विधि से होने वाली पीड़ा…
- **English (as drafted):** "The noteworthy point here is what was thought in the past: both kinds of economics have also gone on believing that for one unit to profit, another unit suffers loss. … This is the point. As a result of the suffering that arises from the method indicated…"
- **Issue:** "यहाँ उल्लेखनीय बात यही है कि विगत में सोचा गया" is complete only as "the noteworthy point is that it was thought in the past", which says nothing. Words may be missing. Near the end, "यही बिन्दु है इस बिन्दु से इंगित…" has no punctuation, so it can be read as "this is the point; by the method indicated by this point…" or "this very point is the one by whose indicated method…".

### U-004
