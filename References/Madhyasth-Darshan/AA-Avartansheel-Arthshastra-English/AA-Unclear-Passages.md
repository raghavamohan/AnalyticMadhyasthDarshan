# AA passages with unclear structure

This index lists the passages of the AA working translation whose **Hindi structure is
unclear**: the subject or referent is uncertain, the clauses of a long sentence can be
related in more than one way, a word seems to be missing or misprinted, or the sentence has
no main verb. The English in the manuscript gives the translator's best reading. Each
passage awaits the author's re-reading and consultation before it is settled.

**Finding a passage.** Each entry carries an ID. In
[`AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-English.md`](AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-English.md) the
passage is preceded by an invisible marker, `<!-- unclear U-nnn -->`. In the side-by-side
[`AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-Hindi-English.pdf`](AA-Avartansheel-Arthshastra-Hindi-English.pdf)
the same ID appears as a yellow tag in the English column, beside the Hindi page.

**Settling a passage.** Replace the English in the manuscript with the agreed wording,
delete its marker, move the entry to [Resolved](#resolved) with the date and the reviewer,
log the change in the
[review ledger](AA-Source-Image-Review-Ledger.md#correction-log), and rebuild the review
PDF (`python Scripts/_aa_build_hindi_english_pdf.py`). IDs are never reused.

### U-001

- **Page:** PDF 11 / p. 1 (chapter 1, second paragraph)
- **Hindi (opening):** इसके पहले अभी तक व्यक्तिवादी समुदायों के रूप में गुज़री बीती मानव परंपरा में तत्कालीन विधियों से मानव को ही स्वीकृत अर्थ का स्वरूप…
- **English (as drafted):** "Before this, in the human tradition that has passed until now in the form of individualistic communities, the methods of those times gave a general clarification of the form of resources as accepted by humans, … Considering it appropriate, we have presented that too."
- **Issue:** The sentence has one verb, प्रस्तुत किए हैं (we have presented), and "सामान्य स्पष्टीकरण को उचित समझकर" (considering the general clarification appropriate). It is unclear whose clarification it is: one made by "the methods of those times" and now reproduced, or the author's own general clarification of how those times worked. The draft takes the first reading.

### U-002

- **Page:** PDF 13 / p. 3
- **Hindi (opening):** इस अवधि तक यह आक्रमण और प्रतिरोध का कार्यक्रम दो विधाओं में गुजरते रहे। पहली विधा क्रूर जन्तुओं के आक्रमण का प्रतिरोध और दूसरी भयभीत मानवों के ऊपर किए गये आक्रमण और उसका प्रतिरोध के इस अवधि तक औजारों को…
- **English (as drafted):** "Up to this period, this programme of attack and resistance passed through two forms. The first was resistance to the attacks of fierce beasts, and the second was the attacks made upon frightened humans and the resistance to them. Up to this period, tools … went on being exchanged voluntarily between people. Second, one person kept seizing another's objects…"
- **Issue:** The Hindi runs the second form of attack and resistance straight into the voluntary exchange of objects ("…उसका प्रतिरोध के इस अवधि तक औजारों को… आदान-प्रदान होते रहा"), with no sentence break and a case ending (के) that does not fit. A following "दूसरा" (second: seizure of objects) has no matching "first". The draft supposes a missing break, with voluntary exchange as the first way of obtaining objects and seizure as the second.

### U-003

- **Page:** PDF 15 / p. 5
- **Hindi (opening):** यहाँ उल्लेखनीय बात यही है कि विगत में सोचा गया। दोनों प्रकार के अर्थशास्त्र यह भी मानते आये हैं… यही बिन्दु है इस बिन्दु से इंगित विधि से होने वाली पीड़ा…
- **English (as drafted):** "The noteworthy point here is what was thought in the past: both kinds of economics have also gone on believing that for one unit to profit, another unit suffers loss. … This is the point. As a result of the suffering that arises from the method indicated…"
- **Issue:** "यहाँ उल्लेखनीय बात यही है कि विगत में सोचा गया" is complete only as "the noteworthy point is that it was thought in the past", which says nothing. Words may be missing. Near the end, "यही बिन्दु है इस बिन्दु से इंगित…" has no punctuation, so it can be read as "this is the point; by the method indicated by this point…" or "this very point is the one by whose indicated method…".

### U-004

- **Hindi (opening):** इसी समस्या के समाधान रूप में आर्वतनशील अर्थशास्त्र को प्रस्तावित करने और प्रणेत प्रेरणा स्त्रोत स्थापित करने का अवसर प्राप्त हुआ।

### U-005

- **Page:** PDF 17 / p. 7
- **Hindi (opening):** इसी के साथ अर्थात् व्यापार संघ के साथ बैर विहिन परिवार, संघ या राष्ट्र या समुदाय ही नहीं बन पाया फलस्वरूप…
- **English (as drafted):** "Along with this, that is, with the trade association, families, associations, nations or communities free of enmity could not even come into being…"
- **Issue:** "व्यापार संघ के साथ" can mean "alongside the trade association" (where trade associations exist, enmity-free families etc. could not form) or "with the trade association" (trade associations could not become enmity-free families, associations, nations or communities).

### U-006

- **Page:** PDF 17 / p. 7
- **Hindi (opening):** उत्पादित वस्तुओं में गुणवत्ता और कला मूल्यों में (उत्पादित वस्तु का आकार-प्रकार, सजावट और सुरक्षा विधि) श्रेष्ठता जिससे मानव में जो अच्छा लगने की एक सहज क्रिया है उसके अनुसार स्वीकृत हो जाए।

- **Issue:** The Hindi has no main verb; "श्रेष्ठता जिससे … स्वीकृत हो जाए" (excellence by which [the object] may be accepted) reads as a fragment, possibly a definition or a stated aim. The draft supplies "have".

### U-007

- **Page:** PDF 17 / p. 7
- **Hindi (opening):** जहाँ तक अलंकार वस्तु होते है और आहार वस्तुएं आदि काल से अभी तक वैसे ही दिखाई पड़ती है।
- **English (as drafted):** "As for adornment objects and food objects, they appear the same from the earliest times until now."
- **Issue:** The sentence says adornment objects have stayed the same, but two sentences later "अलंकार और आवास संबंधी परिवर्तनों को अपनाता आया है" says humans have kept adopting changes in adornment and shelter. "जहाँ तक अलंकार वस्तु होते है" may instead mean "as far as adornment objects go [they change]", with only food objects staying the same, or a word may be missing.

### U-008

- **Page:** PDF 19 / p. 9
- **Hindi (opening):** सर्वशुभ के संदर्भ में जितने भी प्रकार से शोध, अनुसंधान, अवतरण घटनाक्रम में, परिस्थिति क्रम में, पीढ़ी से पीढ़ी के रूप में दिखने वाली मानव परंपरा में पाये जाने वाले आंकलन…
- **English (as drafted):** "In relation to universal wellbeing, all the assessments found in the human tradition — through every kind of research and investigation, in the sequence of events of its appearance, in the sequence of circumstances, as it is seen from generation to generation — … are all found to be assessed as arising from some event or changed event."
- **Issue:** The phrases before आंकलन can attach to the assessments (how they were made) or to the human tradition (how it appears). "अवतरण घटनाक्रम" may be the sequence of events of humankind's appearance or of an event's occurrence. The main clause, "assessments … are assessed as arising from some event", is circular.

### U-009

- **Page:** PDF 20 / p. 10
- **Hindi (opening):** इसका समीक्षा मूल्यांकन यहीं है अभी तक साक्षरता, विज्ञान, उपदेश, आस्था और आंकलनात्मक ज्ञान समेत परस्परता में कुण्ठा, निराशा, भय से होता हुआ, प्रलोभन से ग्रसित होता रहा।
- **English (as drafted):** "Its review and evaluation is just this: until now, along with literacy, science, sermons, faith and assessment-based knowledge, mutual relations have passed through frustration, despair and fear and have remained gripped by temptation."
- **Issue:** The verbs "होता हुआ … ग्रसित होता रहा" have no stated subject. The draft takes परस्परता (mutuality, mutual relations); the subject could instead be "the human tradition" from the previous sentence. "समेत" (along with) may also mean "in spite of".

### U-010

- **Page:** PDF 21 / p. 11 (item 3, after the sentence revised on 13 October 2026)
- **Hindi (opening):** इन दोनों के विचार और कार्य यात्रा के फलन रूप में बीसवीं शताब्दी की अंतिम दशक में भी देख पा रहे हैं जो शिक्षा संबंधी समीक्षा (पूर्व में किया गया है) सहित लाभोन्माद, कामोन्माद, और भोगोन्माद विधियों से ग्रसित, त्रस्त मानस…
- **English (as drafted):** "As the outcome of the thought and the journey of work of these two, we can see even in the last decade of the twentieth century that the human mind — gripped by the methods of profit-obsession, sex-obsession and indulgence-obsession, together with the review of education (made earlier), and harassed — being compelled toward mysterious faiths and tormented by inequality and disparities, is found to be frustrated by the disparities of more and less…"
- **Issue:** One sentence with a chain of participles. It is unclear whether "शिक्षा संबंधी समीक्षा … सहित" means "as the review of education (made earlier) showed" or "together with education", and whether "त्रस्त मानस" is the subject of all that follows. The final "क्योंकि" clause (for the learned, capable, rich and strong were unable…) may explain either the frustration or the disparities.

### U-011

- **Page:** PDF 25 / p. 15 (the definition of cyclicality)
- **Hindi (opening):** आवर्तनशीलता का तात्पर्य जिस किसी बिन्दु से समृद्धि के अर्थ में प्राकृतिक ऐश्वर्य नैसर्गिकता सहज पूरकता क्रम में लक्ष्यपूर्ति तक और पुनः आरंभिक बिन्दु तक सूत्रित होने से है।
- **English (as drafted):** "Cyclicality means being linked (*sutrit*), from any point whatever, in the sense of prosperity, in the course of the complementarity natural to natural abundance and naturalness (*naisargikta*), up to the fulfilment of the goal and back again to the starting point."
- **Issue:** "प्राकृतिक ऐश्वर्य नैसर्गिकता सहज पूरकता क्रम" is an unpunctuated compound. It can be read as "the course of complementarity natural to natural abundance and its surroundings", or "natural abundance, in the course of complementarity natural to the surroundings", or as a list (natural abundance, naturalness, natural complementarity). It is also unclear what is linked (*sutrit*): resources, the process or the points. This is the book's key definition.

### U-012

- **Page:** PDF 25 / p. 15
- **Hindi (opening):** कल्पनाशीलता का उपयोग विविध विधि से होता हुआ कर्म स्वतंत्रता सहज कार्य-विन्यास गति में सर्वेक्षित होना पाया जाता है।
- **English (as drafted):** "Imaginativeness, being used in various ways, is found to be surveyed in the motion of work and design (*vinyas*) natural to freedom of action."
- **Issue:** It is unclear what "सर्वेक्षित होना" (being surveyed) means here, and whether imaginativeness is surveyed (examined) or is to be seen (observed) in the activity of freedom of action. "कार्य-विन्यास गति" may be "the motion of work and its design" or "the pattern of activity".

### U-013

- **Page:** PDF 26 / p. 16
- **Hindi (opening):** प्रत्येक मानव में ये तीनों प्रकार के बंधन सहित कार्य करता हुआ कल्पनाशीलताओं, उसके क्रियान्वयन क्रम में कर्म स्वतंत्रता का नियोजन दृष्टव्य है।

### U-014

- **Page:** PDF 27 / p. 17
- **Hindi (opening):** इस प्रकार जीवन ही दृष्टा पद में होना, जीवन और शरीर का संयुक्त रूप में मानव परंपरा का होना जिसका प्रयोजन सर्वशुभ होना, सर्वशुभ घटित होने के लिए मानव परंपरा ही जागृत होना एक अनिवार्य स्थिति है।
- **English (as drafted):** "In this way — jeevan alone being in the seer status, and the human tradition being the joint form of jeevan and body, whose purpose is universal wellbeing — for universal wellbeing to come about, the awakening of the human tradition itself is an inevitable state."
- **Issue:** It is a list of verbal nouns (होना… होना… होना), and the verb "एक अनिवार्य स्थिति है" can govern all of them ("all these are an inevitable state") or only the last (the draft's reading). "जिसका" may refer to the human tradition or to jeevan.

### U-015

- **Page:** PDF 27 / p. 17
- **Hindi (opening):** जागृत मानव परंपरा में अनुभव का स्वरूप क्रिया उसका प्रमाण जीवन सहज वैभव के रूप में जानना, मानना, पहचानना, निर्वाह करने के क्रम में समाधान, समृद्धि, अभय, सहअस्तित्व रूपी सौभाग्य सम्पन्न होना पाया जाता है।
- **English (as drafted):** "In the awakened human tradition, the form of realisation, the activity, and its evidence are found, in the course of knowing, believing, recognising and fulfilling as the grandeur natural to jeevan, to be endowed with the good fortune that is resolution, prosperity, fearlessness and coexistence."
- **Issue:** "अनुभव का स्वरूप क्रिया उसका प्रमाण" can be three items (form, activity, evidence) or a chain ("the form of realisation, the activity [of evidencing] it, its evidence"). It is unclear what is "endowed with good fortune": these, or the awakened tradition.

### U-016

- **Page:** PDF 29 / p. 19
- **Hindi (opening):** बोध और संकल्प की महिमा ही है अस्तित्व सहज, वस्तु सहज, स्थिति सहज, गति सहज, विकास सहज, जागृति सहज सत्यता बोध और निर्वाह करने की निष्ठा, संकल्प के रूप में होता ही है।

The draft makes "dedication" govern both.

### U-017

### U-018

- **Page:** PDF 30 / p. 20
- **Hindi (opening):** वह चयन, आस्वादन, विश्लेषण, तुलन और चित्रण, इन साढ़े चार क्रियाओं में अर्थात् भ्रमित कल्पनाशीलता की सीमा में बारंबार परिशीलन करने योग्य जागृति सहज पाँचों क्रियाओं में से न्याय परिशीलन क्रम में विश्वास साम्य मूल्य के रूप में होना देखा गया है।
- **English (as drafted):** "In the four and a half activities — selection, tasting, analysis, deliberation and visualisation — that is, within the limit of deluded imaginativeness, and, among the five activities natural to awakening that are to be reviewed again and again, in the course of reviewing justice, trust has been seen to exist as a uniform value (*samya mulya*)."
- **Issue:** The opening "वह" (that / it) has no clear antecedent: imagination, or the human being. It is unclear whether the "five activities natural to awakening" are contrasted with the four and a half, or are what is reviewed within them. (The count of five names as "four and a half" is the darshan's own: JV lists them as "selection, taste, analysis, visualisation, and half of deliberation".)

### U-019

- **Page:** PDF 32 / p. 22
- **Hindi (opening):** सदुपयोग का तात्पर्य शरीर पुष्टि (आहार पूर्वक) शरीर सुरक्षा (आवास, अलंकार पूर्वक) और जीवन जागृति की सार्थकता या चरितार्थता के लिए नियोजन कार्य का नाम है उदारता।

### U-020

- **Page:** PDF 33 / p. 23
- **Hindi (opening):** इस प्रकार परिणाम का अमरत्व, श्रम का विश्राम और गति का गंतव्य ही सहअस्तित्व में परमाणु सहज विकास होने के अनन्तर जीवन लक्ष्य होना, सार्थक होने का तथ्य अध्ययनगम्य है।
- **English (as drafted):** "In this way, the fact that the immortality of result, the restfulness of labour and the destination of motion are the goal of jeevan after the development natural to the atom in coexistence, and are its being meaningful, is studiable."
- **Issue:** "जीवन लक्ष्य होना, सार्थक होने का तथ्य" may be "the fact of [these] being jeevan's goal and being fulfilled", or "the fact of jeevan's goal being [these], and being meaningful". "सार्थक होने" has no clear subject.

### Revised by the author, 13 October 2026

- **PDF 21 / p. 11, item 3 (opening).** "As the fundamental source of awareness, the formation and functioning of the body, the joint system of jeevan, its goal and its study have not become studiable through either of the two kinds of worldview, logic, systematic inquiry and postulation that have gone before." Now reads: "The fact that the fundamental source of awareness is the formation and functioning of the body and jeevan as a joint system, the goals and the method of study of awareness, have not become studiable through either of the previous two worldviews, logic, hermeneutics or postulations."
- **PDF 22 / p. 12, the definitions of science-wisdom, knowledge, wisdom and analysis.** Now reads: "Science-wisdom are meant to bring about consistency between all analyses and knowledge. Knowledge means the knowledge of jeevan, knowledge of coexistence, and knowledge of humane conduct. Wisdom means all the analyses that bring about clarity of purpose, in a form that is acceptable to all. Analyses means the procedure that provides expansive logic that evaluates objects and events that are known by activities of knowing, believing and recognising."
