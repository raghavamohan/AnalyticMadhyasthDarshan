# AA translation glossary additions

This file records terminology decisions specific to the working English translation
of *Avartansheel Arthshastra*. Translation has not begun, so it holds no decisions
yet. Its key-term list sets out the vocabulary the terminology pilot must settle,
with the authority that already exists for each term.

## Policy

- Begin with `../MD-Mapping.xlsx` and the published MVD, SB, and JV translations.
- Consult KD for sentence-level and terminological precedent only when the Hindi
  expression has the same contextual sense.
- Do not promote a KD-specific exception into AA without independent source-image
  review.
- Record alternatives and evidence before settling a departure from established
  terminology.
- Use status `proposed`, `approved`, `deferred`, or `rejected`. Only `approved`
  choices govern the translation body.

## Key terms for review

This list was compiled on 6 October 2026 for review with Rakesh Gupta, translator of
MVD, SB and JV, before the terminology pilot. None of it is a decision: a choice
becomes settled only when it is entered in the Decisions table above with status
`approved`.

**How it was compiled.** OCR search across all 149 body pages found the candidates
and their frequency. Chapter 3's definitions (printed pp. 25-26) were read in full
from the page images. Every Hindi form below was confirmed in the page image at the
page cited. Counts are approximate whole-word OCR counts over the body pages.
"Existing authority" cites `../MD-Mapping.xlsx` and Rakesh Gupta's published MVD,
SB and JV English; "KD" means `../KD-Karm-Darshan-English/KD-Glossary-Additions.md`.
The "For discussion" column offers a starting point only.

**Priority.** **A** = no published rendering, a conflict between authorities, or an
AA definition that may not fit the existing rendering. **B** = existing rendering
very likely fits; confirm only. **Decided** = settled in the Decisions table above,
which overrides the "For discussion" column.
