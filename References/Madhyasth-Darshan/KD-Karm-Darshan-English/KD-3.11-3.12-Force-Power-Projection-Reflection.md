## 3.11 Force–Power (State–Motion) (बल-शक्ति (स्थिति-गति))

[p. 102]

We humans have, from the past, been making a great many conjectures about force (*bal*) and power (*shakti*). After the arrival of the age of modern science, experiments were made to draw further conclusions about power. That no complete conclusion has yet emerged from the discipline of science, and that nothing has emerged for humanness, is a point worth considering. In this context, through the discipline of science there has been an attempt to recognize pressure as force, and to recognize motion, wave, and flow as power. In this, many examples were presented — in the form of electric wave and magnetic force, likewise in the form of sound wave and pressure, in the form of the wave and pressure that occur in flow — in these various ways the attempt was made to impart study. In all of these it was found that the fact of mediation (*madhyasthiyakaran*) occurring in all of them has been denied. All these studies are based only on excited motion. When there is natural-state motion, where do force and power go — this question does indeed arise.

On this issue, from the viewpoint of Madhyasth Darshan it comes to be known that force is manifest in state, power in motion. This means that in the course of evidencing effect upon another, the introduction and recognition of power in the form of mechanical process occurs. It comes to be recognized on the basis of there being states. Many methods for testing this fact or principle are at hand (*samicheen*) before the human. 'At hand' means being nearby, being available. For example a stone: we go to lift it, we lift it; then whatever apprehension arises of the conjunction of the stone and ourselves, whatever comes into understanding, that is called weight. This weight, of its own

[p. 103]

accord, is found in the meaning of evidencing the formula (*sutra*) of coexistence with the earth. This very thing — the situation of the experience of this kind of weight occurring in every human — is clear. Take an empty pot: fill it with water, or fill it with soil, or fill it with potatoes or grain, and then lift it. How it feels upon lifting — that has been called weight. Every human, on the basis of being in himself one who gives concrete form to ideas (*manakar*), and on the basis of being imaginative and free in action, thought about how weight might be measured or weighed, and found the answer to it. The method of weighing was settled. The method of measuring was settled. Instruments capable of weighing and measuring things bigger and bigger than that — the loads of trucks and trains and so on — were made. Every person sees this every day. The entire phenomenon of weight is the arrangement (*vinyas*) that occurs in the course of evidencing coexistence with the earth; it is a principle. In this way weight is found to be animated by coexistence. Every unit, endowed with force in state, evidences and displays power in motion.

In this sequence it comes into understanding that every object maintains its own orderliness within a definite good distance. For example, in one atom more than one atomic particle, remaining at a definite distance, manifests orderliness with inherence (*tva*) in the form of conduct, of which the human is the seer. If, within such a definite good distance, the distance increases, then the mediating activity working in the atom in the form of the nucleus (*madhyansh*) applies its force and calls the particles near. If an atomic particle begins to come close to the nucleus active at the centre, then too the nucleus, applying its force, fixes the particle at the good distance. This is the controlling activity that takes place in the atom. By these very activities, control over mutual distance remains. When atoms are in the form of molecules, it is on the basis of the nucleuss that mutual attraction-force is exercised, on the basis of endowment with magnetic force. As a result, one atom joining with another atom is called a molecule, together with molecular bond. It is natural for such molecules to remain endowed with weight. Because of this very weight, the propensity remains for one molecule to join with another molecule while remaining within a definite good distance. From such propensities, atoms, molecules, and large material objects formed of molecules — bodies (*pind*) — stand presented before the human, such as this earth. All objects joined to the earth, together with this earth, up to the earth's atmosphere, evidence coexistence — solid with solid, liquid with liquid, rarefied with rarefied. The arrangements which the earth and the objects that are limbs of the earth make for this, we name falling from above, running, flying, and so on. From above, only all those things fall down which are in the form of solid and liquid.

[p. 104]

The arrangement that a liquid object makes in its eagerness to evidence coexistence with liquid is called rain or flow. In the same way, the solid object that falls from above — that is the arrangement occurring with the earth, which happens in the effect of that object's evidencing coexistence with the earth. To this very arrangement the name of gravitation has been given. In reality this is nothing but the arrangement of a solid object's evidencing coexistence with a solid object. This is the respectful acceptance. In gravitation, the notion of small and big keeps running. In the form of coexistence it has been seen that each one, together with its environment, is complete. In this there simply is no small or big. This is confirmed by the fact that each one is orderliness together with its inherence. Both these principles are seen being fulfilled in the combined outcome of natural-state motion and definite conduct. In this sequence, each object being a constituent limb of this earth, honouring coexistence with the earth is, as a result, a natural propensity. The arrangement of this propensity is found to remain bounded up to the atmosphere of this earth. This earth too, together with its environment, is complete. However many limbs and organs are contained in the completeness, all these are found to remain bounded with the complete. 'Bounded' means evidencing a definite intent through a definite arrangement. Such definite intent is precisely the evidencing of coexistence — solid with solid, liquid with liquid, rarefied with rarefied. Whatever activity occurs in the course of evidencing coexistence, we call it arrangement. Its fruition is nothing but the evidence of coexistence. By this method the understanding that coexistence itself is existence becomes complete.

In relativity there is apprehension of small and big. Completeness slips out of sight. Only in completeness can each one be respected, be evaluated. It is the human alone who recognizes such evaluation and the standing of respect. When the human evidences each one in coexistence together with completeness, in such a situation it is found natural for the human to give attention toward his own completeness. Contrary to this, by the relative method attention goes toward small and big. Considering oneself big becomes a necessity. To keep that intact, keeping the other small also becomes a necessity. In this sequence treachery, revolt, exploitation, starvation, bombardment — all these deeds get done. When there is dialogue about them, their impropriety is accepted. In this manner the human becomes self-contradictory. Such a situation we address in the language of 'belonging neither to home nor to shore'. From this very thing indeterminism gets prepared. As a result the human falls prey to every kind of frustration or

[p. 105]

excitation. He moves away from natural-state motion. As a result, asociality and disorder come to hand. Unanticipated events in the form of disorder take place. This has proved entirely unnecessary for the human race. Therefore, as its alternative, it became necessary to be endowed with natural-state motion, state, and — along with certainty — hopefulness with definite plan, and evidence. This, by the method of coexistence, is at hand for all humankind.

The main point: coexistence being evidenced is itself the formula. It is natural that along with every work-arrangement, propensity, effort, activity, behaviour, result and consequence, coexistence be evaluated. The one who evaluates is the human alone.

There has been a long wait for the human to be evidenced by the coexistential or truth method. This waiting has been within the human himself. For this to be meaningful, the study of the coexistential activity-system was found sufficient for the human to be evidenced in behaviour together with humanness. Therefore its unveiling remained necessary. Coexistence alone is the ultimate truth.

State-motion, in its own form, is the combined form of pressure and flow. In some examples, while pressure remains, there is no flow — such as a stone upon the earth; in a pond upon the earth, no flow is visible in the water. In a river upon the earth, flow is visible. In the flow of water, pressure is quite clear. Pressure does remain. The flow of water is toward the slope, the slope ultimately joining up to the sea. Hence all rivers' water reaching the sea has become quite clear, whether by the natural method or by the method of destiny (*niyati*). Every pressure and flow of water can change into another kind of flow and pressure. Of these, pressure itself is the principal cause of change. For instance, water's steam, upon definite pressure, becomes capable of operating definite kinds of machines, in whose fruition magnetic electricity is obtained. Of this, it has become clear that magnetism is in the form of pressure and electric wave is in the form of flow. From this it comes to be known that any force can become the agent of another kind of force and flow. By this same method, heating — that is, burning — oily substances to operate certain machines has become readily available in the human tradition. Such machines too are seen doing many kinds of work — seen, for instance, in the form of machines that move on the earth, in water, and in the air. By such oil-combustion processes, machines that generate electricity are operated. As a result there is electromagnetic flow and pressure. Likewise, from radiant (radioactive) materials as well, through the heat-emergence process, electricity-transmitting machines

[p. 106]

being operated too is something the human has practised. Their discharge — that is, the discharge or residue of minerals, coal, oil, and radiant materials — has become the cause of pollution. On this issue we have already made study earlier. In the context of its alternative too, attention has been drawn, by way of proposal, toward the flow-force of water. Along with this, attention also needs to go toward wind and ocean waves. The human's attention going toward the sun's heat as well — there is need to be encouraged in the course of obtaining magnetic electric current. Only then is it possible for the human to gain release from the polluting tradition of great crime — anti-destiny, anti-development, anti-awakening, anti-human, opposed to the earth's environment. On examining state-motion by the existence method it comes to be known (examining means ascertaining by way of testing, observing, surveying) that all units keep displaying and expressing force in state, power in motion. Such a situation is found equally in all units, from the smallest of the small to the biggest of the big. That a unit endowed with state-motion is in the form of orderliness and participation in orderliness has become clear before. **Endowment with force in state means the evidence of orderliness in oneself; power in motion means participation in the overall orderliness.** Fundamentally, it should be known that every unit, as a result of remaining saturated in the pervasive reality, is endowed with energy, endowed with force. At the root of every unit is the atom itself. It is quite clear that the atom itself works in the form of physical, chemical, and jeevan activity. Jeevan atoms remain free from molecular bond and weight bond, and, owing to delusion, remain joined with the bonds of hope, thought, and desire. All other atoms that participate in physico-chemical activity remain joined with molecular bond and weight bond. It is by virtue of such molecular bond and weight bond that being formed as small structures and large structures is found. The smallest structure is the atom itself. Atoms alone manifest grandeur in the form of molecules and molecule-formed structures. The form of such grandeur remains animated by the formula of coexistence alone. For instance, there being more than one particle in one atom, more than one atom in one molecule, more than one molecule in a molecular structure — all these are in themselves evidences that remain animated by the formula of coexistence. Among structures there are only physical structure and chemical structure. In these structures, being complementary and useful for one another has become quite clear. In molecules of all these species, the expression of both state and motion is continuously present. It is never going to stop. From ever and ever these activities and expressions are present indeed. That is why the human stands presented as a unit fit to study all these. That is why the human himself has also to evidence orderliness in state and, in motion, participation in the overall orderliness. Only then is the evidence of mental

[p. 107]

wellbeing (*manah svasthata*) presented in tradition. Without mental wellbeing, all-round resolution in the human is simply not possible. Therefore mental wellbeing is for the human an utterly essential or supremely necessary attainment.

In this same sequence it is natural for the human, on the basis of understanding, to be glorified in himself in the formula of mental wellbeing in the form of all-round resolution. Such grandeur alone — that is, endowment with resolution alone — becomes able, or becomes successful, in evidencing prosperity, fearlessness, coexistence. This very success is the succour and life-breath (*tran* and *pran*) in, from, and for the human tradition. *Tran* means state; *pran* means motion. Being inspiration in motion, orderliness being evidenced in the environment or in the sphere of effect, and in state oneself being orderliness — this is the meaningful form of grandeur. For maintaining such grandeur there is indeed a hopeful propensity in the human. In the same way, in the atom, force in state and power in motion — that is, being endowed with a sphere of effect — is found. The remaining-secure of this mutual sphere of effect is itself the good distance in mutuality. Every unit endowed with such effects is successful in maintaining its own originality in mutuality; the name of this very thing is control.

Good distance in mutuality became clear in this way: that in mutuality the sphere of effect of each other should remain formed, remain secure. This sphere of effect is found to be only in the pervasive reality. State too, and shape, volume, mass also are found to be only in the pervasive reality. These shapes are found to be only together with activity. In every atom, activity is in the form of effort (*śram*), motion, consequence. **Effort is in itself the grandeur of the existent-state; motion is in itself the grandeur of spreading effect; consequence is found to be the grandeur of a different existent-state. In this way every consequence is arrayed in a quantitative and qualitative chain.** Within this, consequence is found in the form of fixed (*dhruva*) and long-enduring consequence. These become clear chiefly in the reckoning of the stability and instability of existent-states. Each existent-state, on the basis of being threaded by the formula of coexistence, remains meaningful in the meaning of complementariness and of usefulness-complementariness. In this way the meaningfulness of all things comes into understanding. Even one molecule has just such a history: one molecule with another molecule also — maintaining the field control related to the atom's sphere of effect — join with one another and become a body. In this the human has no contribution. All these activities, by the method of complementariness and usefulness, keep evidencing meaningfulness right up to awakening and its continuity. All the stages remain joined with one another. For instance, without keeping physical activity apart from chemical activity, chemical activity from the activity of living beings, and physico-chemical activity

[p. 108]

apart from the activity of the human, no work or behaviour is accomplished, nor can it be accomplished. All these remain inseparably joined with the human. They keep honouring their bounds indeed. For honouring the bounds with these, the human needs to be informed.

While the entire other-than-human nature is useful for the human, the human's remaining deprived of being complementary to other-than-human nature has itself been the basis of all problem-creating propensities. Because of this, whatever attainments occurred by the sequential method in giving concrete form to ideas — the road to their use and good use also fell into obstruction for this very reason: that the part of mental wellbeing remained neglected in itself. That is why, in this present, the being extremely necessary of mental wellbeing comes into understanding. Only by the coexistence method is mental wellbeing found to be attainable by all; this very possibility of all-good is quite clear. Only after endowment with resolution in himself does the human become capable of evidencing mental wellbeing. The plenitude of understanding itself is the form of mental wellbeing. Understanding is the spontaneous acceptance of coexistence. In this way, complete knowledge is in the form of acceptance. The right of acceptance and non-acceptance is indeed vested in every human.

The grandeur of state-motion is found to be in the earth, the solar system (*saur vyuh*), many solar systems, the galaxy, all galaxies. The evidence of this is that everything from the atom up to the earth becomes attainable by study, up to each being evidenced in its own state-motion. Up to this point study is readily available to every human. Thereafter there is acceptance of the state-motion of such planetary globes. In the same way the galaxy too, on the basis of being the activity of many solar systems, receives acceptance of state-motion. Together with this remains the acceptance of the pervasive reality. Hence, out of the vastness of acceptance, studying one's own necessities in the form of evidences is one necessity of the human. The provision for study occurring on the basis of acceptance depends according to the human's necessity. The vastness of knowing and believing we have already accepted in relation to the pervasive and the infinite. On the basis of knowing and believing about the pervasive and the infinite, from the study of any one locale, from the study of one unit, being assured about the form, property, intrinsic-nature, and dharma of all such units has become clear. This too has become clear: from testing one drop of the sea's water we accept what the water of the whole sea is like. But who stands ready to measure how much there is? We measure according to our necessity. The human's necessity too is limited. From examining the activity of measuring it comes to be known that this

[p. 109]

is only adding and subtracting; in measuring only 1, 2, 3 are applied. In weighing too. Likewise subtracting also arises: on one side we add, on one side it also decreases. From bringing together both adding and subtracting, only the existent-state comes to hand. As it is, as much as it is — this alone is the existent-state. Such an existent-state is neither more nor less, neither decreases nor increases. From this situation, existence neither decreases nor increases. By this method it becomes clear that the limits of our testing depend on our own necessity. In the course of adding and subtracting, the human has already accepted the meanings in the form of minus infinity and plus infinity. In this way, in existence, the imagination or acceptance of the infinite has been accepted in the human tradition in the form of being unable, to some extent, to enumerate. Until now the apprehension of the pervasive reality has been uncurrent. After apprehension of the pervasive reality occurs, the answer was obtained as to how infinite objects are. Hence study in the form of understanding — the meaning of the infinite being inseparable in the pervasive — was found to be the basis of there being all-round resolution. In this sequence every unit is present with its state and motion. 'The present' (*vartaman*) means to keep on being current. To keep on being current means to remain active. To remain active means conduct remaining clear. Conduct remaining clear means orderliness with inherence in state, and participation in the overall orderliness in motion. This earth comes into understanding as participating in one solar system — the group of all planetary globes joined with the sun. All planetary globes are unceasingly at work each in its own state-motion. Without interference with one another, all, recognizing one another, are performing definite conduct and participating in the orderliness of the solar system.

That is why state indeed is, and motion also is. On this very basis it is natural that study concerning the earth's state-motion also comes into our understanding. In this sequence, one solar system, making its work-arrangement with many solar systems at a definite good distance, displays the grandeur of solar-system inherence. To such numerous solar systems, or planet-arrays, scientists nowadays have given the name galaxy. Galaxies too are found to be many. All these, remaining at a good distance in mutuality, participate in maintaining the larger orderliness.

## 3.12 Projection–Reflection (परावर्तन-प्रत्यावर्तन)

On the issue of state-motion and force-power, the matter has already become clear with units from the smallest of the small to the biggest of the big, and that too in all planes (*pad*) and stages. Now we give attention to knowledge, thought, decision, evidence, fruit, consequence related to projection-reflection, upon knowledge being the witness.

[p. 110]

It is only in the meaning of a destination (*gamyasthali*) that the human wants to accomplish all his activities. As the human begins to walk, his destination needs to be before him. If there be no destination and only walking — what shall we call such a human? Every human can think about this. Likewise an ant too, an elephant too, animals and birds too, living creatures too — however many are in movement, their destination remains joined with each of them. The evidence of this is just that all, moving, keep reaching some definite place. Even the crocodiles that swim in water are, while swimming, at a definite, determined place. In this way some things are such as occur at every site of the earth. Some occur here and there. Some occur in a very small place. For instance, air is on all sides of the earth; the sun's light is on one side, is not on one side; a river flows far and wide; a mountain appears in one place only. Just as shrubs and plants stay in one place only. In this same earth the human also lives, the bird also lives. Some years ago the human used to think that only birds go far and far; that has changed, and now the human too roams the whole earth. Even while roaming, he is in some place, some location, indeed. Being (somewhere) remains inevitable indeed. This is a fact joined with state-motion. Without state there is no motion. This principle — motion is evidenced only along with a positive state. The human's state-motion is quite clear. While going from one place to another, the human's being, and relocation too, is visible. Along with this, the activity of projection-reflection also keeps being carried out.

Such projection-reflection we find to be in ourselves in the form of recognizing and causing recognition, as understanding; in the form of understanding, as learning; in the form of learning. Of this, the part that is understanding and making understood — in this alone projection-reflection becomes quite clear. By the reflection method we understand; by the method of making understood we are projected. However many activities there are for the human, they are by the distinctions of physical, verbal, mental; done (*krit*), caused (*karit*), intended (*anumodit*). At the root of all these is projection; at the root of projection, reflection; in the grandeur of reflection, the activity of projection is carried out by the interwoven method. The meaningfulness of projection has been able to become satisfactory only when there is a human desirous of understanding. Only with such a human is the activity of projection — that is, the activity of making the understood understood — seen becoming meaningful. In the meaningfulness of projection it is necessary to be certain of this much: that projection be consistent with sequence, work, behaviour, result, purpose. It is found meaningful on the basis of production in work, on the basis of justice in behaviour, on the basis of human-aspiration in fruition, and, in purpose,

[p. 111]

on the basis of the jeevan goal. The acceptance that these — that is, production, justice, human-aspiration, jeevan-aspiration — become meaningful is spontaneous in every human. This point has become clear earlier.

Every human becoming understanding-endowed is an ordinary event, a common event, the most desired event. In the fruition of becoming understanding-endowed alone, all the other links join of themselves. It is necessary that, together with understanding, result-consequence become the source of satisfaction. Result-consequence is from human-aspiration being meaningful; the evidence of understanding too is from human-aspiration being meaningful; the meaningfulness of the human's entire work-behaviour too is human-aspiration being meaningful. The human's entire orderliness-process too is from human-aspiration being meaningful. If the meaningfulness of humane orderliness, humane education, and the human's thinking and thought is anything, it is only from human-aspiration being fulfilled. In this the main point is just this — that is, the main point of saying all this is just this: that from human-aspiration being fulfilled, jeevan-aspiration is fulfilled as a matter of course.

In projection, mention of this matter has already come: making the understood understood, teaching the learnt, getting the done done. If there is anything apart from this, it is intention (*anumodan*); intention too is found meaningful in these three meanings. In this manner the human is projected by the done, caused, intended method. The human being projected means: endowment with knowledge, wisdom, science alone is the human's root, fundamental form. It is this that gets projected. Being projected means being evidenced by the above-said done, caused, intended methods alone. It is in such a sequence of projection that the human's evaluation comes about in undivided society, in universal system. In the discipline of undivided society, the recognition of human necessities comes about, or formula-orderliness comes about. By a third method, meaning-explanation comes about. The matter of the human, in the meaning of human necessity, from family orderliness to universal system, has become clear before. Meaning in the form of the human gets evidenced in this very sequence, becomes meaningful. For this very thing, complete knowledge, wisdom, science reaches from formula up to explanation. Of this, the seed in the form of coexistence appears being fulfilled in the form of aspiration, in the form of the knowledge-wisdom-science formula, and in the form of the explanation of work-behaviour and orderliness. This itself is the evidence of the awakened human tradition. Hence the human's spontaneous awakened propensity has become quite clear. Making this clear is itself evidence; however many work-behaviours one will do for evidence, these are all projection. The goal-fulfilment of projection gets evidenced, the intent-fulfilment gets completed. The all-good, beautiful, resolution-filled expectation becomes meaningful.

Intent, goal, expectation become meaningful in themselves by the complementary method. In the situation of jeevan not being there, the protection and nourishment of the body cannot occur. On the contrary, such a body is declared a dead body and is consigned. In this way we come to this conclusion: body, nourishment, protection are accomplished only with livingness. Therefore, as much as we are able to know, we are able to desire less than that; as much as we are able to desire, we are able to do less than that; as much as we are able to do, we are able to consume less than that. The result comes to be known: as far as the limit of consuming goes, it is the body alone. For the nourishment and protection of the body, the use of things has been recognized; and the activity-processes of things useful for societal motion have also been recognized. The tele-communication machines and instruments that are employed or are useful for societal motion are given the name telecommunication, in which far-travel, far-seeing (television), and far-hearing are included.

In the awakened human tradition, the use, good use, and purposeful methods of things get accomplished and get evidenced. Usefulness — that is, the limit of a thing's usefulness — becomes clear in the form of family-based necessity. Production more than necessity has become possible in the human tradition for this reason: that whatever we produce, however much we produce, we are not able to consume that much. Therefore the formula of production more than necessity becomes clear. This production more than necessity became spontaneous by this method: that there is inexhaustible power and force in jeevan; production work being accomplished in the combined form of jeevan and body;

Even now, in the situation of the human tradition remaining deluded, it has been seen that the fewest people are engaged in production work, while more and more people keep using. Even from before awakening, the evidence of production more than necessity being carried out in the human race is clear. Even so, the cause of straying is one alone: we are told in our schooling — "Necessity is infinite, means are limited; therefore only through struggle shall we be able to fulfil our necessity." From this viewpoint, the human's giving more attention toward accumulation and comfort, the satisfaction-point of accumulation-comfort not being found, necessity came to be assumed infinite. This itself is the basis of straying and delusion. We shall be able to escape from this whirl only when it becomes clear and evidenced that the necessity of every family is limited; the possibility of fulfilling necessity is greater; jeevan powers are inexhaustible; production occurs only in the combined form of jeevan and body; the body's necessity is limited. From this viewpoint our attention goes toward prosperity. Its source is precisely production more than the family's necessity.

By the said method, the determination of the family's necessity: within the family itself, one or more than one person holds the capability of producing more than the family's spontaneous necessity. When there is production more than the family's necessity, then in the understanding-endowed family the good fortune of resolution-prosperity being evidenced dawns. From this, this family's becoming endowed with gladness and trust for the family members comes about. Every member of the family becomes worthy of trusting in himself. From trust in oneself alone — respect for excellence occurring, the evidence of understanding occurring, and, in the evidence of endowment with understanding, the evidence of the person being social in behaviour and self-reliant in occupation dawns of itself. Such dawning-capacity is the fruition of the human's being a unit of the knowledge order (*gyanavastha*). Existence in the form of coexistence is continuous. Therefore darshan-knowledge is continuous; jeevan is continuous, therefore jeevan-knowledge is continuous. Humane necessity is continuous, therefore humane-necessity-knowledge is continuous. On the basis of these very treasures of knowledge, ascertaining the method by which the jeevan goal and the human goal are evidenced — by the method of wisdom-endowed science and science-endowed wisdom — is itself the entire coexistential management, treatise, thought, and darshan; this itself is existence-based human-centred contemplation knowledge, Madhyasth Darshan.
