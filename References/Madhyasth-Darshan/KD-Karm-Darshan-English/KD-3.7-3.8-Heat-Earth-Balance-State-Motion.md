## 3.7 Heat and the Earth's Balance (ऊष्मा और धरती का संतुलन)

[p. 73]

We see nature-other-than-human (*manavettar prakriti*) as the greenery spread out upon this earth, replete with mineral wealth, and as the world of living beings. This earth is endowed with mountains, forests, rivers, streams, and living animals. Endowment means that all these are indeed present on this earth. The earth has minerals contained within itself in solid and rarefied form, and the chemical world spread out upon it is seen to exist in solid, liquid, and rarefied forms. Many things in solid form are indeed to be seen. For example, the bones formed in the body

[p. 74]

do remain in solid form. Such things, before becoming solid, do remain in liquid form. Chemical liquid, being combined through conjunction with heat by the disintegration method, remains present in rarefied form. This means that by the heat method a thing begins to expand. If heat is made to enter into some solid thing, then that thing begins to spread into more space than its definite volume-form. It goes on expanding until it becomes liquid, becomes rarefied.

We recognise heat by this method: the effect that occurs when a thing burns or is burnt — to this we are giving the name heat. We are able to recognise it in another way as well: a thing that has become more excited is always heated up. That is, its temperature increases. It is the human who recognises temperature. The rest of nature is affected by temperature. Setting the human apart, temperature does not get recognised by any measuring standard. To deploy temperature in experiments and to be able to assess the resulting outcomes is accomplished only by the human — that is, only the human carries it out.

Recognising occurs in both material and conscious (*jad–chaitanya*) nature; in recognising and fulfilling, material nature stands evidenced. Whereas knowing and accepting occur only in conscious nature, in the human. The human, while being in the combined form of the material and the conscious, is endowed with the capacity for knowing-accepting. On the basic point, recognising too is an activity. One substance projects temperature for another; whatever temperature they express out of themselves, the molecules and atoms around them accept that temperature. This is what we are calling recognising. As a result, transformations come about. For example, whatever heat is deployed for melting iron, the iron accepts it; the molecule and atom of iron accept it. As a result it melts. We humans explain this activity thus: it recognises, it fulfils — only then does it melt. The human alone is such a unit that changes heated iron into various shapes; this is what we regard as casting into a mould. In this, the iron keeps on projecting heat until it sets again, until it becomes cold. All the substances joined with it apportion the iron's temperature to themselves. Eventually the iron becomes cold.

In the effort to stimulate temperature — that is, in the activity of manifesting and increasing temperature — there is always the conjunction of one that burns and one that makes burn. For example, coal is what burns; the air keeps making it burn. Going to its root, for burning coal in the initial stage some burning thing or other is there in the role of the igniter — such as burning fire. Proceeding by these methods,

[p. 75]

the human is seen carrying out the activity of stimulating temperature from coal, wood, natural gas, petroleum gas, or by the radiative process. Along with this, the identification of explosive substances, their combination, and heat-stimulation by the pressure method is also being seen.

Deploying such heat, humans do accomplish necessary tasks — such as heating water, cooking food, warming a room, drying medicines, and so on. In the same way, the human does carry out numerous activities by the conjunction of heat, under control. The human tradition has become habituated to this. Wherever temperature may be, it needs to be more than the temperature of that environment — only then is it temperature. Put another way, wherever there is temperature, there has to be less temperature around it; only then is the greater temperature projected toward the lesser temperature. Just as, wherever we light a fire, greater temperature comes about there. The activity of being projected toward lower temperature is seen thus: where temperature remains manifest, it spreads through the molecules and atoms around it becoming heated. This means that heated molecules, spreading temperature into their sphere of influence, into the environment, make heated those molecules which remain lower than its temperature. The molecules-atoms of the environment accept this and carry this sequence forward. In this way temperature, being projected, goes on diminishing, and eventually they arrive at normal temperature. Normal temperature means being in natural-state motion (*svabhav gati*) (natural-state motion means the existent-states that there are in development progression, development, awakening progression, and awakening — that is, orderliness with inherence (*tva*); participation in the overall orderliness itself is natural-state motion).

An experiment of this can be done as follows: light a candle and place it in one corner of any room, or light a kerosene lamp, or a heater, light a stove, or light bulbs giving as much light as possible; the amount of heat we feel on touching it will be felt as less at some distance from it, still less farther away, and least of all in the opposite corner — every student can observe and test this. If the same experiment is done placing it in the middle of the room, it becomes known that heat is projected equally in all directions.

[p. 76]

In the same way, by the above experimental method we can also accept that the sun's heat is being projected equally in all directions. The heat projected from the sun's orb touches the earth in its facing-ness toward the sun. Along with this it is also understood that, as the earth rotates, the part that remains facing the sun becomes heated. For this very reason sunrise and sunset occur. Even at the time of setting, a minimum of heat remains touching the earth. In the middle between these two, midway, the maximum heat remains touching it. Every person can test and observe this.

It is by virtue of the earth's rotational motion that the configuration of being toward and away from the sun becomes clear. The vibrational motion occurring in step with the dance of this very rotational motion is its form of being evidenced in circumambulatory (orbital) motion around the sun. At the root of this is the very tendency of orderliness with inherence (*tva*). This tendency is in the form of evidencing orderliness while remaining at a mutually good distance. In this sense, definite fulfilment is present in every reciprocity — that is, it stands evidenced. In this way this earth is seen circling around on all sides, keeping in tune with all the planets and orbs participating in this solar system, being toward and away from the sun, remaining at a definite good distance from the sun. Thus this circular motion-path being not perfectly round but elliptic (elliptical) — this is the tendency of the matter. The principal intent of this tendency is the self-inspired tendency of seasonal change in the earth. Ultimately, the intent is precisely that development progression, development, awakening progression, and awakening be evidenced on this earth. How are we humans to understand this intent?

The above point comes to be understood in this way: on this earth all four orders are displayed. What was not, does not come to be. On analysing this fact it becomes known that the root tendency of these four orders remained contained in the material order itself. Had this not been so, the arising of compounds and of greenery would simply not have been possible — whereas on this earth compound activity has been carried out, greeneries have spread. Along with this, from tiny insects and worms up to numerous beasts and birds, the emergence of living animals and their tradition has been established. Not only this, the human body tradition has also been established. The human has no logic or standing for denying any of these. Second, the human's standing is just this: the human studies what 'is'. All four orders are objects of study for the human. From studying, we get the thread of this fact: that on the earth, from the seasonal-sequence orderliness itself, or from its influence, from complementariness, from the physical activity orderliness itself, the chemical activity orderliness

[p. 77]

has emerged upon this earth. In this very sequence, the formations of all four orders have become fully clear. All minerals too, in solid form, are presented as the molecular formation of their own respective species or as mixed formations. On the basis of the various species of these, the compound happening, chemical resonance (*urmi*), and grandeur — that is, dance — has taken place. On the basis of chemical substance, the entire biological cell (*prankosha*), the structures composed of biological cells, plant species from small to large, animal-order species, and the formation of the human body species stand evidenced on this earth itself. From this it became clear that, together with energy-endowment, force-endowment, and activeness, the tendencies of development progression and development remain contained therein.

That jeevan is in the developed stage has been made clear before. It remains in possession of the constitutionally complete stage. In this constitutionally complete atom itself, the arrangement for being in motion according to hope (*asha*) remains in place. It is self-inspired that jeevan, remaining in motion according to hope in this way, by force of hope operates the bodies of some animal order or knowledge order. The possibility of such jeevans reaching from any one earth to another earth remains within reach. All told, it comes into human understanding that on every earth, development progression stands evidenced in the form of chemical-physical formations and their traditions. From this it has become clear that every earth, maintaining a certain definite distance from some enriched earth, working in the form of participation in orderliness, keeps evidencing in itself development progression, development, awakening progression, and awakening. The seer of all this is the awakened human alone, not the deluded human. In this sequence, many earths are indeed at work in some order or other. All undeveloped earths are engaged in moving toward evidencing development. This too is an indication of the fact that all the earths that are in an undeveloped condition — that is, those earths in which development progression has not even manifested — all such earths keep up their tendency toward setting development progression in array. In this same sequence, the notion is current in the human tradition that the sun-orb, which is in the ultimate condition of decline, will also in the course of time be in development progression. All these situations have become clear on the side of development and awakening occurring. In whatever order an earth may be, the root seed of evidencing development progression and development remains contained in it. Because every excitation is understood to be working toward normal motion, toward natural-state motion; natural-state motion toward development progression; development progression toward evidencing development; development toward evidencing awakening progression; awakening progression toward evidencing awakening, and toward evidencing the continuity of awakening. By this method we can trust in the fact that the entire

[p. 78]

coexistence is active with balance precisely in the sense of displaying development and awakening according to the existent-state. In this very sequence, one sees the presence in several planet-orbs of maximum heat such as there is in the sun, and the absorption of heat into all those planet-orbs facing them in which less heat remains. In this sequence, how much effort (*śram*) this earth has performed within itself — this becomes evident to every awakened human.

On the upper surface of this earth are found forests, oceans, and, in a small part, desert. Whatever kind of forest it may be, most of the trees and plants, creepers and shrubs of the forest, making the substance known by the name apana air (*apan vayu* — carbon dioxide) their food-material, or making it their digestion-material, provide the prana air — that is, oxygen. Whereas coal, and the oil-petroleum derived from mineral coal — in all the machines in which these are used as fuel, or where they are used by the technological method in the operation of machinery — the life-fatal air (carbon monoxide) found in the discharged fuel-residues of all these is being regarded as extremely fatal for the environment. From this it becomes known that, at the root of such mineral coal, the great forests which, having been buried in the earth, are today manifest in the form of coal — those forests, before being buried, had digested that very substance within themselves; making this their food-material, they must have become sky-kissing trees. It is natural that the conception of such sky-kissing-ness be made on the basis of the thickness of the mineral coal formed in the earth. At present, in some places, a coal seam of 150 to 180 metres' thickness lies deposited. From this an estimate can be made of how enormous the trees must have been. For one metre of coal to form, at least ten metres of tree length is required; everyone can understand its density by the mathematical method. Thus, what a forest of great trees there must have been to make such immeasurable coal can be worked out only by the mathematical method. It is very difficult even to fit into ordinary conception; what can be brought into conception is just this — that there must have been sky-kissing dense forests. Such forests, buried inside the ground, must have become coal over hundreds of thousands of years; that same forest must have been digesting the life-fatal air (carbon monoxide); now that same thing produces carbon monoxide. At present, plants digest carbon dioxide and produce oxygen. All this has become known to the knowledgeable and the scientists. We are mentioning this matter here for this reason: what is its purpose for this earth?

[p. 79]

We have understood the point that coal, carbon, absorbs heat. It takes into itself as much of the environmental heat as possible. An experiment of this can be done as follows: coat one uniform patch of ground with coal; coat another similar patch with white. At the time of harsh sunshine, where the coal is coated, placing one's foot there feels hot; where the white is coated, it feels less hot. The conditions in between these two can also be seen in other places. From this it becomes known that coal digests more and more heat into itself. It distributes it slowly. From this evidence we come to understand that the cosmic heat reaching this earth — of which the greatest is the sun's heat — needed to be digested on this earth. Because it was only after the earth maintained its heat balance that greenery remained secure on the surface of the earth. Only after greenery remained secure did the emergence of worms, insects, and animals occur, and only after that the emergence of the human — this we humans understand as the sequential formation-grandeur. At the root of this grandeur, the earth's maintaining heat balance within itself has been a principal attainment. In this, the human made no contribution. It is the oil contained in this very coal that, maturing in the earth along with mineral substances, has become available in the form of mineral oil. Its preservation too remained ensured inside the earth, with the protective shield of a suitable environment. Keeping it secure means precisely maintaining an environment such that prana air, i.e. oxygen, is not allowed to reach the oil.

In this way we arrive at the fact that, in step with the earth's vibrational motion, there is vibration and pulsation in the whole of the minerals; more pulsation than that in chemical substances; more pulsation than that in prana air; and as a result, formations stand manifest on this earth. All these are in the sense of manifesting such balanced formations; prior to this is the entire activity accomplished in the background; hence all these have been carried out lawfully indeed — the orders stand manifest with control and balance. We humans need to understand this well, and there is need to set ourselves in order by the method of preservation and enrichment with diligent study. At the root of the human's being in order is the clarity of a harmonious, music-like working of knowledge, science, and wisdom. This very working, being deployed in language and in work-behaviour, propounds the purposes. The human's purpose is precisely to evidence resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, coexistence.

In this sequence, humans by their own logic think thus: when the sun becomes developed (natural-state motion), from where will heat be obtained for the earth; how will these greeneries remain on this earth; all these greeneries are due to sunlight. The thread of the resolution of this is found in the human ourselves, at the very first step. A definite heat (temperature) arrangement of the human body is in place. According to our measuring standard we put it at 98°–99° Fahrenheit. The name of the measuring standard is taken to be the thermometer. This fact has been seen in the living, breathing human on the earth: where the temperature is below 0° Fahrenheit, humans live there too, and the temperature of the human body of the humans there is also measured as that same normal temperature. On this earth where heat of more than 120° Fahrenheit holds sway, there too the same normal temperature of the human body is being measured — only then is the human said to be healthy. If the temperature changes according to the environment, one is called sick, or is said to have died. On the basis of this evidence, the health of the earth can also be considered. The cosmic rays and heat in the earth's environment that, being projected, touch the earth — so that the earth's temperature neither rises nor falls from this, the earth maintains in balance, from within its own self, a heat-digestion arrangement; this has been described before. This is contained in the earth in the form of the seam of coal. The human, out of his own indiscretion and out of greed, tore open the earth's belly, extracted the coal, and the earth became heat-afflicted. No scientist, nor the technological world, is prepared to accept this responsibility. Its remedy remained set aside. By this method where will we arrive; what can happen? The form of the first evil consequence is that, after the earth becomes heated by a certain amount, greenery, animals, and the human will not be able to remain on this earth. In the second situation, after the earth becomes heat-afflicted, in the event of the melting of all the ice stored at the north and south poles, there can be a state of submergence in water everywhere except the high mountain peaks. What the condition of the human can be in such a situation, all can imagine. The situation of the third evil consequence can also be this: whenever on this earth the compound activity in the form of water was accomplished, a situation must certainly have formed of one burning and one burn-causing substance joining in a spontaneous way. At that time, for keeping both the burning and the burn-causing sub-substances normal, the cosmic ray alone has been serviceable. Today's human has come to recognise such cosmic rays — alias the world of radiation and radiativity. On keeping this matter in mind, the conception described below comes to attention, even if the said event should not occur. Today, out of our own indiscretion and greed, we go on making the earth heat-afflicted;

[p. 81]

as a result, the possibility of the earth's safety has become slight. The quantities of prana air have gradually begun to decrease; the substance hostile to prana air has begun to increase — this is what we are calling pollution.

In such a situation, the cosmic ray, which has been the combiner for water to form, can, by force of combination with such pollution, become the cause of the opposite event (the cause of the disintegration of water). In such a situation what will become of the human? For this dreadful catastrophe upon the breast of the earth — all of us who are called scientists, knowledgeable or ignorant, who are called the helmsmen of the technological world — do these have any answer to it? No one is to be seen giving an answer to this matter. Because no one accepts responsibility. This itself is its testimony. There is difficulty in accepting responsibility, because a person becomes answerable for atonement. Therefore, effort toward its correction alone appears to be the sole duty. Its propriety finds acceptance with more and more people.

The fourth calamity: on the basis of the earth becoming heat-afflicted, the deflection of the earth's magnetic field of influence is also one possibility. In this earth, between the north and the south pole, by a self-inspired method, a magnetic current or magnetic field of influence has formed. This matter too has been accepted. On this very basis, direction-indicating instruments and apparatus have been made and put to use. By this method, the bondedness that pole has maintained with pole — on this very basis the unbroken work of the earth's being solid kept being carried out. By this, layer upon layer of the earth, heat-collection, digestion, and equilibration kept being accomplished. By this the earth's health — the arrangement of remaining balanced in respect of temperature — has persisted. At present the human does make the declaration of the earth being unbalanced, but does not accept his liability for it; therefore, the searching out of remedies does not come about; as a result, resolution does not come about. In this sequence, the possibility of temperature increasing further in the earth is being accepted. This warns of the following: the magnetic current bonded from pole to pole, after reaching a certain temperature, can be deflected; from this it is natural for this earth, instead of being solid in itself, to begin to scatter. After this scattering, it comes into human imagination in the form of dust. After that, where will the human remain, where will animals remain, where will greenery remain, where will water remain, where will the arrangement of water's cyclicality remain? All these inverse events are indeed in a position to wound the human psyche considerably.

The undertaking of its remedy is just this: whatever work is to be done from coal and mineral oil, its alternative will have to be identified. Stopping the use of coal and mineral oil is the sole remedy. Along with this, we have already accepted that the radiative fuel system too is fatal for the earth and for the earth's environment. Fuel by the radiation method is fatal; fuel by the coal and mineral method is also fatal. To stop their use is the human's first duty. The second duty is to obtain the alternative of a fuel arrangement for maintaining human convenience. This alternative is apparent in a natural form — converting flow force into electrical energy. From the provision of this single source, by a pollution-free system, electrical energy can become available in excess of need. Along with this, for becoming prosperous: the pressure of wind, the pressure of ocean waves, cow-dung gas, natural gas which in a natural way keeps moving from within the earth toward the sky — making full use of all such sources is in the human's interest, is in the sense of human happiness and prosperity. For those machines which run only on oil, the research into making edible and non-edible oils (obtained from plants, from trees) machine-usable by various combinations should be completed. Only then can the human also maintain the conveniences obtained from machines. Fuel prosperity can remain intact. For oil purposes, the introduction of oil-bearing trees in the forests is certainly possible; this can also happen along roads and in parks and gardens. It can also be on the boundary-ridges of farmers' fields; this is a programme to be accomplished solely on the basis of good sense in the human. In the upper surface of the earth there are vast possibilities — of creating energy sources, of creating fuel sources. Pressure sources, flow sources, solar energy sources — all these are naturally there in abundant measure. Along with these, the possibility of the multiplication of oil-bearing trees is on the earth itself. Inside the earth there is no source of this kind at all. What is there is only for keeping the earth healthy. If we test and observe rightly, the entire source of good fortune is on the surface of the earth itself. Inside the earth, no source equal or similar to the upper part is to be seen.

Attention went toward the earth's nether (deep-ground) water source; in a mere 25–30 years the nether water source became depleted in most places. As a result, the surface water source dried up — ponds and step-wells dried up. The human set off toward ruin. Give a little attention to this issue. We humans, out of delusion, went grappling with the hope of getting more from the nether sources than from the upper surface. From this too an unprecedented loss has already occurred. If meaningful thought is given to this issue, it becomes certain that the source of the nether water is nothing but the water flowing on the earth's upper surface. Along with this, on paying further attention it becomes known that the grandeur of the biological order, the grandeur of the animal order and of the knowledge order, their workings, and the auspicious site of work-behaviour, stand evidenced only on the upper surface of the earth. Because it is the human alone who has interfered in the inner part of the earth; its result has already turned inauspicious. Its clarification has been done. The human wants only wellbeing (*shubh*). There is need to become adept in the formula and explanation of wellbeing. On the basis of such a formula and explanation of wellbeing, or the formula and explanation of universal wellbeing, one lives evidencing the coexistence method — that is, law, control, balance, justice, dharma, and, with truth, resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, coexistence. Universality is the formula and explanation of wellbeing. We have understood this matter well. We have explained it before: undividedness is the formula, universality is the explanation; its bearer-carrier is the human alone. This itself is the evidence of awakening, the grandeur of good fortune. This is also the human's desire; therefore, all improper, unnecessary, ruinous tendencies, work-behaviours, and configurations that have occurred through the human out of delusion — to correct these with awakening is itself the human's honour, and, together with it, good fortune.

## 3.8 State–Motion (स्थिति-गति)

[p. 84]

The human wants to be evidenced in state–motion with awakening. Such evidences are hoped for in relation both to one's own self and to the whole. First of all, this principle will have to be taken to heart: state and motion are indivisible. 'Being', 'remaining in motion' — this is its root evidence. Even an atom remains in motion on the basis of being. A planet-orb too remains in motion on the basis of being. The entire world of matter maintains motion in the form of formation and decomposition only on the basis of 'being'. All plants too, on the basis of being alone, remain in motion in the form of growth (*pushti*). The entire animal order too remains in motion on the basis of being. The human evidences motion only on the basis of being awakened. Thus, in existence in the form of coexistence, every thing that has attained eternal establishment is present in the form of state–motion. By this method the whole of state–motion is indivisible. Force in state, power in motion — or, state is seen as force and motion as power — and force and power are certainly indivisible.

This indivisibility of state–motion is also seen in the jeevan activities occurring in the human. In the human, state appears in the form of realisation (*anubhav*), motion in the form of evidence. The buddhi, together with the enlightenment (*bodh*) of reality, actuality, and verity, is itself the state, and resolve (*sankalp*), in the form of the tendency to display it, is itself the motion. Enlightenment itself is the manifestation of realisation and of evidence. Realisation-evidence (lived testimony) is enlightenment-acceptance, manifestation-information; such information has its purpose for the realisation of others. Direct recognition (*sakshatkar*) of justice, dharma, and truth, in the form of contemplating, is the state in the chitta, and its being pictured in the form of visualisation is the motion. The entire visualisation of the chitta's workings becoming clear in the form of deliberation (*tulan*) — that is, in the form of justice, dharma, and truth — is the vritti's innate state; the deliberation accomplished in the vritti being analysed by the analysis method and being communicated is the vritti's innate thought-motion. In the clear or essential form of the analysis, value gets accepted. To taste this is itself the state of the mind (*mun*); being in motion in the form of carrying out the selection activity for its meaningfulness is surveyed in every human. In this manner it becomes clear that the human too is in state–motion in every way. Thus, after the human becomes endowed with understanding, being evidenced is natural. This means precisely: so long as we are not evidenced, the acquiring of knowledge, the acquiring of wisdom, and the acquiring of science in order to be evidenced is itself the meaning of education and instruction. For this, the coexistential education sequence is appropriate. Hence there is need to pay attention in order to be an understanding human.

[p. 85]

To understand coexistential thought — knowledge, wisdom, science — is itself understanding. Because, however much has been lived and understood according to idealism and materialism, no method of universal wellbeing could be propounded from it. It is necessary to underline this clearly. The source of universal wellbeing is coexistential knowledge, science in the form of thought, and wisdom alone. This can be understood very easily, and can also be made understood. We can let live, and we can also live; every one of us persons can be evidenced in the state–motion of understanding, and can incline other people toward being evidenced. This itself is the method of the dissemination of understanding: the whole of humankind being evidenced in state–motion. All the situations are clear in the form of the universal and the undivided-society relationship. Participating in all these steps is itself the meaning of evidence. Such evidences are projected in, from, and for the human by the realisation-based method. Such realisation-based evidences are universal; for this, to live and see is the only way.

The human wants to be adept in the discipline of science. In becoming adept, the experimental and practical hindrance so far has come down to just these points. First, no trace of the ultimate truth could be found; second, the root measure (*mool matra*), the source of energy, and the goal could not be ascertained; third — the study of the human could not take place; fourth — the purpose of existence could not become clear. In this manner, even after experimenting in science with such long and wide dedication, all those wishing to live clearly ended up frustrated. On this issue it was first settled that whatever claims come before science by the materialist method or by the idealist method — that is, whatever question marks arise — the attempt be made to solve them on the basis of the economic, social, and political poles. But on this basis no logically consistent and purpose-consistent work-behaviour, orderliness, or tradition according to any meaningful view could be established. Compared with this, such a desire is being found in every man and woman who desires wellbeing — in other words, who desires universal wellbeing. On the other side, the matter of becoming frustrated has become clear. Even among those who become frustrated, many people must surely be desiring universal wellbeing. If one proceeds to reason consistently by the method of desiring wellbeing, universal wellbeing alone is the pole. Now here, taking the desire for universal wellbeing as the acceptance of all humankind, or the acceptance of the majority, this treatise has been carried forward in the sequence of thinking, understanding, and presenting evidences further.

Whatever methods have so far been called science — in them, the conception and evidence for clarifying the following points have been from their own standpoint. The standpoint of science is committed to struggle. On this basis, the whole of nature other than the human is

[p. 86]

for the human, for the human's use. Among humans, only he is fit to use it who is the most powerful — that is, he who beats, plunders, murders, terrorises, and slaughters others. Here the further argument may arise: has science ever declared such a thing anywhere? The declaration of science is not in the form of a document. Those who have gone on living in the name of science — that is, who, citing science, have composed treatises in the form of their opinions — in them, mention of this kind is found.

In this, the main declaration — uncertainty, instability — becomes clear as a point from within it: science is independent in itself; the scientist is not. Confirmation of this matter is indeed found in scientific documents. Here this very question arises: is the responsibility of science the book's, or the human's? In this sequence, regarding the document that is in the form of the book: the belief has come to be established that, further on, whatever is evidenced by the instrument is the truth, even if it be not the ultimate truth. Its conclusion came out to be just this: the instrument became the principal evidence. By this method, responsibility, instead of being on the human, was thrust upon instruments. In this, the convenience for human thinking can only be this: not to generate the entanglements of reasoning. It is the human who will use reasoning. With instruments no reasoning is possible at all, because what an instrument does, it keeps doing again and again. Whereas it is the human who makes the instruments.

In the situation of observation and testing we find that every human is a proponent of responsibility. Every human is a proponent of justice; every human is a proponent of orderliness. At the root of all these, every human is on the side of understanding.

Mention must certainly be made of this: that we humans, in the form of the definition of the human, are the ones who give concrete shape to ideas (*manakar* — the mind's forms), and we have remained incapable, by the prevalent education-method, of evidencing mental wellbeing. Mental wellbeing itself is the human's essentiality; it is in the sequence of evidencing this essentiality that we have already examined within ourselves and evidenced the fact of the human's being happy. This is successful by the coexistence method. In every discipline, we become happy only by living with resolution. The formula of living with resolution was found meaningful only with understanding. Understanding is accomplished with the coexistence-complete standpoint. In this sequence, we have already understood before that the eternal source of understanding is in three disciplines. It has already become evident from the study presented up to now that understanding does not get evidenced by the idealist method, by the materialist method, or by logical methods. By the proposed coexistential

[p. 87]

method alone, by the realisation-based method, every man and woman can evidence all-round resolution with humane conduct, in the form of understanding, honesty, responsibility, and participation. They can experience happiness for ever and ever within themselves, because being endowed with universal resolution is itself the very first rung of understanding. This is indeed present in every awakened human. For this very reason, the possibility of being happy for ever and ever also remains contained therein. On this very basis, the need to be evidenced persists. Thus, it became possible for every understanding human to be responsible and to be benevolent in, from, and for the tradition. The meaning of benevolence (*upkar*) too is clear. In the sense of awakening: to make understood what has been understood, to have done what one has done, to teach what one has learnt — all this being resolution-bringing, benevolence consequently becomes meaningful. In this manner it has become clear how the human can be benevolent. It has also become clear how all of us — those regarding themselves as understanding and those regarding themselves as ignorant — created problems. Along with this it has also become clear that the world of science, in its mentality and in its works of enlightening, in the course of arriving very far from responsibilities, tried to recognise the human as being like a machine, and in this failed utterly.

There is simply no thing that does not become clear by the coexistence method. The whole of things is worthy of being understood by the one who understands; it does get understood; it has also been understood. To make understood what has been understood is a self-inspired activity. Thus, the basis of the manifest and the unmanifest has become clear. The human was able to evidence, was able to make understood, what he wished — this itself is the evidence of being manifest. Along with this there is also living. What we kept regarding as understood but could not make understood — this itself was called the unmanifest. Whereas existence in the form of coexistence is eternally present and manifest; in it nothing at all is unmanifest.
