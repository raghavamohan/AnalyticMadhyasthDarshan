| बल (bare, general/ontological sense) | bal | strength (inherent strength; "capacity to apply force") | FINALISED per Raghava (2026-08-05), superseding "force" for बल's general/ontological sense — the standing, endowed capacity a unit or faculty has (KD 3.11's *sthiti*/state term, paired with *shakti*/power in *gati*/motion). Rationale: (1) बल is described as inherent, constant, and present in a unit's natural state independent of any interaction (SB pp. 60–61: "every entity has... inseparability of strength in state and power in motion"; SB p. 98: "forcefulness is existentially inherent in each atomic particle") — this fits a standing capacity/endowment better than the relational, interaction-tied physics sense of "force." (2) Direct precedent in the source itself: SB's own published translation renders अक्षय बल — the five *jeevan* faculties (mun/vritti/chitta/buddhi/atma) themselves — as "**Inexhaustible Strength**" in the Five Forces table header (SB p. 85/SB-Samadhanatmak-Bhautikvad.md line ~772-790), even though the same table's left column (विद्युत-चुम्बकीय बल, गुरुत्वाकर्षण बल, मध्यस्थ बल) is rendered "Electromagnetic/Gravitational/Mediative Force." (3) The mutual-necessity claim ("शक्ति के बिना बल का परिचय, बल के बिना शक्ति का परिचय नहीं होता," SB ~p. 61) reads coherently as strength/capacity requiring exercise (power) to be evidenced, and vice versa — not strictly true of physics-force, which can exist statically without power (P=Fv=0 at rest). **Exception, retained:** where बल names one of SB's Five Forces table's specific, conventionally-established physics categories (विद्युत-चुम्बकीय बल, गुरुत्वाकर्षण बल, मध्यस्थ बल), keep **"force"** (Electromagnetic/Gravitational/Mediative Force) — matching SB's own choice there and preserving KD 3.11's direct rhetorical engagement with physics' own vocabulary ("where do force and power go [in natural-state motion]?", KD p. 102). शक्ति is unaffected by this decision and stays **"power"** throughout (consistently rendered so in SB: "Sentient Power," "Inexhaustible Power," "mediative power," "generative and degenerative powers," "hope-power," "atma-power"). Cross-ref: बल सम्पन्न / चुंबकीय बल सम्पन्न rows above (compounds, already settled as "forceful"/"forcefulness" — consistent with this decision, unaffected). **Applied corpus-wide on 2026-08-23** to 23 bare/general/ontological instances in `KD-Karm-Darshan-English.md`, including the chapter title, state–motion formulations, and the five strengths of *jeevan*. **Translation-policy note:** Rakesh Gupta’s published MVD/SB/JV terminology and `MD-Mapping.xlsx` remain the project’s default authority; this rendering is a deliberate, narrowly scoped exception approved by Raghava where the standing-capacity sense is clearer as "strength." Do not generalise the override: named physical/interaction categories remain "force," and बल सम्पन्न / बल सम्पन्नता remain "forceful" / "forcefulness." Full reasoning trail: chat discussion preserved in *The Ontology of Coexistence* project history; see `Studies/The-Ontology-of-Coexistence/Research-Note-Physics-Of-Satta-Unit-Interaction.md` §4 and §7.1 for the surrounding argument (research note itself not yet updated to match — its "What MD says" claims still say "force" for बल's general sense, and its verbatim SB quotes correctly keep SB's own published wording regardless of this decision) |
