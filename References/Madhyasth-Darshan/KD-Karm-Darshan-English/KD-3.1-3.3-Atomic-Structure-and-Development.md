## 3.1 Atomic Structure and Molecular Composition (परमाणु संरचना एवं अणु रचना)

[p. 53]

The desire to recognise atomic structure has been found in the human being from the distant past.

By the method of decomposition, the human being applied thought, reflection, and experiment in order to reach the fundamental substance. Even at the end of decomposition, the expectation of some definite fundamental substance remained.

Experimenting with the above mindset, the human being arrived at this conclusion: that if we keep on decomposing any substance whatsoever, we will reach a state in which the decomposed units function in a self-organised (*svayamsphurt*) manner — this is understood. Such a functioning unit we call the atom. The desire to understand how all this functions has also remained in the human being.

The atom has, at its middle, one or more particles (*ansh*), and, revolving on all sides in its orbits, one or more particles. Below this, the matter of an atom does not come to be. That the number of particles in the middle or in the orbit might increase — this arises in human imagination alone.

In this connection, two main questions came to attention — 1. How could recognition come to be established in the mutuality of the atomic particles? 2. After recognition, why did they enter into orderliness? On carrying this line of thought further, it was understood that coexistence itself is its principal cause, because the omnipresent substance (Omnipresence) is fully immersed, soaked, and enveloping within every single object, in energy. The omnipresent substance is all-permeating within all of these. In this way it became clear that, from the subtlest to the largest unit, being saturated within the omnipresent is the basis of remaining energy-endowed. Being energy-endowed itself is understood to be being force-endowed and being endowed with magnetic force. It is by virtue of this force-endowment that in the subtle atomic particle there is the propensity to recognise mutuality and to participate in orderliness. For the human being it is necessary to accept the recognition of one by another as evidence of this force. The human being alone is endowed with the innate right to recognise propensity, action, and result. In this very progression, when human beings recognise one another, there they present themselves in the form of orderliness itself. Wherever they have committed error in recognising this meaning, functioning and behaving in the form of disorderliness, the cause of the problem

[p. 55]

comes to be. In this way it is understood that every atomic particle (on the basis of being saturated within the omnipresent) is functioning in orderliness precisely as a result of recognising one another. The functional form of orderliness is definite conduct itself.

In the above manner, as a result of every atomic particle too being energy-endowed, recognising one another is natural for it; the purpose of this recognising is to conduct itself in a combined form — this was understood as an eternal law. From this a further formula also emerges: that on the basis of such recognising alone does constitution-organisation come to be, and constitution-organisation comes to be evidenced in the sense of orderliness, being the ever-present. In this way, that within the omnipresent substance every single entire object is indivisible, and that this is understood in the form of coexistence, has become easy to comprehend. In this progression, that coexistence is eternally influenced — this is acceptable to us humans.

Coexistence itself became the fundamental formula and the basis of orderliness. At the root of every state of motion, coexistence is well established. In this way it became clear that coexistence itself is the basis of the diverse action (activity) and of the conduct of each one of them.

It was found that difference of conduct comes to be only from difference in the number of functioning particles in atoms. On the basis of such difference of conduct alone, the human being made the attempt to determine the species of atoms and the number of species. Human beings have so far laid claim to there being 108 species of atoms on this earth, and hold the hope of discovering the atom of a further species.

On understanding the atom in this way, we humans become clear in an orderliness-centred mindset. As a result, it became natural for the human being to be ready to recognise the innate orderliness within himself. In this progression it became natural for the human being to understand humane conduct. From this it is found that value, character, and morality — their combined expression, communication, and manifestation — have, within the human tradition, the basis of orderliness, from it, and for it.

By virtue of this very fact, in the animal-order, the plant-order, and the mineral-order — in as many species as are reckoned in solid, liquid, and rarefied forms — at the root of all of them is the atom itself, and jeevan too is the atom. The atom of every species performs definite conduct. Therefore the human being too (who is the combined form of jeevan and body) wishes to live in orderliness with definite conduct. It became natural that the glory of atomic structure comes to be clear only in conduct, and that conduct comes to be in the form of its own activity.

[p. 56]

In the context of atomic composition, this too becomes clear: that atomic particles come to be evidenced only in the coexistence of one another. An atomic particle that remains separate in the limitless void (the omnipresent) does not remain evidencing the intent that is in the form of orderliness; therefore, functioning in a whirling form in the limitless void, it remains in search of another particle. Ultimately, it becomes absorbed into some other atom, or forms a definite atom together with another atomic particle. From this it is learnt that only after the constitution of the atom does the manifestation of orderliness come to be; in the course of orderliness being manifested, the manifestation of conduct comes to be. This itself is clear as the physical, chemical, and jeevan activity-processes.

That atomic structures are based upon one or more particles has already become clear; this too has become clear: that the atom of every species is self-actuated. Their conduct is definite.

The composition of every atom comes to be understood in the form of the nucleus (*madhyansh*) and the particles functioning in the orbits. However many particles are functioning in the orbits, that many particles are also present in the middle; more than that is possible. "Orbits" means the particles circling on all sides of the middle. Such orbits are one or more than one. When these orbits become more than four, the possibility of decreasing, and when fewer than four, of increasing, always-always remains. Such atoms remain present in the forms of their respective species, in various numerical states. Weight remains dependent on the number of nucleuss inherent in these atoms. In this very form, atoms endowed with the inherent-weight recognise one another and gather together — this we call the molecule. Such molecules are found in solid, liquid, and rarefied forms. Solid compositions have already become clear as the forms of large-large compositions like the earth. Liquid substance is considered the initial substance of the chemical world. All these come to be composed by the compound method. In this the human being has no contribution; the human being only studies it. Study is necessary precisely because through it, recognising the innate functional orderliness with these becomes possible. It is only on the basis of chemical substances that the activity-process of the prana cells, in the fruition of the composition-of-nourishment-element and composition-element, comes to be clear.

At the root of the physical-chemical activity-process, it is the atom and the molecule alone that come to be recognised as performing the activity. In this way the molecule, in its own form, works continuously precisely in the form of orderliness. The evidence of working in the form of orderliness is that the onward state of motion in the sequence of orderliness comes to be clear (from its being the present).

[p. 57]

Every atom and molecule is found to be a rung of its own species. What is meant by being a rung is its existent-state, its continuity. Just as two particles are in one existent-state in the form of an atom. This itself is one step. Their having definite conduct is itself their grandeur. This very having of definite conduct becomes the basis of the further step, and remains so. Atoms and molecules with various numerical particles remain with their definite conduct established. This itself is a wondrous self-organised form of orderliness, clear before the human being.

Such physical molecule-atoms, participating in the chemical activity-process, are amenable to study, each in the form of a state of motion. The well-established combining of all these molecules-atoms comes to be clear in the form of large-large compositions like the earth. This is indicative of the fact that however many species' atoms and molecules are necessary before chemical action begins on the earth, that has come to be accomplished by the destiny-borne innate method itself. What is meant by the destiny-borne innate method is the coexistence method itself. Only after there comes to be endowment with the atoms-molecules of all species do the compound propensity and processes come to be accomplished. On this earth the testimony to this is well established.

That all the above manifestations, in the material state, are based upon molecule-atom species, and that the species of molecule-atoms are dependent on the number of atomic particles — this is well established. The changing of the number of particles, and, as a result of the change, the result — such results remain continuously coming to be. The name of this activity-process is absorption-displacement (*prasthapan-visthapan*). In atoms, absorption-displacement remains occurring in order to fulfil the result-oriented intent. In this the intent of the process of being complementary to one another also comes to be accomplished. Before some particle is displaced from any atom, that atom is found to remain excited; it is only as a result of being excited that being displaced from some atom becomes possible. That particle becomes absorbed into some atom. The name of this action is absorption. In this way, after being displaced, the displaced atom comes to be in natural-state motion; the emplacing atom, after absorbing the absorbed particle, having welled up, comes to be in the establishment of natural-state motion. Only in the establishment of natural-state motion does every atom, molecule, and molecular composition come to be clear as accomplishing definite conduct. Definite conduct itself is the evidence of orderliness. In this progression the intent of development remains contained.

## 3.2 Development Progression, Development (विकासक्रम, विकास)

[p. 58]

In the development progression, the molecules, atoms, and molecule-composed compositions of many species are well established. Just as the earth and many planet-globes are present in existence. All these compositions are the present precisely in the forms of solid, liquid, and rarefied. On this earth these three states are well established. Among these, that which is water, in the form of liquid substance, is itself the very first grandeur of compound action. Water is found to become acid and alkali on the basis of the earth's combination. Only after these come to arise up to a definite quantity does, through the combination of cosmic heat, from the influence of the reflection (the basis for recognition in mutuality), substance-composition in the form of nourishment-element and composition-element come to be found in the water. Its root form is called 'algae' (*kaai*). It is right after such algae that compositions composed from the prana cells have already come to be formed by the single-celled, two-celled, and multi-celled method. This exhibits the development progression in composition. This earth has within it small-large compositions in the form of soil, stone, gem, and metal. As the grandeur of compound action, the compositions composed from prana cells are called the plant-order. All these plant-orders are indivisible from the earth; they remain joined with the earth.

From this it is learnt that on the earth, from the combination of diverse substances, the grandeur of compound action comes to be accomplished, and there comes to be a festival of becoming manifest as distinct from its own root form. This itself is chemical resonance (*rasayanik urmi*). We are calling the festival of chemical substances resonance.

In every prana cell, prana sutras are found to be present. In it itself the composition-element and the nourishment-element remain contained in definite quantity, and the composition-method remains inherent. Such prana sutras remain accomplishing the process of accretion within themselves. In them there remains a festival of multiplying sutras like themselves. For this, the availability of chemical substances and the conjunction of definite heat remains necessary. Such prana cells are found to be formed in the marrow-filled fluids in the hollows of the bones of living beings, of the human being, of the body, and this comes to be distributed as per the body's need. Along with this, from the combining of the prana sutras, many sutras and cells come to be formed. As a result such cells, along with chemical substances, keeping their own existence secure in the form of blood, are found functioning in the composition of the body. In the plant-order, in the sap-circulation system, from the activity-process of the leaf and the root there comes to be accumulation of sap,

[p. 59]

as a result of which prana cells keep on forming. In this way that there is development-propensity in the compositions, in the compositions, that remains contained in the prana cells, is well established. The evidence of this is the very diversity in the compositions. The various compositions found in the present are testimony to the development progression in composition. In this progression the grandeur up to human body-composition has already become clear on this earth.

From prana cells, compositions manifest themselves in the forms of small-large plants, trees, creepers, and shrubs, and further, in the forms of the bodies of small-large living beings. The prana cells functioning in the compositions of the plant-order, having developed, express change in the composition-method — which becomes the basis of the compositions of the living-being body-order, that too up to the composition of the human body; this has already become clear. In nature (*prakriti*) four states are perceivable. The material state is in the forms of soil, stone, and so on. The prana state is in the forms of trees-plants, the plant-order. The jeevan state and the knowledge state are perceivable in the combined form of body and jeevan. In the composition progression of the body of the prana state and the jeevan state, there is also found to be the sweat-born world. This comes to be seen: that when the residue of the plant-order — dried leaves and so on — gather, upon getting water and heat, many worms-insects arise of their own accord. This itself becomes manifest further in the form of the egg-born world. By this method these are found establishing propagation. This sweat-born world comes to be seen in the forms of the water-dweller, land-dweller, and sky-dweller. The egg-born world, being powerful, comes to be seen in the form of birds flying up in the sky, and in the forms of large-large water-dwellers and land-dwellers. Such an egg-born world joins to the womb-born world. The womb-born world is a composition developed, within itself, from the egg-born world. After the egg-born and womb-born worlds, the analysis-synthesis of chemical substances, coming to be in the form of the seven metals, has become well established up to the composition of the human body.

From the study of the progression of all these compositions and of the human body, this too is learnt: that in the human body the richness of the medhas (*medhas*) composition has completely and fully become clear. The evidence of this is precisely that in the human being, imaginativeness, freedom-of-action, and its satisfaction, in the form of knowing, believing, recognising, and carrying-out through the medium of the medhas, is a unit worthy of expression. The non-human order is limited up to recognising and carrying-out. The basis of recognising and carrying-out is force-endowment itself. This very energy-endowment, force-endowment, has already come to be evidenced, in the living human being, in the form of knowing, believing, recognising, and carrying-out. This is also testimony to the medhas-system being rich. By the above method the outline of there being development in compositions comes to be known. Before this it has already been made clear that

[p. 60]

it is only in the atom that development comes to be. Such developed atoms are constitutionally complete; this itself is the conscious unit; this itself is jeevan. Jeevan itself, keeping the body alive, evidences understanding.

## 3.3 Development in the Atom (परमाणु में विकास)

Before this, the development progression in compositions has become clear. This development progression has clarified the development progression in composition up to the atom, the molecule, molecule-composed bodies, the plant-order, the animal-body, the human-body composition. The human body is amenable to study as the highest developed composition.

The human body is the highest developed composition precisely because in the human body the medhas composition has already fully developed. From the distant past, the human being has remained desirous of unveiling facts concerning justice, dharma, and truth. In this progression, as many experiments as have taken place, and the need to develop discernment further too, remained. In the course of the attempts, idealist and materialist discernment came into experiment. The fruit-result has already been reckoned within the human family.

The expectation of justice, even today, remains in the human being most of all.

In the above progression, the attempt to understand existence in the form of coexistence, to express it, is presented. On the basis that existence is itself coexistence, the development progression in compositions became clear. At the root of every composition, there being the atom, is clear. That there is development progression in atoms, that there is development, too comes to be understood.

What is meant by development in the atom is the atom's becoming constitutionally complete. In the human being one desire has been present — for the existent-state, grandeur, and its continuity — this is naturally acceptable. That there is decreasing-increasing of particles in atoms, we have already understood in the form of result. The imperishability of the result, the expectation of its continuity, is found in the human being alone in the form of imagination, sense, semblance, and conviction. For this, that there be many attempts is also natural. By the coexistence-oriented method this has become clear: that in the development progression many compositions, states-circumstances have already come to be perceptible. At their root, that the atom alone is functioning, has already become clear. Such a result-engaged atom itself comes to be composed into the constitution-accretion status. For this event, the natural action is the result-method itself.

[p. 61]

In the result-progression, it has become easy to understand atoms in the terms of hungry and undigested (*ajeeran*). Undigested atoms remain in the attempt to expel some of the particles contained within their constitution. Such atoms remain endowed with radiative grandeur. Radiativeness can be understood thus: that in atoms activeness is always-always kept up; in the fruition of activeness, sound, heat, and electricity come to be produced of their own accord. As a result of remaining soaked within the omnipresent substance, they keep energy-endowment and force-endowment maintained. This very force-endowment comes to be reckoned in the form of magnetic force. Therefore, radiative atoms remain expressing undigestedness within their constitution, because from their constitution the attempt to expel some particles remains ever present. In this progression, radiativeness remains produced in such a way that in the undigested atom there is an internal-conjoining of heat toward the nucleuss. In this very progression electricity too remains present. It is as a result of these two processes that radiativeness remains manifest.

In hungry atoms, the possibility of absorbing some more particles into themselves remains present. This is precisely why they have the name "hungry" atoms. Being undigested and hungry in atoms is understood precisely in the sense of the satisfied (*tript*) atoms (the human being is the one who studies it). Such a satisfied atom — that is, the constitutionally satisfied atom — is found to come to be in the jeevan status. As a result, in the constitutionally complete atom, force and power become inexhaustible. What is meant by inexhaustibility is precisely this: that however much one may express or spend, it does not decrease. However much one may express hope, thought, desire, or evidence, it does not decrease at all, and they remain keen. That is why the name "inexhaustible" has been given. Such inexhaustibility every human being can recognise within himself by examination.

The human being alone is a unit in existence that can study both itself and study the entirety too. By virtue of this very glory, it has already been made clear that the human being is, in the form of its own status-establishment, in the knowledge state. Every man-woman has equal grandeur in the knowledge state. This itself is the formula of mutual equality. To let this formula be forgotten is itself indicative of delusion.

In this way, that jeevan is, within itself, in the form of a constitutionally complete atom, and is in the form of conscious activity-process, and comes to be evidenced through the human body in human tradition — this is well established. That in order to keep this grandeur-establishment maintained, propensities are engaged in the task of studying every human being's being established, and being disestablished, as fundamental necessity, of understanding it, and evidencing it — this too is clear. In this way the destination of development in the atom is precisely the constitutionally complete status.

[p. 62]

By the above method it has become well established that at the root of physical action, chemical action, and jeevan action, it is the atom alone that is present. Jeevan is a constitutionally complete atom, and, participating in physical and chemical action, the atom is constitution-oriented. From constitution-orientation, weight bondage and molecular bondage come to be found. Whereas the jeevan atom is found to remain free of weight bondage and molecular bondage. The evidence of this is precisely that the hope, thought, desire, resolve, and evidences inherent in every human being cannot be reckoned or enumerated on the physical scale. The physical scale is one means for recognising weight. From this testimony this too is clear: that it is only the physical atom that, passing through the development progression, comes to be understood by the human being as accomplishing the event of transition into the constitution-complete status. By the second method, the atom itself, in the constitution-oriented status, is inert (*jad*), and in the constitutionally complete status, is conscious. Without the development progression, the study of development does not happen at all. The conscious atom (which is jeevan itself) is found, in the form of tradition, studying and evidencing development, the developed status, the awakened progression, and awakening. On the basis of this fact, that the orderliness of awakening coming to be evidenced within human tradition — that is, the orderliness of human tradition coming to be grand through awakening — is formed by the destiny method. What is meant by the destiny method is the existence-grandeur method that is in the form of coexistence, its process, fruit, result, and its continuity.

Development in the atom is in the constitution-complete status, or in the form of the constitutionally complete atom. The human being studies "is". What is meant by study is coming to recognise, in existence, the objects indicated by words, through remembrance in the light of experience, from the meanings. In this way, the remembrance of words, and the objects indicated by the meanings of words coming to be clear in existence, is itself awakening. What is meant by clarity is precisely experience within oneself, and being evidenced in action-behaviour.

What development in the atom means, this became clear: that the atom comes to be in the constitution-complete status, and that the constitutionally complete atom is awakened — this itself is destiny. This comes to be evidenced only within human tradition. For this, the body-composition worthy of expressing it has already been formed. In order to evidence its awakening through this body, the human being made experiments of various kinds. But, on the basis that the intent of development in the atom is the constitutionally complete atom did not come to be explained in tradition, the study of the jeevan status could not come to be. The study of the jeevan status comes to be found only in the light of the awakening that occurs in jeevan. That is why speaking of it here has been natural.

[p. 63]

To the extent that the constitutionally complete atom, through delusion, understands the body to be jeevan, it does not remain capable of understanding development in the atom. In the physical, chemical development progression, it is precisely in the course of evidencing jeevan and jeevan-awakening development-fully in the human body and in the atom that awakening comes to be evidenced within human tradition. What the evidence of awakening means is precisely coming to be evidenced justly, resolution-fully, with actuality, with reality, and with truth. What being evidenced means is precisely being, in tradition. In the course of the above five faculties coming to be evidenced, that they naturally come to be evidenced from generation to generation — this itself is awakened tradition.

In the human being this grandeur comes to be seen: that he is found making the research-investigation that has been evidenced as useful, well-utilised, and application-worthy, come to be universalised through education, through study, through practice.

By the above method, however much experiment has so far taken place in giving form to the imaginary, all of that has been universalised. And however much experiment has taken place for mental well-being (*manah-svasthata*), it has not been universalised. Whereas the very definition of the human being is: the one who gives form to the imaginary, the one who presents the evidence of mental well-being (the idealist certainly exists).

Development-completeness, in the course of development in the atom, remained a transition event. What is meant by transition-ness is precisely: not coming again into the previous state. Freedom from the result means freedom from the event of some particles decreasing, increasing in the atom.

By the coexistence-oriented method, the human being is grand in the combined form of body and jeevan. Jeevan-awakening itself is the evidence of mental well-being. That which comes to be accomplished only within jeevan, that which comes to be grand — by the experience-based method — the perception of experiences in the intellect (*buddhi*), in the contemplative mind (*chitt*), justly, dharma-fully, truth-fully, in the appropriate weighing-action propensity (in reflection-action), and the savouring-action of values in the mind (*mann*) — is found to come to be. The savouring-action of values comes to be possible in action-behaviour only when the recognition of relationships remains acceptable. Only after recognising relationships does one come to carry out values.

Along with relationships, values; along with values, the dedication-surrender of objects; along with the dedication-surrender of objects, usefulness, well-utilisation, application-worthiness — this is found to come to be evidenced, that is, to come to be grand. This itself is the intent of development in the atom and of awakening. Only the human being is a unit worthy of presenting the evidence of awakening.
