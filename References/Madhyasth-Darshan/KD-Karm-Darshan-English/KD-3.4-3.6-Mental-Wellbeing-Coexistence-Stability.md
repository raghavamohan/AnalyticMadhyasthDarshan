Awakening's entire form is nothing but knowing, believing, recognising, and carrying out. The material state, the prana state, and the jeevan state — recognising and carrying out all of these, these units evidence their own conduct in the form of participation in orderliness-with-inherence (*tva*) and in the overall orderliness. Whereas in the human being, along with recognising and carrying out, knowing and believing have remained a necessary process as well. In the atom, as the outcome of development progression and development, physical, chemical, and jeevan activity is found to occur. In this way, development and awakening in the atom are completed in these three stages — constitutional completeness, activity-completeness, conduct-completeness. This itself is the meaning of development in the atom.

## 3.4 The Nature of Mental Well-Being (मनःस्वस्थता का स्वरूप)

In the sequence of studying physical, chemical, and jeevan activities, it is necessary that the study of the human being be complete. Tireless effort has already been made on this issue in the past. In the human context, whatever study was carried out without recognising human-ness (*manavatva*) — in that, the study of physical and chemical activity too remained incomplete. In the same way, however much study of physical and chemical activities has taken place, on that basis too the understanding of the human being could not be completed. In both ways this can be understood thus: on the basis of however much of the thread of physical, chemical activity is available to the human being so far, on that basis the human being's thread and

[p. 65]

explanation does not occur. For this reason we humans have not arrived at a position of trusting humans. We, humans living in every country, do trust physical activity, whereas trusting human activity has not yet come about.

Because of this problem, it has not so far been possible to arrive at a determination regarding the human being's human-ness, the human being's conduct, the human being's mentality, the human being's nature. By way of indirect statement, all governing institutions, educational institutions, religious-cultural institutions, technological institutions, and trading institutions keep saying this in many ways. It is precisely the emptiness at this site that has been the reason for the need to understand development and awakening.

This point has already been made clear: that it is the atom alone that participates in physical, chemical, and jeevan activity. It has already been made clear that at the root of jeevan activity is the presence of the constitutionally complete atom. Whether it be physical activity, chemical activity, or jeevan activity — it is on the basis of the conduct of each of these three modes that we are able to determine their recognition, the recognition of their diversity, and the recognition of their unity. We determine the recognition of iron on the basis of iron's forms of conduct. In the same way, we recognise soil, stone, metal, donkey, horse, cat, dog, ant, and elephant, each on the basis of their own respective forms of conduct, and, along with this, we are also able to determine what conduct and behaviour humans are to have toward them.

It is clear that, in existence, the human being is the combined form of jeevan activity and physical-chemical activity (the body). The atom, in its constitutionally complete condition, carries out jeevan activity. It is jeevan alone that expresses, communicates, and manifests hope, thought, desire, resolve (resoluteness, *ṛtambharā*), and evidence (realisation). The testing of this has to be carried out by every human being, both within himself and within every other human being. For this reason the human being is an object worthy of study in the human being, by the human being, for the human being.

Mental well-being can be called happiness. This is the realisation and the evidence of resolution. The evidence of mental well-being — that is, in the tradition of behaviour — has been found to occur in the form of evidence being resolution. Along with this, the human goal has been recognised in the form of resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence, and every man and woman can have the realisation of this. It has also been found that resolution alone, in realisation, is happiness. The evidence of happiness is resolution in behaviour. In this way resolution came to be anchored. It became clear that resolution is the expression, communication, and manifestation of jeevan activity, or of mental well-being, or of jeevan awakening.

[p. 66]

Mental well-being is a most necessary issue in, from, and for human tradition. Only with mental well-being does the human goal come to be evidenced. In the process of the human goal becoming meaningful, the human being becomes ordered within himself, and participates in orderliness.

The principal reason that orderliness in the human being is not confined, in itself, to chemical-physical activity, is the presence of jeevan activity in the human being. Every human being is indeed the testimony of jeevan activity. Although jeevan activity does occur in animals as well, in animals it is confined to acting in accordance with the jeevan-body, because every animal keeps evidencing its tendency and its actions by the hereditary method alone. This has been seen to remain confined to the meaning of sensations. Whereas in the human being, the effect of both jeevan cognisance (knowing, believing) and sensitivity (recognising, carrying out) is seen. In the animal order only sensations have come to be evidenced, whereas in human tradition both cognisance and sensitivity come to be evidenced, and every awakened human being keeps sensations under control by means of cognisance. This is the fundamental distinctiveness of the awakened, or natural, human being. Therefore it is precisely this — that sensations be controlled, balanced, and purposeful by means of cognisance — that is the human being's fundamental distinctiveness. In this sense, the whole of purpose comes to be found as the evidence of cognisance.

The definition of the human being is meaningful in the sense of giving concrete form to ideas (*manakar*) and evidencing mental well-being. This is accepted in, from, and for every human being. The human being does indeed want to have a healthy mind (*manas*), and does keep on giving concrete form to ideas. This can be tested in every country and time. In this way it is self-evident that the human being, as the combined manifestation of chemical-physical and jeevan activity, is distinct from other kinds of nature — such as animal nature, plant nature, and mineral nature.

According to coexistence-ist thought, according to knowledge, wisdom, and science, it is necessary for all humankind to adopt education-and-culture in the sense of the human goal. In this, it is necessary that humane conduct remain clear in the sense of the human goal, because it is only on the basis of conduct that the fulfilment of the goal of each of these states becomes intelligible. For instance, in the material state, on the basis of the outcome for the whole substance, one gets to see conduct acting toward the goal in the form of its actual condition (*yatha-sthiti*). In the same way, in the prana state and the jeevan state too, all the units at work are seen acting for the sake of the goal of each of those states — for example, the prana state is seen making the journey, in the sense of existence together with growth (*pushti*), from seed to tree and from tree to seed. In this

[p. 67]

way the entire animal order is seen acting, together with existence and growth, in the hope of living, by the hereditary method. Having seen such entirely clear conditions, it also remained necessary that human conduct be in the sense of evidencing happiness together with existence, growth, and hope — and it is for this very reason that the evidencing of mental well-being remained natural.

Human conduct is found to become clear in the combined form of value, character, and ethics. Only on the basis of fundamental distinctiveness does the evaluation of values come to be; in the human being, the carrying-out of relationships, its continuity, and its acceptance, is itself a fundamental distinctiveness. In this very sense, the recognition of relationships, the carrying-out of values, evaluation, and mutual two-sided satisfaction, or mutual satisfaction, are found to occur. This is what is meant by value and fundamental distinctiveness. The second part of conduct is character, which is found to become entirely clear in the form of fidelity to one's own wealth, one's own wife, one's own husband, and compassionate work-behaviour, by which the human being's fundamental character, or universal character, becomes entirely clear. In the third mode, ethics is found, in the human being, to be the expectation of, and the evidence for, moral policy and the state's economic policy. As evidence, every awakened man and woman is found presenting himself in the form of the protection and proper use of the wealth that is body, mind, and money. Of these, the protective methods are known by the name of statecraft; the methods of proper use are known by the name of moral policy; and production beyond the family's need is known by the name of economic policy.

By the coexistence-ist method, every human being is a unit worthy of participating, together with human-ness, in orderliness and in the overall orderliness. In this, the principal issue is just this: there is need to accept, through study, that one's own self, for one's own self, is in the combined form of chemical, physical, and jeevan activity. It is extremely necessary that the glory of jeevan activity, and its necessity within human tradition, remain kept in mind. Only then is the human being found ready for research. Such readiness comes to be proposed, by the natural method of awakening, in the sense of universal wellbeing. Only then does the situation become clear of all humankind expressing itself with resolution, and of every family becoming resolved and happy through understanding; as a result, the good fortune of prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence being brought to bear comes about — this is the principal point. This is also the evidence of universal wellbeing, because it is only with resolution and prosperity that the human being experiences happiness and peace. In this same sequence, contentment and joy, together with resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence, are found to be entirely meaningful in themselves. In this way, once the human goal has become meaningful, the jeevan goal (happiness, peace, contentment, joy) does indeed become meaningful. Jeevan

[p. 68]

goal and the human goal becoming meaningful is itself the universality of study and teaching. With such a goal, human tradition itself becomes, in itself, the thread and the explanation for recognising oneself and for recognising the whole of humankind. As evidence, it is found to be an explanation; as understanding, it is found to be a thread. This is found, by the natural method of destiny, to remain appropriate. What is meant by the method of destiny is development progression, development, the awakening sequence, and the tradition of natural evidence for awakening. By the second method, it is in the form of the combined expression, communication, and manifestation of the physical-chemical composition, the body, and jeevan activity.

The human goal — evidencing resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence naturally, and, on that basis, the jeevan goal (mental well-being) — the evidence of this, in the sense of making happiness, peace, contentment, and joy meaningful, is precisely why the necessity of human education-and-culture has remained in place for ever and ever. The success of this alone is the good fortune of the human family.

### The Meaningful Evidence of the Human Family and of Chemical-Physical Activity (मानव कुल और रासायनिक-भौतिक क्रियाकलाप का सार्थक प्रमाण)

The entire substance of education lies, within coexistence-as-existence, precisely in the form of chemical, physical, and jeevan activity. Of these, in their indivisible form, is the entire activity, behaviour, thinking, and understanding of human tradition. It is in the sense of understanding alone that every human being has to carry out study. Understanding, in itself, is found evidenced in the forms of knowing, believing, recognising, and carrying out. It is in this very sense that the whole of study is found to be meaningful.

In existence, the whole of units is indeed reflected in the forms of chemical, physical, and jeevan activity. From the constitution-forming atom up to the molecule, the molecule-composed body, the prana-cell, the plant order, and the animal order — all of these, remaining within their own orderliness, are found intelligible as they express their own determinate conduct. Of such evidences, this earth is the greatest evidence. The earth functions in the form of an orderliness. It is precisely the evidence of functioning in the form of an orderliness that, on this earth, chemical-physical and jeevan activity is manifest in the forms of the material state, the prana state, the jeevan state, and the knowledge state. What greater testimony could there be than this? On the basis of this testimony — that is, according to the determinate activity of the material, prana, and jeevan states — the human being can trust in his own being within orderliness.

[p. 69]

The thread and the explanation for the human being to live, by the natural method of existence-as-coexistence, in the form of orderliness of and by himself, comes to be understood by the self-motivated method.

By the coexistence-ist method it becomes possible to recognise the whole of humankind as one undivided society (*akhand samaj*). It is the complete understanding of the mentality of undivided society, in the form of knowledge, science, and wisdom, that keeps the weave of universal orderliness clear. It is only in the sense of such universal wellbeing that the human being came to be found meaningful in the form of orderliness. Therefore the accomplishment of universal orderliness is, for the human family, the ultimate glory, the thread, and the explanation. In this way it is found that it is precisely with human consciousness that the human family's own tendency to participate in universal orderliness, in undivided society, comes to be self-motivated. The sole reason for this is that, by the coexistence-ist method, the human being recognises himself.

In the conduct of humankind, dharma predominates; in animal species, disposition predominates; in plants, property predominates; in material things, form predominates. It has already become necessary that human dharma, in itself, evidence resolution on the basis of happiness. All-round, resolution-complete education-and-culture is itself the tradition and the source for this. This itself is awakened tradition.

## 3.5 Coexistence Is Stable; Development and Awakening Are Certain (सहअस्तित्व स्थिर है, विकास और जागृति निश्चित है)

Existence-as-coexistence is stable. It is in coexistence alone that development and awakening are certain. We human beings on this earth have already arrived at a condition fit for studying this fact. The seer, doer, and enjoyer of universal wellbeing, and the bearer and carrier of the knowledge, science, and wisdom obtained at its root from education-and-culture, is the awakened human being alone. This fact is found, in itself, to be well understood. It is only with coexistence-ist philosophy, knowledge, science, and wisdom that every human being comes to evidence himself, in the sequence of the event of universal wellbeing, naturally in the form of orderliness, and comes to participate in the overall orderliness. This itself is the reach and the manifest form of the programme of universal wellbeing. For this — that is, for universal knowledge, science, and wisdom — study alone is the one and only source. The nature of knowledge and science, in itself, is complete knowledge only when it comprises: the knowledge of the holistic view of existence-as-coexistence, the knowledge of jeevan in the form of conscious nature, the knowledge of activity-behaviour in the form of humane conduct, and the knowledge of participating in the overall orderliness. Of these, existence-as-coexistence comes to be studied in four states and in four categories (*pad*). Its root form, in the pervasive substance, is that each and every single substance

[p. 70]

remains submerged, saturated, and enclosed within the whole. This alone is the ever-present form of coexistence. This does not, in itself, undergo decrease or increase; it does not expand or contract; in every circumstance, its being and remaining continue precisely according to the manifestation of development and awakening. In the mutuality of units, at certain good distances, there is indeed an arrangement in every unit for natural-state motion to be evidenced. Because it has been found intelligible that it is only when every atomic particle is in a position to recognise other atomic particles that determinate conduct comes to be fulfilled as the outcome of atomic constitution. It is precisely determinate conduct that bears the name natural-state motion. Therefore, in the establishment of natural-state motion, every unit, or the whole of units, is found seen and understood as participating in orderliness-with-inherence and in the overall orderliness. This same state is also disclosed from the study of oneself. In this sense, development and awakening are certain.

We human beings have the tendency to participate, together with human-ness, in orderliness and in the overall orderliness only in natural-state motion — that is, only after the accomplishment of natural-state motion, with awakening. Such natural-state motion is found to occur only after awakening. Awakening is evidenced only when the human being becomes accomplished in understanding, honesty, and responsibility, as a result of which he evidences, with participation, his own glory, his actual condition. In the second stage it also became understood that the human being alone is fit to be the one who understands. Only after understanding stability and certainty does the human being himself become capable of being honest and responsible in respect of certainty and stability. As a result he is able to be evidenced with participation. Therefore it is necessary for the human being to be understanding; the expectation of this — that is, the expectation of understanding — is found to be present in all humankind.

## 3.6 The Stability and Certainty of Realisation and Awakening (अनुभव और जागृति की स्थिरता और निश्चयता)

Certainty and stability have indeed remained formed in all humankind in the form of an abiding aspiration (*chira-akanksha*). Existence-as-coexistence is the basis of knowledge and acceptance. This is a fact that comes to be known in all humankind through survey. This becomes clear also from examining oneself. All of us have continued to accept stability and certainty alone, and not instability and uncertainty. It is natural for certainty to occur only on the natural basis of stability. The certainty natural to development and awakening is evidenced, in all humankind, in the form of jeevan activity. It is natural for all humankind to study the five activities of jeevan in the form of state, and five activities in the form of motion. In carrying out this study

[p. 71]

too, it is the human being alone who is engaged. It is the human being, too, who evidences after study, and it is the human being, too, who lives in evidenced form. In this way the human being alone is the evidence of awakening. Jeevan activity is found, in the form of motion, to occur in all humankind in the forms of selection, analysis, desire, resoluteness, and evidence — this itself is projection. Along with this, the five activities of state are found in the forms of tasting, comparison, contemplation, enlightenment, and the activity of realisation. Only then does human tradition come to evidence the evidence of realisation. The five activities of state of jeevan are expressed as force, and the five activities of motion are expressed as power.

The selection made by awakened jeevan in the sense of relationships, their being carried out, and their purposes, is hope — the hope of living with happiness; this is the first power of jeevan. Second: in accordance with hope — that is, analysis being accomplished on the basis of the hopes natural to awakened jeevan — this itself is thought. Third: the continued forming of images in accordance with the analysis of awakened jeevan is itself desire. Fourth: the putting-to-purpose and putting-into-practice of the justice, dharma, and truthfulness of awakened jeevan is resoluteness (*ṛtambharā*). Fifth: the designation of authenticity given to the evidences natural to realisation. In this way the human being comes to understand the presence of five powers. In the same way, five forces too have their authentication —

1. In awakened jeevan, the activity that is realisation and its continuity is *atma*.
2. The activity that gives right enlightenment of realisation as it actually is — that is, that fully accepts it — is *buddhi*.
3. The activity that maintains the continuity of accepting justice, dharma, and truth in accordance with realisation, namely contemplation, is *chitta*.
4. The activity that compares, by the realisation-based method, the justice, dharma, and truth that have been directly recognised, is *vritti*.
5. The activity that, in accordance with comparison, expects, or tastes, the entirety of value, character, and ethics, is *mun* (mind).

In this way, the clarification of the five forces and the five powers is given in the form of the activity that occurs in jeevan.

In the awakened human being, evidence, or the entirety of evidence, within existence-as-coexistence, is nothing but the light of realisation. In the light of realisation, no substance other than evidence remains contained at all. For this reason evidences are also indicated by the names supreme, sacred, and complete. In the second stage, the supreme acceptance of the evidences of realisation is in resolves; the forming of images of resolve is in desires; and of such forming of images —

[p. 72]

of analysis — is in thoughts. It is intelligible that the selection activity of such thoughts, by the tasting-based method, comes to be accomplished in hopes. By the realisation-based method, certainty becomes clear across all ten activities.

In the discipline of the accomplishment of the knowledge of jeevan, this is a matter fit for study. It is meaningful that the activities of state be realisation-directed and be expressed by the realisation-based method, and be evidenced by the realisation-based method. There is no third meaningful process or method in existence, in, from, or for human tradition. By the natural method of the human being, this alone is found, on the basis of necessity, to be the ultimate (*param*). The human goal's becoming meaningful is itself what comes to be recognised, and understood, as the ultimate necessity. As a result, the human being is also to be seen becoming earnestly active (*sachesht*).

That the human goal is resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence — this is found to be evidenced only by the realisation-based method. Realisation is the evidence and the activity of glory natural to jeevan. It is only on this basis that it is fitting for the tradition of evidence to be fulfilled within the human family. In this same sequence, the good fortune of evidencing certainty for us becomes clear. This is fitting for every man and woman.

Realisation alone is the ultimate expression, communication, and manifestation of awakening and of jeevan. What is meant by ultimate is that it is never more than realisation; jeevan remains incomplete in anything less than that. It is found that satiation (*tripti*) in jeevan comes about through realisation. Jeevan-satiation, in itself, is what comes to be recognised, in human tradition, in the forms of happiness, peace, and contentment, on the basis of resolution, prosperity, and fearlessness. In coexistence, realisation alone is the ultimate; this itself is joy. The evidence of this is ultimate in, from, and for human tradition. The evaluation-system of these — that is, of the human goal — remains contained within orderliness; this itself is the justice-and-security system. By the method of justice and security, the human being is found to remain trusting and assured in every state and motion. As a result, the path remains cleared for the entire glory natural to jeevan to be evidenced within human tradition. This itself is awakened tradition. The evidence of jeevan-satiation is the human being's being a participant in undivided society, in universal orderliness. It remains natural that, once there is orderliness within human tradition, it is by the method of orderliness alone that resolution, prosperity, fearlessness, and coexistence come to be evidenced. This itself is the evidence of awakening, and the evidence of the whole of human jeevan as well. It is the ultimate glory of human tradition. Orderliness in the form of purpose is indeed the highest attainment of human tradition. In these very attainments, stability and certainty are clear.
