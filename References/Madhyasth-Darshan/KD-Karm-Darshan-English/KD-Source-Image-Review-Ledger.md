# KD source-image review ledger

This ledger records direct comparisons between the English translation and the rendered Hindi source pages in `_page-images/`. It supplements the corpus-wide terminology checks; it is not generated from the Hindi PDF's corrupt embedded text layer.

## Review method

- Read the Hindi from the rendered source-page image.
- Compare logical sequence, negation, enumeration, agency, and technical terms with the English page.
- Revise only where the Hindi supports the change.
- Re-run the terminology guardrail and rebuild both PDFs after accepted revisions.

## Directly reviewed pages

The editorial pilot directly reviewed printed pages **8, 15, 31, 39, 43, 70, 102, 119, 133, and 147**.

The subsequent terminology and high-density fidelity pass directly reviewed printed pages **2, 4, 14, 19, 21–22, 31, 56, 63, 68, 70, 73, 76–77, 86, 88, 90, 99–100, 105–112, 123–125, 130, and 134**. These pages cover the Gratitude and Foreword sections and the principal high-risk passages on the contextual senses of *artha*, jeevan-cloud and *pranavayu*, development and earth-balance, measure, property/essential nature/dharma, strength–power, projection–reflection, electricity, distance, and time.

Clear corrections from this pass include **worldly grandeur** for दुनियावी वैभव, **measure** for मात्रा while retaining **quantity** for राशि, **excitation / excited** for आवेश / आवेशित, *pranavayu* for प्राण वायु, and the restored **realisation-based** faculty sequence on printed page 63. Mechanical English syntax was also revised where the Hindi meaning remained unchanged.

## Scope boundary

The full translated body has received a chapter-by-chapter editorial pass and deterministic terminology checks. The page list above identifies passages that have additionally received sentence-level source-image comparison; it should not be read as a claim that every sentence on all 153 printed body pages has received an independent second source-image review.

The still-open lexical items are **विन्यास**, **कासा–आकूति–मेधा**, and **ता-त्रय**. Their transliterations or contextual glosses remain provisional and are tracked in `KD-Glossary-Additions.md` and `KD-Rakesh-Gupta-Alignment-Audit.md`.
