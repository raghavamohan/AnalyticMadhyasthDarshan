# Ontology review implementation, 20 September 2026

This records the decisions on the supplied review of Section 1 and Editorial Notes in [The Ontology of Coexistence](../Studies/The-Ontology-of-Coexistence/The-Ontology-of-Coexistence.md). The study itself contains the revised argument, not this review record. Project follow-up work belongs only in [PENDING.md](../PENDING.md).

## Review decisions

| ID | Review intent | Disposition and implementation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Introduce foundations before sentient faculties and values. | Adopt. Time and causation now follow the four aspects (§1.4); composition follows (§1.5), then orders and conformance (§1.6). Constitutional completeness (§1.7), faculties (§1.8), harmonies (§1.9), and karma (§1.10) precede the fuller awakening account (§1.11), planes (§1.12), and values/evaluation (§1.13). |
| R2 | Give conformance one complete explanation. | Adopt. The four modes are defined together in §1.6.1. The progression table refers back to them. Later applications retain the terms when needed. The review's final checklist says §1.11, but its roadmap places the combined orders/conformance account at §1.6; the latter fits the intended teaching order. |
| R3 | Preserve the verified doctrinal strengths. | Adopt. Retain two co-eternal realities, the cumulative dharmas, distinct compositional and atomic development, five faculties and ten activities, thirty values, and six evaluative perspectives. A clearer order does not replace these connections. The review incorrectly expands AVD as Avartansheel Arthashastra; the study's cited AVD is *Adhyatmvad*. |
| R4 | Clarify all-unit versus human presentation, communication, and expression. | Adopt in part. SB PDF p.179 and MVD pp.109–110 support the all-unit scope; PS printed pp.22,119,206 supplies the fuller human meanings. §§1.3 and 1.14 make the distinction explicit. Avoid “existential transmissions”: SB pp.251–252 distinguishes imaging from motion or pressure and supplies no transmission mechanism. |
| R5 | Explain why restricted deliberation is called half an activity. | Adopt in part. §1.8 names the six perspectives and explains the restriction to the first three (MVD p.67; JV p.73). Retain the distinction between all ten inherent activities and four-and-a-half effectively organised around bodily sensitivity. Decline “literally half-operational” as a measured ratio: the sources supply no quantitative measure of cognitive capacity. |
| R6 | Simplify saturation's abstract nominalisations. | Adopt. Explain being soaked, submerged, and surrounded through energy/force, activity/mutual recognition, and regulation. Preserve the overlapping SB p.57 / PS p.114 formulations in Editorial Notes. |
| R7 | Simplify permeability and transparency. | Adopt in part. §1.1 states permeation in units and transparency in mutuality (MVD p.32; PS p.114). Decline the proposed active “connects all units across empty space,” which adds a mechanism to actionless Omnipresence. |
| R8 | Simplify guna, svabhav, and dharma. | Adopt in part. Explain relative powers and their characteristic usefulness in ordinary language. Retain dharma as inseparable innateness, supported by MVD pp.47,336; “intrinsic purpose” is not an equivalent definition across all orders. |
| R9 | Reduce defensive hedging in exposition. | Adopt in part. Replace repeated disclaimers with affirmative accounts in the opening sections and karma discussion; retain material distinctions and evidential limits. Translation choices and analogy limits remain in Editorial Notes; criticisms remain in §6. |
| R10 | Add a physical illustration of saturation. | Adopt in part. A cloth immersed in water illustrates the three everyday relations. It is explicitly this study's illustration, not an attributed primary example. Its scope is limited: water is bounded matter, while saturation in formless Omnipresence is universal and inseparable. |
| R11 | Ground the jeevan–body distinction in an intuitive example. | Adopt. §1.8 briefly uses driver/vehicle while retaining the biological medium and its constraints. JV p.94 supports the body as vessel and driven medium; MVD pp.83,92–93 supplies the nervous-system context. |
| R12 | Reconcile harmony between faculties with its location at one faculty. | Adopt. §§1.9 and 1.13.1 consistently identify adjacent-faculty harmony borne at the outer faculty. JV p.138 and MVD p.208 give the pairs; MVD pp.328,331,333,346 locate the activities. Realisation at atma remains ultimate bliss, distinguished from buddhi–atma harmony as bliss. The figure now observes the same distinction. |
| R13 | Consolidate seer–doer–enjoyer. | Adopt. §1.8 gives the complete account of the same jeevan knowing, acting, and undergoing results. Awakening sections apply that account without repeating the full definition. |
| R14 | Distinguish art as object value from skilful expression. | Adopt. §1.13.1 identifies usefulness-supporting beauty/convenience as kala-mulya; §1.14 treats kala as meaningful expressive activity. PS printed p.56 and JV p.138 support both connected roles. |
| R15 | Consolidate Editorial Notes without losing necessary scholarship. | Adopt in part. Seven topic headings replace sixteen headings plus the unheaded scope paragraphs. Retain consequential translation and scope decisions, including gyan, which the review's proposed seven-heading list did not explicitly place. See the disposition table below. |
| R16 | Move ordinary science definitions out of Editorial Notes. | Adopt. Existing glossary entries already define physicalism, methodological naturalism, scientific realism, and instrumentalism/constructive empiricism. Add a concise science entry and preserve the classification point about competing interpretations without duplicating those definitions. |
| R17 | Synchronise timestamps, references, catalog, and outputs. | Adopt. Remap references by meaning, including ranges split by the new order, related studies, companion sources, SVGs, decks, and presenter notes. Refresh each changed canonical source's timestamp, render affected artifacts with the repository pipelines, and run shared finalisation and checks. |

## Source and scope checks

The contested wording was checked directly against hash-verified SB, MVD, and JV PDFs through the repository reference store, and legible PS page images for printed pp.22,56,114,119,206. The review evaluates source support within the darshan; neither successful citation checks nor successful rendering independently establishes its metaphysical claims. Existing comparative sections were reread for consistency and their section references updated; this revision does not claim a fresh audit of every scientific or comparative source.

The canonical study remains Released. The work changes its explanation and organisation, not its lifecycle status. Generated PDFs remain local verification outputs; publication follows the protected workflow after merge.
