# Weekly YouTube channel

Status: Planned 23 September 2026. The channel does not exist yet. Open items:
[PENDING.md](../PENDING.md#youtube).

## Objective

Publish one video a week that takes a current-affairs hook and states one
Madhyasth Darshan claim, then sends the viewer to the study that carries the
argument.

The news is the door. The video asks one question, states one claim, and gives
one lived example. It does not recap the week, preach, or take a party side.

## Kept separate

Three public products stay distinct:

| Product | Role | Home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly video | Current affairs as the way into one claim | YouTube; later a thin page on coexistentialism.org |
| Start here audio | 4–5 minute introduction to a core study | [docs/start-here-audio-plan.md](start-here-audio-plan.md); `AUD-*` |
| Studies and slides | The argument, citations, and teaching decks | analyticmadhyasthdarshan.org |

A weekly episode is not a catalog study, a deck, or a PDF. Publishing one does
not refresh a study's `**Edited on:**` line or its catalog timestamp. Edit a
study only when the video has exposed a real error in that study, and then
follow the normal study workflow.

This channel is independent work. It is not a publication of Divya Path
Sansthan. Official Madhyasth Darshan texts and lectures remain at
[madhyasth.org](https://www.madhyasth.org/).

## Episode shape

Target 8–12 minutes. One primary study. One claim.

| Approximate position | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | The news hook, in a few sentences. Name the event; do not brief the week. |
| The usual answer | The public frame (safety vs speed, emissions vs finance, exams vs jobs, and so on). |
| The question | The Madhyasth question that frame does not ask. |
| The claim | One claim from the primary study, with one lived example. |
| Close | What would change if the claim were true. Speak `coexistentialism.org`. The study URL goes in the description. |

**Description block**, in this order:

1. One-sentence claim.
2. **Read the study** — the canonical study URL on analyticmadhyasthdarshan.org.
3. Optional second link: slides, or one related study.
4. **Start here:** `https://analyticmadhyasthdarshan.org/Studies/index.html#start-here`
5. The independence line from the About text.

Use the same three links in the end screen and the pinned comment. Chapter
titles may follow the study's section numbers. YouTube comments stay on
YouTube. Link Discuss only when the episode is inviting inquiry into that
study's argument.

If the fitting study is still unwritten, say so. Point to Start here or that
study's Discuss page. Do not present the video as the study.

## Publishing loop

1. Choose the hook and the one primary study from the spine.
2. Write the script from that study's claim. Keep qualifications the study
   keeps. Do not add a new doctrine in the video.
3. Record and publish with the description block above.
4. Leave study markdown, catalogs, and PDFs unchanged unless the video exposed
   an error in the study.
5. After four episodes are public, `YT-03` may start. Until then,
   coexistentialism.org continues to redirect to the catalog, which is a
   sufficient channel website.

## Site boundary

`YT-03` is the only site work in this plan. When its gate is met:

- Replace only the coexistentialism.org homepage with one screen: this week's
  video, **Read the study** (analyticmadhyasthdarshan.org), **Start here**, and
  the independence line. No autoplay.
- Keep every study, PDF, slide, and Discuss path on the AMD canonical URL.
  coexistentialism.org may redirect `/start` and `/study/<slug>` to those URLs.
  It does not serve a second reader.
- On the AMD catalog, a cited study may show one **Watch** link to the latest
  related episode. Hide it when that study has no episode. Do not iframe
  YouTube inside the reader, the PDF, or Start here.
- Keep episode records in a small registry in git (YouTube id, date, title,
  primary slug, optional extra slugs, one-line claim), on the same pattern as
  `Scripts/presentation-pipeline.json`. Do not call the YouTube API on page
  load, and do not send video through the PDF pipeline.
- Do not add episodes as catalog rows, and do not precache video in the
  service worker.
