## Editorial choices

Retains the study's stated inclination towards irreducible sentience while giving
the physicalist alternative substantive space. Detailed anatomical claims,
dream evidence, infancy studies, the four orders, faculty enumerations, and
post-death studies are omitted to keep the introduction within five minutes.
The Ontology episode can develop the wider account of nature. This introduction
cannot reproduce every argument or critical qualification in the study.

Revision 2 replaced the meal-and-friendship opener with the study's own opening sentence, added the definition of "just material", added Nagraj's diagnosis of matter-centred and mystery-centred inquiry (§1.1), and replaced the abstract statement that brain changes alter motivation with the study's concrete injury case (§2.2.1). Paid for by trimming the duplicated continuous-happiness point in the body/jeevan paragraph and the closing invitation. No study follow-up is required; the transcript now tracks §§1.1 and 2.2.1 more closely.

Revision 3 preserves the broadened opener and the three-way comparison while
restoring §4.4's substantive argument about understanding reasons and revising
aims. The business-and-water illustration replaces the earlier generic gain
example. Justice receives a brief positive explanation so fulfilment cannot be
heard as private contentment alone. Nagraj's diagnosis remains attributed to him,
with “adequately” preserving the source's scope.

The injury account now says “were less willing than comparison groups”, replacing
“became less willing” and “largely unchanged”, which could suggest a longitudinal
measurement. The physicalist reply about different ways of knowing the same
reality is now explicit. Advaita retains responsibility within ordinary life
alongside its non-dual conclusion. Constitutional completeness is explained in
plain words, and the separate evidential step from irreducibility to a distinct
bearer is stated. The closing invitation includes possible counterevidence.
Trimming repeated statements of the comparison and of physicalism's acceptance
of love and responsibility creates room for these additions. No study edits
were required.
