## Editorial choices

Centres the episode on understanding becoming conduct and returning through evaluation. Keeps the detailed jeevan faculty model and the several epistemic triads in the parent study.

Revision 2 replaced the family food decision with the three practices (§1.6), which are the study's own account of how a proposal is tested and which lead directly into the three forms of evidence; the food motif is now confined to the Value episode. Added universal knowability, the believing/knowing formula, the living-proof claim, and the knowledge–wisdom–science distinction, the last of which also sets up the hand-off to Value. Paid for by shortening the transmission paragraph and the generic comparison sentences. The closing hand-off now echoes the Value card's question of what makes value definite rather than a matter of preference. The study's §1.4 food example remains correct and unchanged; no study follow-up is required.
