## Editorial choices

Centres the episode on understanding becoming conduct and returning through
evaluation. The detailed faculty enumeration and several epistemic triads remain
in the parent study, while Revision 3 introduces the inward and outward movement
needed to explain the relationship between knowing and living.

Revision 2 replaced the family food decision with the three practices (§1.6), which are the study's own account of how a proposal is tested and which lead directly into the three forms of evidence; the food motif is now confined to the Value episode. Added universal knowability, the believing/knowing formula, the living-proof claim, and the knowledge–wisdom–science distinction, the last of which also sets up the hand-off to Value. Paid for by shortening the transmission paragraph and the generic comparison sentences. The closing hand-off now echoes the Value card's question of what makes value definite rather than a matter of preference. The study's §1.4 food example remains correct and unchanged; no study follow-up is required.

Revision 3 keeps that structure but corrects two compressions: "never examining"
made delusion sound like an absolute incapacity to inquire, and "that person
alone validates the teaching" overstated the study's circularity objection.
The new wording retains both substantive concerns without those additions.
The source's September 20 clarification of the ground, intelligible coexistence,
and active knower is reflected explicitly. Inward reflection and outward
guidance, equal capacity, evaluation's feedback, ecological responsibility, and
the standards governing practical judgment now make the positive account fuller.
The Advaita comparison identifies the contrasting status of separate knowers,
instead of only naming Brahman. Its sentence concerns the ultimate standpoint;
it does not deny Advaita's empirical knowledge or ethical practice.

These additions replace repetition in the evidence and transmission discussion
and shorten the opening. The broad warning about science becoming destructive
has been replaced with the more explanatory relation between purpose, practical
means, and governing criteria. The closing qualification now names post-death
continuity alongside constitution. The final script is 19 words shorter than
Revision 2. Canonical study content and publication status are unchanged.
