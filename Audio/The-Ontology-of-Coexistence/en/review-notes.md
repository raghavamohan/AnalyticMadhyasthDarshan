## Editorial choices

Uses satta and dharma only where their meanings can be explained in the narration. Omits the faculty inventory, progressions, and detailed physical comparisons. The tree illustrates the four aspects; it is not presented as a demonstration of the ontology.

Revision 2 moved the opening from the tree to the study's own question, added beginninglessness and the "Brahma is truth, the world is perpetual" slogan (§1.14) so that the later perpetuity-versus-permanence paragraph has something to answer, and rewrote the comparison paragraph with concrete contrasts. Paid for by removing the interdependence framing of the old opener and tightening the satta and awakening paragraphs. The optional note on the meaning of the name Madhyasth (§1.11) was drafted and dropped for length. No study follow-up is required.

The section pointers in that Revision 2 account refer to its September 6 source
baseline. The Source map above uses the current September 20 study.

Revision 3 preserves the central question, tree illustration, real plurality,
four orders, two development processes, practical fulfilment, evidence limits,
and Knowledge-stage handoff. Its consequential changes are:

- Corrects "the sciences agree" about an observer-independent world to the
  narrower agreement with **scientific realism**. The current study explicitly
  distinguishes scientific method from a complete metaphysical position
  (§§3.1, 4.1, 5.3.2). Physicalism is presented as one family among modern
  alternatives, not as their shared answer.
- Makes Advaita's empirical validity audible alongside its denial of independent
  absolute plurality (§2.4), avoiding an implication that it dismisses everyday
  reality.
- Explains constitutional completeness as no longer gaining or losing
  constituent particles, connects that premise to claimed persistence, and
  distinguishes it from understanding acquired through awakening (§§1.7.1,
  1.11). Moves the tree/body impermanence qualification beside the perpetuity
  claim so the listener need not wait for its correction.
- Restores substance to saturation by including its connection to energy,
  activity, mutual recognition, and order. Explains insentient recognition as
  definite responsiveness (§1.2); keeps knowing with jeevan and preserves the
  knower's distinctness in realisation (§1.14).
- Clarifies properties as powers effective through interaction, rather than
  only the effects already produced (§1.3). The tree's usefulness remains
  relative to the affected body and conditions.
- Connects fulfilment to the human desire for continuing happiness and names
  mutually fulfilling participation with nature. This preserves the affirmative
  direction of the account without treating the bare fact of coexistence as
  a sufficient argument for every obligation (§§1.6, 1.13–1.15, 6.2.4).

The faculty inventory, order-conformance terms, detailed completeness goals,
karma, time, imaging, and individual modern philosophical alternatives remain
for the full study. Omitting them keeps the audio an introduction rather than
an abbreviated catalogue. No canonical study change is required by this review.
