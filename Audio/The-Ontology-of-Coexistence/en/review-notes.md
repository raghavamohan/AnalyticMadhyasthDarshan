## Editorial choices

Uses satta and dharma only where their meanings can be explained in the narration. Omits the faculty inventory, progressions, and detailed physical comparisons. The tree illustrates the four aspects; it is not presented as a demonstration of the ontology.

Revision 2 moved the opening from the tree to the study's own question, added beginninglessness and the "Brahma is truth, the world is perpetual" slogan (§1.14) so that the later perpetuity-versus-permanence paragraph has something to answer, and rewrote the comparison paragraph with concrete contrasts. Paid for by removing the interdependence framing of the old opener and tightening the satta and awakening paragraphs. The optional note on the meaning of the name Madhyasth (§1.11) was drafted and dropped for length. No study follow-up is required.
